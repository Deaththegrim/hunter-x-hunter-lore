Game modes

How a story in the known world leans.

Another Kurta Lives

RUN · Hard
A second survivor of the Kurta Clan, with the scarlet eyes and the grief, in a world that still buys and sells their people's eyes.
Expect · grief, revenge, the eyes' black market, a second survivor's choice
Lighter on · light-hearted scenes

Beyond the Lake

WHAT IF · Very hard
The voyage does not stop at the New Continent: the party cross the lake's edge into the Dark Continent, where everything the world's governments feared is real.
Expect · exploration, impossible creatures, survival, the price of hope
Lighter on · the known world's safety

Chaos

CHAOS · Very hard
One cursed lot from the auction is opened in front of the whole party, and every one of them is bound by a different Nen curse at once.
Expect · curses, conditions, desperate cures, strange gifts
Lighter on · the canon's order

Family Business

ROLE · Hard
The party serve a Mafia family: guards, collectors, fixers and soldiers, under bosses who reward loyalty and bury failure.
Expect · loyalty, money, rival families, the code
Lighter on · law and order

Guards of the Princes

ROLE · Very hard
The party guard a prince of Kakin aboard the Black Whale, where the throne goes to whoever is left and every corridor holds a rival's soldiers.
Expect · intrigue, close protection, martial law, the long voyage
Lighter on · open travel

House of Assassins

ROLE · Hard
The party are assassins and their household: killers raised from childhood, butlers who guard a mountain, and the family that rules them.
Expect · contracts, family pressure, poison, the price of leaving
Lighter on · innocence

Hunters for Hire

ROLE · Medium
The party are licensed Hunters taking jobs: bounties, ruins, rare beasts and lost people, for whoever pays and whatever their licence opens.
Expect · contracts, travel, a different job every arc
Lighter on · home life

Multiplayer

TABLE · Medium
A party of applicants, Hunters, fighters and drifters from every corner of the map, on one road.
Expect · shared scenes, a spotlight for everyone, mixed callings
Lighter on · solo arcs

Players of the Game

ROLE · Medium
The party are Greed Island players inside a game that is real, hunting the hundred cards that clear it, among players who kill for them.
Expect · cards, spells, puzzles, rival players
Lighter on · the outside world

Prodigies of Nen

POWER · Easy
The party are prodigies whose auras grow faster than anyone's, strong enough to change a city's fate, and the world's strongest have noticed.
Expect · fast growth, big Hatsu, heroic wins
Lighter on · slow training

Reborn Among the Ants

RUN · Very hard
Humans taken by the Chimera Ants and reborn in their bodies, remembering the lives they had, in a colony that eats people.
Expect · memory, hiding, a monstrous body, choosing a side
Lighter on · a human world that welcomes them

Summer on Whale Island

GENTLE · Very easy
A slower, kinder road: island harbours, forest paths, fishing, good food and small troubles, with the world's dangers far away.
Expect · warm scenes, kind neighbours, small stakes
Lighter on · battles and loss

The Canon Road

RUN · Medium
Gon's story as the series tells it, with the party living alongside it, meeting the cast where the cast is.
Expect · the canon's great moments, the cast at their canon points
Lighter on · what-ifs

The Hunter Exam

SPECIALIST · Hard
Applicants on the road to a Hunter's licence, through the hidden tests, the phases and the final bout, and the one secret exam after it.
Expect · the exam's phases, rival applicants, examiners who cannot be predicted
Lighter on · long arcs

The Junkyard's Children

ROLE · Hard
The party are people of Meteor City, a junkyard city of millions with no records, whose people take whatever the world throws away and look after their own.
Expect · survival, loyalty, the world's contempt, revenge
Lighter on · comfort

The Specialist's Road

RUN · Hard
A Conjurer or Manipulator whose aura has already turned Specialist, with an ability nobody else can learn and a world that wants to know how it works.
Expect · a unique Hatsu, attention, the price of rarity
Lighter on · ordinary training

The Tower of Fighters

ROLE · Medium
The party climb Heavens Arena floor by floor, for prize money, then fame, then the secret of the fighters above the 200th floor.
Expect · bouts, rivals, training, the climb
Lighter on · travel

Under a Teacher

SPECIALIST · Medium
Students of Nen and their teacher: opening the aura, the four basics, the water test, and the first Hatsu built by its own rules.
Expect · training, the basics, a teacher's wisdom, a first ability
Lighter on · constant fights
