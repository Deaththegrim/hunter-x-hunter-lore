District: Gordeau Desert and the outskirts

Gordeau Desert and the outskirts is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The dry wasteland outside Yorknew City, crossed by the highway to Lingon Airport, and the empty buildings on the city's edge where nobody asks who is staying. Cars cross the desert in an hour and a half; anything that happens out among the rocks is seen by nobody. Its hub is Lingon Airport; its other places: Gordeau Desert, Desert Highway, Troupe Hideout, Balloon Crash Site, Gordeau Rock Field, Desert Roadhouse, Dry Salt Pan, Lingon Private Apron, Lingon Shopping Galleria, Lingon Departure Lounge, Lingon Arrivals Kerb, Lingon Cargo Terminal, Lingon Customs Hall, Hotel Lingon Skyway, Lingon Gas Farm, Lingon Ground Crew Village, Lingon Security Barracks, Mile Forty Truck Stop, Gordeau Motor Court, Desert Tow Yard, Gordeau Highway Patrol Post, Gordeau Drive-In Cinema, Gordeau Line Terminus, Rustline Factory Row, Gordeau Scrapyard, Tin Roof Camp, Gordeau Cement Works, Gordeau Pumping Station, Gordeau Potter's Field, Gordeau Wayside Chapel, Gordeau Night Clinic, Gordeau Settlement School, Gordeau Date Farm, Gordeau Gun Club, Gordeau Balloon Ledge, Nostrade Lookout Ridge, Owl's Ambush Gully, Gordeau Footpath Wash, Gordeau Gypsum Quarry, Old Gordeau Silver Camp, Gordeau Dune Sea, Gordeau Arroyo, Relay Mast Mesa. By land it joins Meteor City, Yorknew City.
