District: NGL

NGL is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The NGL Autonomous Region, the Union's western state, where nothing made by machine or from synthetic stuff may enter. Visitors are stripped, scanned and dressed in natural cloth at a border stop built inside two hollow trees; inside, people travel by horse and write letters, and the forests run unbroken to the sea. Its hub is NGL Border Stop; its other places: NGL, NGL Village, NGL Horse Trail, NGL Shore, Nest Woods, NGL Reserve Post, NGL Interpreters' Lodge, Saddlebag Post House, Nettlecloth Weaving Sheds, Sourapple Orchards, Haruna's Cottage, Tallow Cross Inn, Barrowgate Market, Hoofiron Forge, Slatehouse School, Feverwort Infirmary, Green Creed Meeting House, Founder's Longhouse, NGL Stockade, Thatchwright's Yard, Mossford, Waxlight Chandlery, Longmane Stud, Kelp Strand Saltworks, Sedge Fen, Blackpeat Moor, Charcoal Burners' Clearing, Oxbow Felling Camp, Forest Warden Lodge, Grindstone Quarry, Trappers' Longhut, Signal Beacon Hill, Greyback Hills, Shale Lookout Cliff, Wild Hive Gully, Gunshot Glade, Impaling Grove, Reedwater Ford, Plume Thicket, Hemlock Deep, Far Forage Ridge. By land it joins Rokario and the near Union.
