Kurta Clan

The Kurta were a clan of a little over a hundred people who kept to a single village deep in the Lukso Province, ruled by an elder and seldom seen in any town. Not every Kurta's eyes turned, but those that did burned scarlet in anger or grief, and outsiders who saw it were frightened enough to turn violent while collectors offered fortunes. So the clan hid, rode its Piko birds to the nearest market and back, and let few of its young go out into the world. It is said a Kurta who wished to leave had to pass the elders' trials first, the last of them a day in town without once losing their temper. Nothing of the clan remains in the forest now but an empty village.
