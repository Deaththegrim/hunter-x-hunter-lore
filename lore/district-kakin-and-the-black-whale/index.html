District: Kakin and the Black Whale

Kakin and the Black Whale is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The Kakin Empire in the middle of the Azian Continent, more than a billion people and growing richer every year, and the great ship Black Whale 1, built to carry the king's expedition to the New Continent. The ship's five tiers divide its passengers by wealth and rank, and the Royal Army keeps martial law aboard. Its hub is Kakin Empire; its other places: Hoihoi Hotel, Kakin Survey Forest, Black Whale 1, Tier 1, Banquet Hall, King's Living Quarters, Princes' Residential District, Beyond's Detention Room, Tier 2, Tier 3, Political Ward, Medical Ward, Observation Deck, Movie Theatre, Tier 4, Tier 5, Kakin Festival Quay, Kakin Royal Military Academy, Kakin Parliament House, Hui Guo Rou Palace, Kakin Zoological Agency, RG Foundation Tower, Kakin State Lottery Hall, Kakin Central Station, Kakin International Airport, Have-Not Day-Labour Yard, Cha-R Bonded Warehouses, Xi-Yu Shipping Office, Heil-Ly Brokerage House, Lantern Noodle Row, Kakin Paddy Terraces, First King's Temple, Afterlife Companions' Barrows, Camp Tiger Uplands, Azian Frontier Highlands, Black Whale Shipyard, VVIP Living Quarters, Queens' Living Quarters, Soldiers' and Associates' Quarters, Tier 1 Supreme Court, Tier 1 Cell Block, Lifeboat Launch Site, Tier 1 VIP Casino, VVIP Recreational Facilities, VIP Witness Protection Area, Black Whale Bridge, Black Whale Switchboard, Whale Back Airship Pad, Black Whale Cargo Holds, Black Whale Propeller Hall, Tier 3 First-Class Cabins, Tier 3 Central Court, Tier 4 Passenger Clinic, Tier 5 Sick Bay, Buor Toll Passage.
