District: Meteor City

Meteor City is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. A junkyard city that appears on no map and in no record: millions of people living on what the rest of the world throws away, ruled by an assembly of elders. Its people keep no papers, take care of their own and answer an insult with a bomb. Outsiders who come for recruits or salvage come by its one road. Its hub is Meteor City; its other places: Assembly Hall, Meteor City Church, Trash Heaps, Junk Market, Dumping Shore, Outer Gate Road, Uga Forest, Kirimori Valley, Zazan's Palace, Church Video Cellar, Church Screening Hall, Little Graves Yard, Inner Residential Zones, Caretakers' House, Heap School, Meteor Elder's House, Old Mafia Recruiting House, Gyudondond Quarter, Meteor City Last Bus Stand, Kirimori Embalming House, Uvogin's Old Territory, Videotape Diggings, Meteor City Outskirts, Traffickers' Track, Dictator's Camp Ruins, Hybrid Pyre Ground, Paper Mound, Road End Caravan Lot, Foundlings' Doorstep, Meteor Scrap Smelter, Heap Generator Yard, Rain Cistern Terraces, Mask Lane Clinic, Smoulder Tyre Fields, Bottle Glass Ridge, Wrecked Car Valley, Dead Set Hill, Drum Barrens, Gullscrap Point, Compost Terraces, Heap Goat Folds, Relief Sack Depot, Lantern Shrine of All Faiths, Masked Watch Barracks, Visitors' Bunkhouse, Mask Stitchers' Row, Wire Burners' Hollow, Heap Radio Shack. By land it joins Gordeau Desert and the outskirts.
