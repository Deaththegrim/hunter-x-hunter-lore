District: Heavens Arena

Heavens Arena is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The tower of Heavens Arena and the town at its feet, on the far side of Padokea's continent. Thousands of fighters climb its floors every day for prize money and fame, and the crowds who come to watch fill its hotels, shops and restaurants. Above the 200th floor the fights change, and the fighters there talk of tricks the lower floors only whisper about. Its hub is Heavens Arena; its other places: Arena Lower Floors, Arena Hundredth Floors, Arena 200th Floor, Floor Master Floors, 251st Floor Penthouse, Arena Concourse, Arena Clinic, Arena Town, Wing's Lodging, Shingen-ryu Practice Room, Arena Lift Bank, Arena Prize Office, Arena Waiting Hall, Arena Commentary Booth, Arena Judges' Gallery, Arena Administration Floor, Arena Ticket Hall, Arena Souvenir Arcade, Arena Security Office, Arena Service Levels, Cloudline Restaurant, Hundreds Floor Gym, Arena Airship Port, Skyfoot Station, Arena Coach Terminal, Tower Freight Yard, Hotel Skyreach, Ringside Hostel, Skyfoot Patisserie, Skyfoot Night Market, Tenth Point Bar, Tower Workers' Tenements, Lucky Ring Betting Parlour, Tower Pawn and Loan, Ringwear Outfitters, Poster Press Printworks, Arena Weekly Offices, Tower Gondola Depot, Skyfoot Bonesetter, Dalmont Boxing Gym, Skyfoot Police Station, Skyfoot Chapel, Skyfoot Primary School, Hall of the Burning Will, Tower Shadow Park, Tower River Flats, Skyfoot Quarry Hills, Skyfoot Market Gardens, Skyfoot Cemetery. By land it joins Padokea and Kukuroo Mountain; by sea Yorknew City (across the inner sea).
