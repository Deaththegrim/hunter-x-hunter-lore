Kukuroo Mountain

Kukuroo Mountain is a dormant volcano 3,722 metres high in Padokea's Dentora Region, wrapped in jungle at its foot and cloud at its top. The Zoldyck family owns the mountain and all the land around it, and walls it with stone. The mansion is said to stand at the summit. Nobody outside the family and its butlers has seen it and talked about it afterwards.
