District: Padokea and Kukuroo Mountain

Padokea and Kukuroo Mountain is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The Dentora Region of the Republic of Padokea, where entry is free and a daily bus tour runs up into the mountains to look at the gate of the most famous family of assassins in the world. Beyond the gate the whole mountain is theirs. The town of Parasta and its airport lie down the highway, and the land south runs on into the Mimbo Republic. Its hub is Dentora Region; its other places: Lamentation Road, Testing Gate, Zebro's Cabin, Estate Jungle, Butlers' Quarters, Kukuroo Mountain, Zoldyck Mansion, Mansion Lower Levels, Dentora Highway, Parasta, Parasta Airport, Gatekeeper's Booth, Kukuroo Bone Midden, Zoldyck Kennels, Butlers' Schoolhouse, Estate Motor Pool, Senior Butlers' House, Silva's Chamber, Milluki's Room, Mansion Watch Room, Butlers' Firing Range, Zoldyck Proving Ground, Kukuroo Summit Crater, Kukuroo Fumarole Gully, Kukuroo Boundary Wall, Estate Airship Mast, Estate Supply Yard, Estate Infirmary, Wallside Woods, Raiders' Clearing, Kukuroo Crash Cliff, Kukuroo Lowland Forest, Parasta Back Road, Dentora Station, Lamentation Tours Depot, Dentora Souvenir Arcade, Seventh Door Bar, Ironweight Outfitters, Dentora Clinic, Dentora Police Post, Dentora Chapel, Dentora Hot Springs, Dentora Orchards, Dentora Pumice Quarry, Cedar Gorge Sawmill, Upland Goat Farm, Dentora Reservoir Dam, Dentora Mountain Rescue Hut, Halfway Diner, Parasta Blimp Works. By land it joins Heavens Arena; by sea Kukan'yu and the exam road (across the western sea).
