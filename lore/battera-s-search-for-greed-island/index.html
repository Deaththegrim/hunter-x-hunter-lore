Battera's search for Greed Island

In 1988 a tycoon named Battera began buying every copy of an expensive, out-of-print game called Greed Island and offering rewards to anyone who would play it for him, with a fortune promised to whoever beat it. He has paid ever since. Strong people go to his castle, sit down at a console and vanish, and many never come back. The rewards keep rising, and the business pages call it a rich man's obsession. Hunters who know what the game is call it something worse.
