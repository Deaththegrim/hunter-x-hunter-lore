Kakin's castes

Kakin keeps a caste system that its revolution never touched, and the bottom of it is the Have-Nots, barred from public office and from rank in the army. In the old days they were the ones sacrificed and buried with failed princes as afterlife companions. The custom is gone; the bar on office is not. Kakin's princess Camilla made a show of taking Have-Nots into her own army with full soldiers' rights.
