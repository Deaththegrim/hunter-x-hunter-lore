District: Whale Island

Whale Island is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. A small forested island with mountains on either side, visited mostly by fishing boats. Few people live there and fewer children; the town by the harbour, the swamp inland and the old forest behind it are the whole of it. Ships call when the weather allows, and the open sea lies between the island and everywhere else. Its hub is Whale Island; its other places: Mito's House, Whale Island Swamp, Snakebeech Forest, Kon's Den, Captain's Ship, Whale Island Fish Market, Whale Island Lighthouse, Twin Ridges, Whale Island Rock Pools, Whale Island Weighing Square, Snakebeech Fishing Bend, Snakebeech Denning Hollows, Foxbear Glade, Whale Island Seacliff, Whale Island Outer Mole, Whale Island Post Office, Whale Island Harbourmaster's Office, Whale Island Ice House, Whale Island Fuel Dock, Whale Island Boatyard, Whale Island Smokehouse, Whale Island Net Works, Whale Island Drying Racks, Gullwing Tavern, Harbour Rest Guesthouse, Harbour General Store, Whale Island Bakehouse, Whale Island Chapel, Whale Island Schoolhouse, Whale Island Clinic, Whale Island Constable's Post, Whale Island Generator House, Whale Island Terrace Gardens, Whale Island Goat Commons, Whale Island Orchard, Whale Island Spring Cistern, Whale Island Quarry, Snakebeech Charcoal Camp, Snakebeech Sawpit, Twin Ridges Radio Mast, Whale Island North Cove, Whale's Back Headland, Whale Island Sea Caves, Twin Ridges Falls, Whale Island Sand Cove, Gannet Stack, Whale Island Reef Wreck, Whale Island Fishing Grounds. By land it joins nothing; by sea Kukan'yu and the exam road (across the open sea).
