District: The exam's far sites

The exam's far sites is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The remote sites the exam carries its applicants to by airship and boat: a prison tower with no door in its walls, an island where applicants hunt each other's badges for a week, a hall where the last phase is fought, and the small city where a later year's exam began behind a shop. Most people here are applicants, examiners or prisoners. Its hub is Beeskafmarro; its other places: Diksakura, Exam Airship, Trick Tower, Trick Tower Cliffs, Trick Tower Landing, Zevil Island, Zevil Woods, Zevil Grasslands, Zevil Shore Caves, Final Phase Hall, Lurka Ruins, Togari's Duel Chamber, Leroute's Forfeit Cell, Trick Tower Fork Room, Trick Tower Drop Shafts, Trick Tower Cell Block, Trick Tower Infirmary, Tower Warders' Village, Bourbon's Snake Cave, Gittarackur's Burrow, Zevil Hollow Oak, Zevil Butterfly Glade, Spear Clearing, Amori Ravine, Zevil Crossing Launch, Zevil Observers' Blind, Beans's Briefing Room, Finalists' Rest Hotel, Commission Airfield, Commission Proving Range, Lurka Royal Tomb, Lurka Restoration Trust, Lurka Volunteers' Bunkhouse, Lurka Gate Village, Lurka Valley Woods, Beeskafmarro Freight Yard, Hotel Marro, Beeskafmarro Ward Clinic, Beeskafmarro Police Box, Beeskafmarro Fish Cannery, Beeskafmarro Bus Terminal, Beeskafmarro Pawnshop, Beeskafmarro Middle School, Beeskafmarro Chapel, Tower Strait Pier, Zevil Strait Lighthouse, Saltgrass Oyster Flats, Far Coast Road, Basalt Quarry, Hillside Dairy. By land it joins Kukan'yu and the exam road.
