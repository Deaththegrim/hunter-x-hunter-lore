District: Yorknew City

Yorknew City is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The sprawling metropolis at the heart of the Yorbian Continent, in the United States of Saherta. Every September its auction houses sell the rarest things in the world, the street markets sell everything else, and the Mafia's own auction runs behind closed doors. The Mafia paid for the mayor's campaign, and the police bend accordingly. Its hub is Yorknew City; its other places: Cemetery Building, Southernpiece Auction House, Bull Market, Preview Market, Hotel Beitacle, Hotel Bayloke, Dayroad Park, Castor Line, Lipa Station, Saloma Mall, Continental Street, Motoba Building, Tarsetol Station, Sengi Guild, Nostrade's Mansion, Yorknew City Hall, Yorknew Police Headquarters, Hide-and-Seek Auction Club, Zepile's Workroom, Duranti's Lunch Counter, Gridline Internet Café, Ninth Avenue Pawnbrokers, Handset Bazaar, Fountain Corner Plaza, Merchant Bell Square, Yorknew Metropolitan Hospital, Hotel Lavanda, Hotel Grandvale, Hotel Starling, Dons' Retreat, Hacker's Loft, Yorknew Stock Exchange, YNB Broadcasting Tower, Diamond Row, Saherta Gallery of Modern Art, Cathedral of the Nine Bells, Yorknew Freight Yard, Disused Castor Tunnels, Hollis Wholesale Market, Lantern Alley Workshops, Kessel Firearms, Casino Spade Royale, Iron Mitt Boxing Gym, Club Velours, Eastbank Power Station, Yorknew Stadium, Yorknew Coach Terminal, Grimes Auto Salvage, Dross Street Tenements, Mercy Street Free Clinic, Azian Quarter Night Market, Yorknew Metropolitan University, Gasworks Wasteland, Bunkhouse Eighty-Eight. By land it joins Gordeau Desert and the outskirts, Greed Island; by sea Heavens Arena (across the inner sea), Kakin and the Black Whale (across the eastern ocean), Kukan'yu and the exam road (across the northern strait), Rokario and the near Union (across the Balsa waters).
