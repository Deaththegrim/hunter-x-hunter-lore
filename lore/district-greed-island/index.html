District: Greed Island

Greed Island is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The world of the game Greed Island and the castle where a billionaire keeps the consoles that open it. A player who enters wakes in a tower on a grassy plain and plays for the game's cards in towns of prizes, magic, love, gambling and pirates. Only those who can see a certain energy can play at all, and those who die in the game do not come back.
