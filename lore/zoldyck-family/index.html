Zoldyck Family

The deadliest house of assassins in the world, who live on Kukuroo Mountain, a dormant volcano in Padokea's Dentora region. Their name frightens the underworld more than the Phantom Troupe; their faces are almost unknown, and one photograph of a Zoldyck sells for a fortune. Tourist buses run to the Testing Gate, and its first door is too heavy for most men to move; beyond it a giant hound eats anyone who climbs the wall. The children are trained to kill from birth, grow used to poison, pain and current, and choose a speciality as they grow. The family takes contracts from anyone who can pay its very high fee, and no government in the world moves against it. A stranger meets the family as a closed gate; one who serves it starts as a butler and never speaks of what they see.
