District: East Gorteau

East Gorteau is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The Republic of East Gorteau at the Union's eastern end, a closed dictatorship under its Supreme Leader, whose palace stands in the wilderness outside the capital, Peijin. Informers are rewarded for denouncing neighbours, the rich live well and most people do not, and foreigners are rarely let in. Its hub is Peijin; its other places: Peijin Assembly Grounds, Royal Palace of East Gorteau, Luonton City, Luonton Highway Forest, Mandai City, Taba City, Underground Lake, Underground Clinic, Gorteau Border Post, Peijin Underground Church, Peijin Central Station, Peijin Informant Bureau, Peijin Secretariat of State, Peijin Revolution Monument, Gorteau National Gungi Hall, Peijin Go and Shogi Club, Peijin State Dance Theatre, Peijin State Department Store, Peijin Broadcasting House, Peijin Foreigners' Hotel, Peijin Model School, Peijin Ration Hall, Peijin People's Cinema, Peijin Armour Depot, Palace Guard Barracks, Peijin Military Airfield, Hwaryu River, Kaedong Barrens, Soeyang Fishing Harbour, Sarohan Village, Yaoshan Farmlands, Komugi's Family Home, Mandai Sports Stadium, Mandai Textile Mill, Mandai People's Hospital, Mandai Black Market, Luonton Lumber Mill, Luonton Bus Depot, Taba Collective Farm, Taba Road Inn, Juhan Coal Pits, Kuksan Labour Camp, Gemmo Marshes, Kwanlo Ford, Paektae Hill Temple, Yorim Pine Ridges, Chongso Radar Station. By land it joins Rokario and the near Union.
