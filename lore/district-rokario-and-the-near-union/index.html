District: Rokario and the near Union

Rokario and the near Union is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The Republic of Rokario in the Balsa Islands, the modern country on the Mitene Union's western side, with its small crowded cities close to the closed border of NGL, its mountains and its desert, and east of it the Republic of Hass and the Republic of West Gorteau. Trains run between its cities and buses run to the border. Its hub is Doli City; its other places: Aguilor, Tri-Orb Hotel, Doli Woods, Pata City, Miera Mountains, Ininge City, Quen City, Setl City, Quant City, Bilgai Desert, Roto Mountains, Republic of Hass, Nebaska, Republic of West Gorteau, Palm's Mansion, Doli Municipal Park, Doli Night Bar, Doli Firefly Marsh, Doli Boxing Gym, Doli Border Truck Yard, Doli Covered Market, Doli Night Clinic, Doli Chapel, Doli Estuary Wharf, Pata City Police Station, Pata General Hospital, Pata Freight Sidings, Miera Limestone Caves, Miera Ranger Hut, Ininge Grain Exchange, Ininge Motor Works, Quen Orchards, Quen Fire Station, Rokario Border Guard Barracks, Setl Truck Stop, Setl Glassworks, Quant Pumping Station, Quant Motor Lodge, Bilgai Gypsum Quarry, Bilgai Rest House, Roto Pass Customs Post, Roto Coal Mine, Hass Wheat Cooperative, Hass Rail Junction, Hass Parish Church, Hass Border Barracks, Hass Highland Dairy, Hass Vineyards, West Gorteau Army Garrison, West Gorteau Highway Motel, West Gorteau Textile Mills, West Gorteau Radio Station, West Gorteau Customs Warehouse. By land it joins East Gorteau, NGL; by sea Yorknew City (across the Balsa waters).
