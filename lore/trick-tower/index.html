Trick Tower

Trick Tower is a prison, a great stone cylinder on a plain with its cells hidden inside a maze of traps and puzzles. Its warden sometimes lends it to the Hunter Exam: candidates are dropped on the roof and told to reach the bottom alive within seventy-two hours, while the prisoners inside earn time off their sentences by stopping them. The outer walls are no shortcut; flying beasts hunt there.
