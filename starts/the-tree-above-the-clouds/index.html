The Tree Above the Clouds

ERA · the election and after · 1-5 players · guided
📍 World Tree, The known world. The only sapling of its kind in the known world, a tree 1,784 metres tall whose top stands above the clouds.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 4-10)

Climbers come from every continent to the only World Tree in the known world, nearly two kilometres high. Past the 500-metre platform there are no lifts, only bark and wind. Word at the gate is that a famous Hunter has climbed to the top today.
