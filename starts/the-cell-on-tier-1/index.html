The Cell on Tier 1

ERA · the Black Whale · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Beyond's Detention Room, The known world. A small holding cell on Tier 1 where the expedition's leader, Beyond Netero, is kept under watch for the whole voyage.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: ☠️ Deadly. Endgame ground; nothing here is scaled to a beginner. (Recommended Level 30-40)

The leader of the expedition to the New Continent surrendered to the Association a year ago, and he sails in a small cell on the top of the ship with Zodiacs at his door. He seems to be enjoying himself.
🎭 Beyond Netero, Male, the son of Isaac Netero. Ancient Chinese commander robes over lamellar armor. A bearded man with a cross-shaped scar laughing over a drink as the world floods his phone lines with applications.
🎭 Kanzai, Male, the Zodiacs' Tiger. Green eyes, yellow pinstriped baseball jersey with black collar. A tiger-striped man in a baseball uniform leaping across a table at a smiling vice chairman.
🎭 Saccho Kobayakawa, Male, the Zodiacs' Horse. Small dark eyes, purple kimono with three flower designs. A horse-faced swordsman reading the Association's rules in silence while the others argue.
🎭 Saiyu, Male, the Zodiacs' Monkey. Black mandarin-collar jacket with gold trim and gold knot buttons. A lanky man in a monkey costume jabbing a finger at Ging across the table.
