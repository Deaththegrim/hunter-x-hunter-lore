(Party) Heavy Cups and Heavy Doors

PARTY · Kukuroo Mountain and Heavens Arena · 2-5 players · Gon, Kurapika and Leorio · guided
📍 Zebro's Cabin, The known world. A small cabin near the Testing Gate where the gatekeeper lives.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 6-20)

Killua went home after the exam, and his home is a mountain full of assassins. Three friends have come to fetch him, and the old man at the gate has offered them a bed while they get strong enough to knock.
🎭 Gon Freecss, Male, a boy from Whale Island. Large hazel-brown eyes, green high-collared jacket with orange-red trim. A small boy with spiky hair grinning in a crowd of hard-faced adults, a fishing rod on his shoulder.
🎭 Kurapika, Male, a slight. Grey-brown eyes. A slender blond youth in a blue tabard reading a stranger in silence, his eyes flickering red for an instant.
🎭 Leorio Paradinight, Male, a tall man in a dark suit and tinted glasses who looks ten years older than he is. White shirt. A tall man in a suit and tinted glasses bellowing at the top of a staircase, briefcase raised.
🎭 Zebro, Male, a balding. Narrow sleepy eyes, grey security guard uniform with pockets. A balding guard straining to push open a vast door while three visitors watch in disbelief.
