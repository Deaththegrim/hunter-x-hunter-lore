The Bus to the Gate

ERA · Kukuroo Mountain and Heavens Arena · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Lamentation Road, The known world. The road the daily Lamentation Tours bus takes up through Dentora's mountains to the foot of Kukuroo Mountain.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 6-14)

Tourists pay to ride up into Dentora's mountains and look at the gate of the most famous assassins in the world. The guide tells the same jokes every trip. This morning two big men in the back seat are not laughing.
🎭 Coco Loo, Female, the guide on the tourist bus that runs up the Dentora road to the gate of Kukuroo Mountain. Big curly messy orange-pink hair, green eyes, navy bus guide uniform jacket with light blue trim and pencil skirt. A cheerful guide in a blue uniform pointing out the window at a gate in the mountainside.
