The Clone Fighter

ERA · Kukuroo Mountain and Heavens Arena · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Arena 200th Floor, The known world. The 200th floor and those above it, where there is no prize money, only fame, and every weapon is allowed.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 12-24)

A fighter in white has waited two years for a rematch with the magician who once beat him and let him live. The match is tonight, the Big Ring is sold out, and everyone on the 200th floor is betting on how it ends.
🎭 Hisoka Morow, Male, a tall, muscular man dressed like a jester. Narrow amber eyes, pink sash. A painted jester smiling at a boy with a fishing rod, a playing card turning slowly between his fingers.
🎭 Kastro, Male, a celebrated fighter on the 200th floor of the Heavens Arena. Blue eyes, yellow hoodless cloak with high collar. A white-haired fighter in a yellow cloak raising a clawed hand toward a smiling jester across the ring.
🎭 Cocco, Female, the ring announcer for Heavens Arena's upper floors. Brown eyes, pink and navy Heavens Arena staff uniform with a fist emblem. An orange-haired announcer leaning out over the ring rail, shouting a fighter's record into her headset.
