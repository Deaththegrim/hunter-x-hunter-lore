(Party) The Climb

PARTY · Kukuroo Mountain and Heavens Arena · 2-5 players · Gon, Killua and Zushi · guided
📍 Arena Lower Floors, The known world. The floors from the first to the ninety-ninth, where a fighter climbs ten floors for a win and drops ten for a loss, fights with bare hands only, and is paid a little for every floor gained.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 4-12)

Two boys walked into the tallest fighting tower in the world because they needed money, and their first bouts sent them straight to the fiftieth floor. A small boy in a white gi registered the same day and went up with them, and he is better than he looks.
🎭 Zushi, Male, a small, brown-haired, heavy-browed boy in a karate gi over a black turtleneck who climbs. Very short buzzed brown hair, big hazel-brown eyes. A small boy in a gi bowing deeply to his opponent before flooring a man four times his size.
🎭 Maita, Male, a large, bulky prizefighter with a bald head, narrow eyes and thick bushy eyebrows that. Narrow eyes. A huge bald fighter with bushy eyebrows eating a rice ball on the bench while his name crackles over the speaker.
