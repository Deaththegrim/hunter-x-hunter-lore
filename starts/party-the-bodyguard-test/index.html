(Party) The Bodyguard Test

PARTY · Yorknew City · 2-5 players · Kurapika, Melody and Basho · guided
📍 Nostrade's Mansion, The known world. The luxurious house of the Nostrade family, a Mafia family rising fast, where Light Nostrade lives with his daughter Neon and keeps her collection.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 12-26)

A Mafia boss is hiring Nen users to guard his daughter at the auctions, and the agency has sent its best applicants to his mansion. The interviewer has given them a list of items to fetch, and he has not said what comes after.
🎭 Kurapika, Male, a slight. Grey-brown eyes. A slender blond youth in a blue tabard reading a stranger in silence, his eyes flickering red for an instant.
🎭 Basho, Male, a Hunter from Jappon with a pompadour. A muscular man with a pompadour whistling as he writes a poem with a brush on a scrap of paper.
🎭 Dalzollene, Male, the head of Neon Nostrade's bodyguards and Light's right hand. Short messy black hair, tired heavy-lidded eyes, black v-neck sweater with olive and cream striped collar. A tattooed man on a mansion screen telling a room of applicants that the door is locked.
🎭 Melody, Female, a Music Hunter. Large round bulging eyes, long dark blue tunic. A small woman in a hat lowering her flute as a frightened room slowly goes calm.
🎭 Baise, Female, a Hunter with sharp green eyes. Sharp green eyes. A woman with an upright pink braid kissing a terrified man's cheek and smiling as his eyes go blank.
🎭 Shachmono Tocino, Male, a licensed Hunter and senior Nostrade bodyguard. Short cropped orange hair, wide pale eyes. A slender man with curled sideburns sitting among applicants, smiling as hooded gunmen burst in.
