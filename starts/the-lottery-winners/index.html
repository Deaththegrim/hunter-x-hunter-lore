The Lottery Winners

HOOK · the Black Whale · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Kakin Empire, The known world. A country in the middle of the Azian Continent with more than a billion people, an economy that has grown fast for years and a king, Nasubi Hui Guo Rou, at the head of a parliamentary state.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 20-34)

In Kakin the Have-Nots can hold no office and no rank, and most of them never leave the quarter where they were born. This week some of them have won places on the great voyage, and the quarter does not know whether to celebrate or mourn.
