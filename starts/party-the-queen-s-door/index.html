(Party) The Queen's Door

PARTY · the Black Whale · 2-5 players · Kurapika and Bill · guided
📍 Princes' Residential District, The known world. The walled district on Tier 1 where each of the fourteen princes lives in one of fourteen identical rooms, numbered by birth order, set back to back in two rows with walkways between and walls round them all.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: ☠️ Deadly. Endgame ground; nothing here is scaled to a beginner. (Recommended Level 30-40)

The youngest of Kakin's fourteen princes is a baby in his mother's arms, and the Association has sent its new Rat to guard them. Their room is one of fourteen identical rooms behind one wall on the top of the ship, and the ship has just sailed.
🎭 Tserriednich Hui Guo Rou, Male, the fourth prince of Kakin and second son of the first queen Unma. Half-lidded black eyes, elegant dark sports jacket over an open-collar shirt. A tall blond prince smiling courteously over a glass of wine in a room full of glass cases.
🎭 Benjamin Hui Guo Rou, Male, the first prince of Kakin and the eldest son of the first queen Unma. Short upright spiky blond hair, small black eyes, light military puffer jacket. A huge blond prince in a puffer jacket wrestling a lion while he talks on the phone.
🎭 Camilla Hui Guo Rou, Female, the second prince of Kakin and eldest child of the second queen Duazul. Large clear black eyes, elegant frilled gown. A blonde prince in a ball gown smiling sweetly while her guards check their pistols.
🎭 Furykov, Male, one of the first prince Benjamin's personal soldiers. Wide eyes, white high-collared puffer jacket. A bearded soldier grinning at a room of guards, daring the hidden ones among them to show themselves.
🎭 Kurapika, Male, a slight. Grey-brown eyes. A slender blond youth in a blue tabard reading a stranger in silence, his eyes flickering red for an instant.
🎭 Babimyna, Male, one of the first prince Benjamin's personal soldiers. Stern dark eyes, white high-collared puffer jacket. A young soldier with fair curls standing at the back of a crowded room, watching everyone and saying nothing.
