The Castle on the Cliffs

ERA · Greed Island · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Battera's Castle, The known world. An old castle on high ground above walls and cliffs, the property of the billionaire Battera, who has spent fortunes buying every copy of the game Greed Island.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 12-24)

Battera's castle stands on cliffs above high walls, and its basement holds thirty-two consoles running one game. The new recruits have been warned that the castle's security system kills anyone who wanders off.
🎭 Biscuit Krueger, Female, a two-star Hunter of precious stones and a master of Shingen-ryu who looks like a frilly. Large pink eyes, crimson and pink frilly dress with lace-up bodice and short capelet. A tiny frilly blonde girl in a queue of hard-bitten Hunters, smiling sweetly and lying through her teeth.
🎭 Gon Freecss, Male, a boy from Whale Island. Large hazel-brown eyes, green high-collared jacket with orange-red trim. A small boy with spiky hair grinning in a crowd of hard-faced adults, a fishing rod on his shoulder.
🎭 Killua Zoldyck, Male, a white-haired boy on a skateboard who has run away from home to take the Hunter Exam. Messy spiky white hair, sharp blue cat-like eyes. A white-haired boy on a skateboard gliding past a line of exhausted runners, not even breathing hard.
🎭 Bara, Male, a muscular player with long straight purple hair parted in the middle and a trident. Pale wide eyes, black long-sleeved shirt with a large white trident print. A long-haired man with a trident tattoo stepping out of shadow with a severed head in a sack.
🎭 Sub, Male, a player with spiky red hair. Short round puffy spiky red hair, magenta long-sleeved shirt with a large black bat-winged heart print. A red-haired man flicking a stone from his hand to knock a spinning yo-yo out of the air.
🎭 Tsezguerra, Male, a single-star Jackpot Hunter. Olive drab military flight suit with ribbed collar and chest pockets. A lean bearded man in a flight suit watching applicants fail his test one after another.
