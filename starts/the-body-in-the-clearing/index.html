The Body in the Clearing

ANT RUN · the Chimera Ant crisis · reborn among the Ants · guided
📍 Nest Woods, The known world. Dense forest in the NGL interior, old, unmapped and far from any village.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 24-34)

You remember a life: a name, a face, a village. You woke in a clearing in a body with an insect's shell and a soldier's orders in your head, and the others around you are waking too.
🎭 Meleoron, Male, a chameleon-like ant with two big toes on each foot. Bulging round green eyes, grey hooded sweatshirt with hood up. A chameleon-man in a grey hoodie half-faded into the trees, sighing at his own soldiers.
🎭 Zazan, Female, a scorpion-like ant with long tangled fuchsia hair. Long wavy unkempt fuchsia hair, golden eyes. A purple-skinned woman with a scorpion tail lowering her sting into a fallen Hunter's neck.
🎭 Koala, Male, a small pink koala-like ant with fluffy round ears. Small brown eyes, black tuxedo with a white shirt and dark tie. A small pink koala in a tuxedo standing over a fallen man, tipping water from a gourd.
🎭 Rammot, Male, a thick-thighed rabbit-like ant with feathered arms. Long straight green hair, laced shin wraps. A rabbit-like ant grinning beside three animals impaled on branches, watching two boys approach.
