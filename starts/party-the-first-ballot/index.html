(Party) The First Ballot

PARTY · the election and after · 2-5 players · Leorio · guided
📍 Hunter Association Headquarters, The known world. The headquarters of the Hunter Association in Swardani City, where the chairman and the twelve Zodiacs meet, the exams are planned and the licences are issued.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Safe inside; the ground around it is not. (Recommended Level 2-10)

The chairman is dead, and his will says the next one will be chosen by every Hunter alive. Hundreds of them have come to the Association's headquarters to vote, and a medical student with a licence has come to shout at someone.
🎭 Hisoka Morow, Male, a tall, muscular man dressed like a jester. Narrow amber eyes, pink sash. A painted jester smiling at a boy with a fishing rod, a playing card turning slowly between his fingers.
🎭 Illumi Zoldyck, Male, the eldest son of the Zoldyck Family. Very long straight jet-black hair, large black pupil-less eyes, green tunic with short puffed sleeves and round yellow studs. A tall young man with endless black hair and empty eyes, telling his little brother pleasantly that he has no friends.
🎭 Morel Mackernasey, Male, a sea Hunter and sea captain. Shoulder-length straight white hair, grey shirt. A big white-haired man with a pipe as tall as he is blowing smoke that turns into a squad of soldiers.
🎭 Leorio Paradinight, Male, a tall man in a dark suit and tinted glasses who looks ten years older than he is. White shirt. A tall man in a suit and tinted glasses bellowing at the top of a staircase, briefcase raised.
🎭 Tsezguerra, Male, a single-star Jackpot Hunter. Olive drab military flight suit with ribbed collar and chest pockets. A lean bearded man in a flight suit watching applicants fail his test one after another.
🎭 Barry, Male, a Hunter on Tsezguerra's team. Heavy-lidded eyes, olive drab military flight suit with ribbed turtleneck collar. A freckled man in a sideways cap ducking under a punch and answering with three of his own.
