(Party) The Hoihoi Hotel Meeting

PARTY · the Black Whale · 2-5 players · Hanzo and Basho · guided
📍 Hoihoi Hotel, The known world. A hotel in the Kakin Empire, one of the royal family's many, whose staff are discreet about who comes and goes and why.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 20-32)

Kakin's royal family is hiring Hunters to guard its princes on the voyage, and the applicants have been told to meet at seven in a hotel the family owns. A loud ninja and a Mafia poet have come for the same job.
🎭 Biscuit Krueger, Female, a two-star Hunter of precious stones and a master of Shingen-ryu who looks like a frilly. Large pink eyes, crimson and pink frilly dress with lace-up bodice and short capelet. A tiny frilly blonde girl in a queue of hard-bitten Hunters, smiling sweetly and lying through her teeth.
🎭 Izunavi, Male, a tall, muscular, middle-aged Hunter with scruffy black hair, narrow brown eyes. Narrow grey-brown eyes. A bearded man in a practice robe watching a leaf turn in a glass of water held by a pale young student.
🎭 Basho, Male, a Hunter from Jappon with a pompadour. A muscular man with a pompadour whistling as he writes a poem with a brush on a scrap of paper.
🎭 Bill, Male, a tall Hunter with messy fair hair. Messy upswept fair hair, down-turned heavy-lidded eyes. A tired-eyed Hunter in a black suit calmly advising a furious young man to put down a gun.
🎭 Hanzo, Male, a bald young ninja from Jappon. Sharp dark eyes, tight black short-sleeved shirt with round shoulder pads. A bald young ninja crouched on a branch above the applicants, talking loudly about how silent he is.
🎭 Melody, Female, a Music Hunter. Large round bulging eyes, long dark blue tunic. A small woman in a hat lowering her flute as a frightened room slowly goes calm.
