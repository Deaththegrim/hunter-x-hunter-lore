The Wind in the Corridor

ERA · Kukuroo Mountain and Heavens Arena · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Arena 200th Floor, The known world. The 200th floor and those above it, where there is no prize money, only fame, and every weapon is allowed.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟠 High. Dangerous ground; a party that picks its fights survives it. (Recommended Level 12-24)

You have climbed to the 200th floor of Heavens Arena. From here there is no prize money, only fame, and when the lift doors opened something in the corridor pushed against you like a wind that nobody else seemed to feel.
🎭 Hisoka Morow, Male, a tall, muscular man dressed like a jester. Narrow amber eyes, pink sash. A painted jester smiling at a boy with a fishing rod, a playing card turning slowly between his fingers.
🎭 Machi Komacine, Female, Member number three of the Phantom Troupe. Blue eyes, white short-sleeved uwagi with purple collar. A small woman with pink hair sewing a severed arm back onto a smiling man, unimpressed.
🎭 Gido, Male, a fighter on the 200th floor of the Heavens Arena whose first fight there cost him. Red robe with a tall pointed hood covering the head. A masked man on a peg leg cackling as a dozen spinning tops whirl toward a boy who cannot see them.
🎭 Riehlvelt, Male, a fighter on the 200th floor of the Heavens Arena with spiky purple hair and yellow. Spiky purple hair, black-grey eyes. A man in a big wheelchair cracking two whips across the ring, grinning at a boy who can barely stand.
🎭 Sadaso, Male, a tall, pale fighter on the 200th floor of the Heavens Arena with white hair, slanted. White hair, slanted narrow eyes, mask-like expressionless face. A one-armed fighter smiling as an invisible hand closes around a boy's throat.
