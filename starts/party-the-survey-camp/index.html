(Party) The Survey Camp

PARTY · the Chimera Ant crisis · 2-5 players · Kite, Gon and Killua · guided
📍 Kakin Survey Forest, The known world. Wild forest in the Kakin Empire where new species still turn up, and a survey camp of tents.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🔴 Severe. Deadly to the unready; the top of this band eats mid-level parties. (Recommended Level 20-30)

Two boys used a card from a game to find a boy's father and landed instead in a forest camp with the man who once saved that boy's life. The man is a Hunter cataloguing new species, and a piece of something very strange has just come in from the coast.
🎭 Gon Freecss, Male, a boy from Whale Island. Large hazel-brown eyes, green high-collared jacket with orange-red trim. A small boy with spiky hair grinning in a crowd of hard-faced adults, a fishing rod on his shoulder.
🎭 Killua Zoldyck, Male, a white-haired boy on a skateboard who has run away from home to take the Hunter Exam. Messy spiky white hair, sharp blue cat-like eyes. A white-haired boy on a skateboard gliding past a line of exhausted runners, not even breathing hard.
🎭 Kite, Male, a tall, gaunt Hunter with long white hair to his knees, a hooked nose and a light blue. Small hazel eyes, light blue sash wound at waist. A gaunt man with knee-length white hair crouching to look a strange animal in the eye in a forest camp.
🎭 Spinner Clow, Female, a young Amateur Hunter called Spin. Long wavy magenta-pink hair, teal-blue eyes, dark purple long-sleeved shirt over white shirt. A young woman in a beret photographing a strange bird, glaring at two boys who have just arrived.
🎭 Banana Kavaro, Female, an Amateur Hunter on Kite's survey team with short fair hair shaved at the sides. Small green eyes. A young woman in baggy overalls sketching the ranks of an insect colony in a notebook.
🎭 Lin Koshi, Male, a short Amateur Hunter on Kite's team with fair hair combed neatly to one side. Squinty eyes, lavender short-sleeved polo shirt tucked into dark trousers. A short young man in a dress shirt checking a species list, stammering out a correction.
