(Party) The Majority Path

PARTY · the 287th Hunter Exam · 2-5 players · Gon, Killua, Kurapika and Leorio · guided
📍 Trick Tower, The known world. A huge cylindrical stone tower with no windows and no stairs on its outside, used as a prison, its inside a maze of traps and puzzle rooms.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟡 Moderate. Real fights at the edges; walk in with your eyes open. (Recommended Level 6-14)

The exam airship set the applicants down on the roof of a prison tower and told them to reach the bottom in seventy-two hours. Five trapdoors dropped five people into one room, and every door from here opens only on a vote.
