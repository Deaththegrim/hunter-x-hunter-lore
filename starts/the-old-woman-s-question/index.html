The Old Woman's Question

HOOK · the 287th Hunter Exam · 1-5 players · guided
📍 Quiz Road, The known world. A road through a small town on the way from the port to the pine, where a hunched old woman stops every applicant and asks a question that has no good answer.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-10)

Halfway between the port and the pine, a little town has put masks on and gathered in its square. A hunched old woman stands at the fork in the road and will not let anyone pass without answering her.
🎭 Quizzing Lady, Female, a tiny, hunched old woman with long grey hair and only two teeth left. Heavy-lidded eyes. A tiny old woman at a fork in the road, two fingers raised, waiting for an answer she does not want.
