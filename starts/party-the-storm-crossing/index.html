(Party) The Storm Crossing

PARTY · the 287th Hunter Exam · 2-5 players · Gon, Kurapika and Leorio · guided
📍 Captain's Ship, The known world. A small passenger ship that carries hopefuls from the islands to the exam's landing port at Dolle Harbor.
Pace: Guided. An authored opening plays out, then the world is yours.
Danger: 🟢 Low. Quiet ground; trouble here is social before it is lethal. (Recommended Level 1-4)

A small ship is carrying hopefuls toward the exam's landing port, and a storm is coming that only the captain saw. Three strangers aboard are about to become friends: a boy from a fishing island, a quiet blond youth and a loud man in a suit.
🎭 Captain, Male, the captain of the ship that carries exam hopefuls from Whale Island. An old bearded captain at the wheel of a heaving ship, laughing at passengers sick over the rail.
🎭 Katzo, Male, a crewman on the Captain's ship that carries applicants from Whale Island toward Dolle. Timid eyes, orange shirt. A thin deckhand in an orange shirt clinging to a rope as a wave breaks over the rail.
