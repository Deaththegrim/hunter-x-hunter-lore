Phantom Troupe

A band of thieves and killers carrying a bounty of the highest class, known across the world by the twelve-legged spider each member wears tattooed with a number. Most of them grew up together in Meteor City. They take what their leader wants, let him enjoy it, then sell it all, and they kill whoever stands in the way; the extermination of the Kurta Clan for its scarlet eyes is laid at their door. There are never more than thirteen of them, a head and twelve legs. A new member is nominated by the others or takes a seat by killing the one who held it. A stranger meets them as a rumour, a ruin or a corpse; the city they came from still counts them as its own, at a distance.
