Hui Guo Rou royal house

The ruling house of the Kakin Empire, a nation of over a billion people on the Azian Continent. Its king, Nasubi Hui Guo Rou, has eight queens and fourteen children, every one called a prince whatever their sex. Thirty years ago the country passed from empire to parliament in what is called the most silent revolution in history, and the economy has grown fast since. In 2000 the king announces that Kakin will lead a great voyage to the Dark Continent, open to all comers, on the Black Whale, and the whole royal family sails with it. A stranger meets the house as a parade, a portrait or a guard who says no.
