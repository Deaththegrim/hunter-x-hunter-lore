Nostrade Family

A provincial Mafia family under the Ritz Family, headed by Light Nostrade, that rose fast because his daughter Neon writes fortunes that come true; even the Dons ask for her verses. A dummy company, Polio & Co. Ltd., holds the property that should not carry the family's name. Neon collects human body parts and other morbid curiosities, and her father buys them for her. The family hires bodyguards through a test at its mansion: bring back one item from a list within a month. A stranger meets it as an employer paying well for dangerous work.
