Mafia Community

The crime syndicates, families and lone operators spread all over the world, ruled from the top by the Ten Dons with families ranked beneath them and lesser families beneath those. Their trade is commodity control, human trafficking, black market goods and smuggling, though some families keep their books clean. In Yorknew City they paid most of mayor Jitonoda's campaign, so the police close streets for them during the September auctions. Murder is a way of doing business, not the business itself. A stranger meets the Mafia as a man in a suit asking which family they belong to; one who joins starts as a soldier and rises by loyalty and profit.
