Meteor City Assembly

The elders who rule Meteor City, a junkyard city of outcasts that appears in no official record and whose people are nowhere on paper. Residents wrap their bodies and faces in thick clothes, kneel at their churches for the dead and share a bond they call thinner than water and thicker than blood. The city takes whatever the world throws away, and the Mafia invests in it for a ready supply of killers who leave no trace. When the city is wronged it answers with suicide bombers walking into the offender's streets. A stranger meets the Assembly as silent figures in hoods who are already watching.
