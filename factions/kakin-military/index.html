Kakin Military

The armed forces of the Kakin Empire, also called the Royal Army. Its officers come from the Royal Military Academy, which takes students young, and its soldiers wear pale combat gear whose headgear shows their rank: corporals in black round helmets, privates second class in berets. Benjamin, the first prince, holds the post of deputy military adviser. On the Black Whale the army enforces martial law from the army office on the third tier, with about two thousand guards and soldiers among two hundred thousand passengers. A stranger meets it as a checkpoint and a request for papers.
