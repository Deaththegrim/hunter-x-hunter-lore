Hunter Exam Commission

The people who run the Hunter Exam each year. Applications close on 31 December and the exam opens on 7 January at a site kept secret until the last day; only a candidate who finds the way, often through a navigator who tests them first, reaches it. Candidates wear numbered badges, the phases change every year and anyone may try as often as they live, but deaths and maimings are common and nobody is told how the scoring works. Examiners are elite Hunters who work unpaid; attacking one ends a candidate's year. A stranger meets the commission as a test they did not see coming.
