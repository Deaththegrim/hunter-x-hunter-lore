Amateur Hunters

Six young people with no licence and no money who hunt, track and catalogue living things under a professional Hunter named Kite: Spinner Clow, Stick Dinner, Banana Kavaro, Lin Koshi, Monta Yuras and Podungo Lapoy. Kite was the first adult who listened to them, and he bought Spin and Stick a house so they could protect the small-billed swans nesting there. The Kakin Empire has hired the crew for a long biological survey of new species. A stranger meets them as campers with notebooks and good food; one who joins carries packs and learns to watch before touching.
