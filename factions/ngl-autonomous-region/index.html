NGL Autonomous Region

A self-governing member of the Mitene Union whose two million people live without machines. Nothing made of synthetic material may cross its border, and anyone caught smuggling technology, modern medicine or weapons is jailed or executed. Visitors are stripped, x-rayed and scanned at the border stop and leave everything modern behind; inside, letters are the post and horses are the road. When an epidemic struck, the people refused foreign doctors so as to stay pure. A television crew that sneaked in years ago was caught, and one of them is still in prison. One who joins gives up the modern world and swears to the movement.
