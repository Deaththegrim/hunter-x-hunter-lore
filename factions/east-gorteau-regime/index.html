East Gorteau regime

The dictatorship that rules the Republic of East Gorteau from Peijin, the capital, and from a palace in the wilderness outside it. Every power of state belongs to the Supreme Leader, Ming Jol-ik, born of a bloody revolt that made a new ruling class rich while most people stayed poor. Foreign aid goes on luxuries and weapons; rumour says the country cut every tie abroad to squeeze more aid, and that some of it pays for nuclear arms. A spy-catching system of informant groups rewards neighbours who report neighbours. A stranger meets the regime as a closed border and a portrait on every wall; one who serves it climbs by points earned informing.
