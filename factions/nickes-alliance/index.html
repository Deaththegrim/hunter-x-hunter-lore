Nickes' Alliance

A great alliance of players inside Greed Island, sixty-three strong at its height, every one of them hired by Battera. Ten founders run it and split the largest share of the reward; the rest are paid by how long they have served and what they risked. Its plan is plain: corner the island's attack and defence spell cards so no one else can fight back, then collect the specified cards at leisure. New members are brought to a hidden base by a roundabout route and must learn every spell card by heart in an hour, or they are made errand runners. A stranger meets it as a recruiter with a pitch.
