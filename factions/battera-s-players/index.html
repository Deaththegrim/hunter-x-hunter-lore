Battera's players

The Hunters and adventurers hired by Battera, an old billionaire who has bought up every copy of the game Greed Island he can find and keeps the consoles in the basement of his castle. Since 1988 he has offered a famous reward to anyone who clears the game, and he uses his money to keep its details out of the press. Tsezguerra, a Jackpot Hunter, runs his tryouts and turns away nearly everyone. Of the more than a hundred Hunters he has sent in, few have come back. A stranger meets the company as an advertisement or a test in a hotel ballroom; one who joins signs a contract and is sent into the game.
