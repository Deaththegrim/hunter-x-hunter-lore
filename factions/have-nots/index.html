Have-Nots

The lowest caste of the Kakin Empire, barred by an unchangeable caste law from public office and military rank. In ancient Kakin, princes who failed to become king were buried with afterlife companions to stop them turning into vengeful ghosts, and the companions were always chosen from the Have-Nots. The custom ended; the caste did not. Camilla, the second prince, has opened her private army to them, built them a ward on her land and given them a soldier's rights, and they now ask of their own accord to be her companions in death. A stranger meets them as servants who will not meet the eye.
