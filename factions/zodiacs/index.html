Zodiacs

The chairman's council: the vice chairman and eleven staff advisors, each a top Hunter whom Netero picked because their skill caught his interest. Each takes a codename from the zodiac and most dress or style themselves to match it, out of loyalty to the old man. They advise on anything the bylaws leave open, take the Association in hand in an emergency and spar with the chairman for his amusement. Hunters treat them with respect; outsiders rarely know their names. A seat is given by the chairman and nothing else, so a Hunter who wants one has to be noticed.
