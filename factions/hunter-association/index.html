Hunter Association

The body that tests and licenses Hunters, answerable to no single government. A licence is earned once, through the yearly Hunter Exam, and is never revoked and never reissued: it opens almost every border and public facility, lets its holder into restricted grounds and records, and is worth a fortune to collectors, so a careless Hunter loses it fast. Licensed Hunters chase rare beasts, lost ruins, treasures, criminals and cures, alone or for a client, and the Association ranks the best of them by stars. Its headquarters stands in Swardani City and its airships carry its people and cargo. A stranger meets it as a card shown at a gate; one who joins passes the exam and climbs by the work done.
