Stencils in the Register

Before he decides anything, Sindri Ahl wants to know whose stencil that is. Corwin Strand keeps the harbour registers back past the year the ship struck, and he opens them for nobody without a reason he likes.

Goal: Trace the stencil from the wreck to the firm that shipped her cargo
