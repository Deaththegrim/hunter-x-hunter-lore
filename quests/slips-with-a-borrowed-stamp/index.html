Slips with a Borrowed Stamp

The tout has moved on from maps to boarding slips for the applicants' ship, with the harbourmaster's stamp copied crookedly at the foot. Anyone who shows one at the gangway will be turned away, and some of the hopefuls have handed over everything they brought to get one.

Goal: See the cheated applicants paid back
