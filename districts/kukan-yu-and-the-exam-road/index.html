Kukan'yu and the exam road

Kukan'yu and the exam road is one of the parts of the known world as this map draws it. The kingdom north of the Yorbian Continent where the 287th Hunter Exam is held: the landing port where applicants gather, the forest of the navigators, the swindlers' streets of Zaban City, and the wetlands, forests and split mountain the exam runs through. Swardani City, home of the Hunter Association, the quiet forests of the Lukso Province and a single great tree that climbs above the clouds are drawn on this part of the map too. Its hub is Dolle Harbor; its other places: Lone Pine Tree, Quiz Road, Zaban City, Tsubashi Street, 287th Hunter Exam Site Restaurant, Exam Tunnel, Milsy Wetlands, Visca Forest Preserve, Split Mountain, Swardani City, Hunter Association Headquarters, Swardani Hospital, Lukso Province, Kurta Village, Nancha City, World Tree, Dolle Fish Auction Hall, Dolle Seafarers' Chapel, Dolle Shipyard, Dolle Coast Guard Station, Gullrest Hostel, Dolle Pinewood, Kessel Road Truck Stop, Ostmere Paddy Farm, Panda Frog Ponds, Zaban Central Station, Zaban City Police Headquarters, Tsubashi Tower, Gilder Lane Pawnbrokers, Hotel Sunwheel, Zaban Free Clinic, Zaban Waterworks, Zaban Technical High School, Black Lantern Gambling Hall, Reedmouth, Ape Hollow, Visca Lakeside Lodge, Visca Ranger Post, Spider Eagle Gatherers' Hut, Northridge Coal Mine, Swardani Central Station, Association Airship Field, Pell Street, Swardani Daily Courier, Swardani Medical University, Swardani Royal Barracks, Nancha Piko Stable, Nancha Bus Terminal, Piko Road, Ferrin Creek Sawmill, Nancha Old Quarter Inn, Treefoot, World Tree Rescue Station, Treefoot Climbers' Outfitter, Milsy Field Station. By land it joins The exam's far sites; by sea Padokea and Kukuroo Mountain (across the western sea), Whale Island (across the open sea), Yorknew City (across the northern strait).
