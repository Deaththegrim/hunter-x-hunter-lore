What to say

The exact lines that set off the known world's systems: travel, Nen, factions and atonement.

  • Board an airship, train or ship: At a city's port or station. Name where you are going; the ticket clerk takes the fare in the scene. Anyone Barred keeps the whole party from boarding.
  • Ask a faction for a job: At the faction's seat, sworn to it, asking an officer.
  • Swear an oath to a faction: In a scene, naming the faction, before someone with the standing to accept it.
  • Atone for killings: On the steps of the Meteor City church, carrying infamy: it falls by 10, at most once every 30 turns.
  • Ask a Nen teacher to take you on: With the teacher in the scene; the first lesson is a quest, and it starts with Ten.
  • Have your aura nodes forced open: Unawakened, with a Nen user in the scene willing to do it: fast and dangerous, and the aura pours off you until you can hold Ten.
  • Train your Nen: Awakened, between fights: training raises the aura you can hold.
  • Hold a Nen state: Awakened: Ten, Zetsu, Ken, Ko or In. A state holds until you drop it; Ken and Ko cost aura.
  • Find your Nen type: Awakened, with a glass of water and a leaf on it.
  • Make your Hatsu: Awakened: name your own ability and say what it does; the narrator weighs what it costs.
  • Imagery training (Conjurers): A Conjurer studies the shape of the thing until it holds; a conjured Hatsu is built on it.
  • Swear a vow on your Nen: Awakened: a limitation or vow makes an ability stronger, and breaking it costs.
  • Everything for one fight: With the Vow: Everything for One Fight: one fight at a power beyond anything, and the Nen is gone after.
