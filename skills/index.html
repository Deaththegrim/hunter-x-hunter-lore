Skills

The skills of Hunter x Hunter: what each covers and how hard things are.

agility

Running, climbing, leaping, falling well and keeping balance on a wall, a rope or a moving roof. A basic success gets there; a great success gets there first, or where nobody thought a person could.

Covers: running, climbing, leaping, falling well and balance on a wall, a rope or a moving roof. Not: moving unseen, which is stealth, and holding on for hours, which is endurance. Easy: climb a ship's mast in a swell. Medium: climb down the outside of Trick Tower past the beasts that nest on it. Hard: jump into Split Mountain's canyon, take a Spider Eagle egg from its web and climb out. Very hard: climb a World Tree's trunk to the top. Extremely hard: dodge a hail of thrown cards in a corridor.

Primary Attribute: Speed

appraisal

The Treasure Hunter's field: the worth of a thing, the forged and the real, auctions, collectors and the market. A basic success tells a fake from the real thing; a great success finds the treasure the seller does not know he holds.

Covers: the worth of goods, art and relics; forgeries; the auction floor, the collector and the Nen-made treasure that shows only to Gyo. Not: bargaining, which is negotiation, and the street's black market, which is streetwise. Easy: spot a cheap fake at a street stall. Medium: pick a Nen-made piece out of a bargain table at the Bull Market. Hard: value a lot in the Southernpiece Catalog. Very hard: value a pair of Scarlet Eyes before the bidding opens. Extremely hard: buy a treasure for a fraction of its worth because the seller did not know it.

Primary Attribute: Wit

assassination

The killing arts of the Zoldyck Family: the silent step, the hand shaped into claws, the joint slipped from its socket, the afterimage, the mind that does not hesitate. A basic success kills or escapes quietly; a great success does it in a crowd and leaves nothing behind.

Covers: the Zoldyck arts: Silent Gaits, the claws, joints slipped to escape bonds, Rhythm Echo's afterimages, a mind that removes hesitation; taught only by a Zoldyck. Not: moving unseen with no killing in it, which is stealth, and a school's fight, which is shingen-ryu. Easy: walk across a floor without a sound. Medium: slip a joint and step out of handcuffs. Hard: pull a heart out with a hand shaped into claws, as Killua Zoldyck did in Trick Tower. Very hard: leave three afterimages with a shifting step. Extremely hard: kill a Mafia don in a guarded room and leave no witness.

Primary Attribute: Speed

awareness

Noticing: the tail in a crowd, the trap in a floor, the lie in a smile, the shape that is wrong. A basic success spots the obvious; a great success spots what was hidden on purpose.

Covers: noticing: the tail in a crowd, the trap in a floor, the lie in a smile, the shape that is wrong. Not: reading aura, which is nen, and solving a whole crime, which is deduction. Easy: spot a pickpocket on a train. Medium: notice that the friendly veteran's juice was opened. Hard: spot the scout hidden among ordinary passengers. Very hard: spot the Kiriko wearing a human shape among the guides. Extremely hard: see the feint before the blow.

Primary Attribute: Wit

beast-lore

The Beast Hunter's field: Magical Beasts, the rare and the undocumented, how they hunt, what they eat and where they break. A basic success names the creature and its danger; a great success finds the weak spot it hides.

Covers: Magical Beasts and wild fauna: Kiriko, the lure-beasts of Milsy Wetlands, the Great Stamp, the Spider Eagle, guard dogs and the undocumented. Not: a trail in the mud, which is tracking, and cooking what was caught, which is gourmet. Easy: tell a Foxbear's tracks from a bear's. Medium: tell the real examiner from the Man-faced Ape that wears his face. Hard: bring down a Great Stamp with a blow to the soft spot on its forehead. Very hard: walk past Mike's kennel and live. Extremely hard: find a creature no book has ever named.

Primary Attribute: Wit

blades

Swords, knives and the hidden blade: the iaido draw, the umbrella sword, the knife from a sleeve. A basic success cuts true; a great success ends the exchange with one draw.

Covers: swords, knives and hidden blades: the iaido draw, the paired swords, the sword in an umbrella, the knife made by a famous killer. Not: a thrown blade, which is thrown-weapons, and a club or staff, which is weapons. Easy: cut a rope in one stroke. Medium: parry a knife with a wooden sword, as Kurapika did. Hard: cut down anyone who steps into your sword's reach before he finishes the step. Very hard: fight with a sword hidden in an umbrella, as Feitan Portor did. Extremely hard: cut a thrown blade out of the air with one draw.

Primary Attribute: Speed

Covers: plants, herbs, forests and trees: the useful, the poisonous, the rare and the ancient. Not: a toxin's effect, which is poisons, and living off the forest, which is wildcraft. Easy: name a medicinal herb by its leaf. Medium: find water in a forest by the plants that grow near it. Hard: find a rare flower in a swamp full of predators. Very hard: climb a World Tree and name what grows on its branches. Extremely hard: find a plant every botanist believed extinct.

Primary Attribute: Wit

Covers: wanted lists, bounties, the habits of fugitives, the Association's rule on hunting a Hunter, and taking a quarry alive. Hard: take a bounty alive and deliver him to the police. Very hard: track a member of the Phantom Troupe through Yorknew City. Extremely hard: take a top-class bounty that veteran hunters refused.

Primary Attribute: Wit

Very hard: hold a team together after its leader dies. Extremely hard: lead a royal guard on a ship of assassins and keep your charge alive.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

conjuration

Conjuration: an object made from aura, real enough to touch, bound by the conditions its maker set. A basic success calls the object up; a great success makes it do what its conditions promise against someone strong.

Covers: making a material object from aura, the only Nen a non-user can see and touch; the imagery training that makes it real; the conditions bound into the object. Not: aura given a property, which is transmutation, and an object already owned, which is manipulation. Easy: conjure a small object you have lived with for months. Medium: conjure a chain that answers your hand, as Kurapika did. Hard: conjure a vacuum cleaner that swallows only what its rules allow, as Shizuku Murasaki did. Very hard: conjure copies of the dead so true that the Mafia believes them, as Kortopi did. Extremely hard: conjure an object whose conditions hold against a stronger user who is trying to break them.

Primary Attribute: Wit

Primary Attribute: Wit

Very hard: keep an emitted creature working while you sleep. Extremely hard: loose a rain of aura shots that wrecks a whole building from a hilltop away.

Primary Attribute: Spirit

Extremely hard: keep fighting with an arm torn off, as Hisoka Morow did at Heavens Arena.

Primary Attribute: Body

Extremely hard: stand in the blast of an anti-tank missile and walk out of the smoke, as Uvogin did.
