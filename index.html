Hunter x Hunter - Dreamer

The lands and seas humanity has charted, drawn as one map: the exam country of Kukan'yu in the north, the auction city of Yorknew at the centre of the Yorbian Continent, the fighting tower of Heavens Arena and the Zoldyck mountain on the north-western continent, Meteor City off every record to the west, the islands of the Mitene Union to the south and the rising Kakin Empire on the Azian Continent to the east. Airships, trains and ships tie them together. Past every edge of the map lies the Dark Continent, which the great powers forbid anyone to reach.
