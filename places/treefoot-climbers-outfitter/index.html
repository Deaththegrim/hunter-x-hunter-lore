Treefoot Climbers' Outfitter

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 2-10 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A climbing shop in a converted barn on the edge of Treefoot, selling ropes, harnesses, gloves and advice. The owner climbed to the crown twice when she was young and now spends her days talking customers out of trying.

Areas

  • Outfitter Bouldering Wall: A plywood wall studded with holds where customers prove they can hang on.
  • Outfitter Shop Floor: Racks of harnesses and boots, rope hanging from the beams.
