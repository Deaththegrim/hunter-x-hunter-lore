Paektae Hill Temple

[Recommended Level: 28-32 · Danger: Severe]

An old temple on a pine-covered hill in the middle of the country, shut since the revolution and kept as a 'cultural site'. A caretaker lives in the gatehouse and sweeps the stair.

Areas

  • Hill Temple Bell Court: A courtyard with a great bronze bell and an empty altar.
  • Hill Temple Stair: A long stone stair through the pines up to the gate.

People here

  • Ilgak (NGL villager)
