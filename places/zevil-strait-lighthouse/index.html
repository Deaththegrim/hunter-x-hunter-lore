Zevil Strait Lighthouse

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 5-10 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A lighthouse on a rock in the strait between the mainland and Zevil Island, kept by a family who live in its cottage.

Areas

  • Lighthouse Keeper's Cottage: The cottage at the base where the keeper's family live.
  • Lighthouse Lamp Room: The lamp room at the top, with its lens and a view of the island.
