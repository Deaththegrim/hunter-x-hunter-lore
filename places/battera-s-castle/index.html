Battera's Castle

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-24 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

An old castle on high ground above walls and cliffs, the property of the billionaire Battera, who has spent fortunes buying every copy of the game Greed Island. Players he hires come by train, sign his contract and go down to the basement, where thirty-two consoles run the game.

Areas

  • Castle Gatehouse: The gatehouse where the train passengers are met and checked in by armed guards.
  • Castle Upper Halls: The ornate upper floors where Battera receives his players.
  • Console Room: The basement room of thirty-two consoles, each with a monitor showing a player's status.
