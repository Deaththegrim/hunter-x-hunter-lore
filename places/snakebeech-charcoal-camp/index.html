Snakebeech Charcoal Camp

[Recommended Level: 1-4 · Danger: Low]

A clearing on the forest's eastern edge where two brothers burn beech into charcoal in turf mounds. Their smoke goes up for days, and they sell the charcoal to the town's stoves and the smokehouse.

Areas

  • Charcoal Camp Bark Hut: A shelter of bark and tarpaulin with two cots and a stove.
  • Charcoal Camp Kilns: Low turf mounds that breathe smoke through holes in their sides.
