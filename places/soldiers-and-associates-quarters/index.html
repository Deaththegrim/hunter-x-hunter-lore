Soldiers' and Associates' Quarters

[Recommended Level: 28-38 · Danger: Deadly]

The living area on Tier 1 for the Royal Army's soldiers and the hundred and fifty provisional Hunters who passed the 289th Hunter Exam with licences good only for the voyage. It is reached from the heavily guarded left side of the princes' district.

Areas

  • Associates' Bunk Rows: Rows of steel bunks with a locker at each and a rota on the wall.
  • Quarters Lower-Tier Passage: The guarded stair and passage that runs from the barracks down through the ship.
