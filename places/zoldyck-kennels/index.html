Zoldyck Kennels

[Recommended Level: 12-24 · Danger: High]

Mike's kennel, behind a stand of giant trees: a pen, a run and a meat store for the family's guard hound, who learns every stranger's face and scent and obeys nobody outside the family.

Areas

  • Kennels Feeding Pens: The great pen and its run, with a trough and a drain in the floor.
  • Kennels Keepers' Shed: The shed where the meat is cut and the rules are pinned to the wall.
