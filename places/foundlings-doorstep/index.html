Foundlings' Doorstep

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 12-16 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A sheltered shelf beside a shrine door where babies are left, and a bell to ring when one is. Whoever answers it takes the child in, and the city counts the child as its own.

Areas

  • Doorstep Bell Shelf: The covered shelf, its boards padded and a bell rope hanging beside it.
  • Doorstep Night Bench: A bench inside the door where a volunteer sleeps sitting up, within reach of the bell.

People here

  • Hanne Bell (Meteor City resident)
