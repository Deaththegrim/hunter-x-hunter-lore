Dolle Shipyard

[Recommended Level: 2-7 · Danger: Low]

Two slipways, a dry dock and a gantry crane at the eastern end of the port, where trawlers and the ferries that carry applicants from the islands are scraped, welded and painted. The yard works all hours and smells of hot metal and paint.

Areas

  • Shipyard Dry Dock: The drained dock where a hull sits on blocks, weeping rust onto the floor.
  • Shipyard Gantry: The crane's walkway high over the yard, open to the wind off the sea.
  • Shipyard Welding Shed: A tin shed full of blue light and the hiss of torches.
