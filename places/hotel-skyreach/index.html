Hotel Skyreach

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 4-12 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The town's grandest hotel, across the plaza from the tower, where rich spectators, sponsors and visiting promoters stay. The upper suites keep telescopes at the windows for watching the tower's lit floors at night.

Areas

  • Skyreach Front Steps: Marble steps and a doorman who knows who tips.
  • Skyreach Lobby Bar: A long bar in dark wood where business is done over whisky.
  • Skyreach Telescope Suites: Top-floor suites with brass telescopes trained on the tower.
