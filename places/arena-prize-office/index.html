Arena Prize Office

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 4-18 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

Where fighters below the 200th floor collect what each win pays. A winner shows the slip the referee signed, the clerk stamps it and slides an envelope through the slot; the envelopes get fatter the higher the floor printed on the slip.

Areas

  • Prize Office Bench: A bench in the corridor outside, where nobody sits for long unless they are waiting for someone.
  • Prize Office Counter: The glassed counter with its slot, its stamp and its queue of bruised winners.

People here
