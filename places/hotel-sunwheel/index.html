Hotel Sunwheel

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 1-6 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A ten-storey hotel near the station with a sign like a sunburst on its roof, clean, plain and fully booked every January. Its bar is where applicants with money size each other up before the exam.

Areas

  • Sunwheel Bar: A dim bar off the lobby with leather booths and a television showing the weather.
  • Sunwheel Roof: The flat roof under the great wheel sign, humming with neon.
  • Sunwheel Rooms: Corridors of identical rooms, each with a narrow bed and a view of the street.

People here

  • Ines Varga (Hunter Exam applicant)
  • Mortimer Quayle (Freelance assassin)
