Wrecked Car Valley

[Recommended Level: 16-28 · Danger: Severe]

A hollow where whole cars are dumped, stacked and stripped down to the frame. Families live in some of the better ones.

Areas

  • Car Valley Parts Pit: A pit where engines, doors and wheels are sorted for the market.
  • Car Valley Stacks: Cars piled four high, with ladders between them and washing on the bonnets.

People here
