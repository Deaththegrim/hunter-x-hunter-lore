West Gorteau Customs Warehouse

[Recommended Level: 18-28 · Danger: Severe]

A bonded customs warehouse near the West Gorteau border where goods seized at the frontier are held until someone claims them or the state sells them off. It is full of things nobody admits to owning.

Areas

  • West Gorteau Bonded Dock: A dock of roller doors where the seized lorries back in.
  • West Gorteau Bonded Racks: High steel racks of sealed crates in rows, lit by strip lights.
