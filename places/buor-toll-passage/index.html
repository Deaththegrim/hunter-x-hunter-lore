Buor Toll Passage

[Recommended Level: 20-30 · Danger: Severe]

The one passage that leads to the central dining hall of Tier 5, where three men of the Buor Family have set up a turnstile and charge passengers to pass.

Areas

  • Buor Instant Food Stall: A trestle table of instant noodles and a kettle.
  • Toll Passage Turnstile: A turnstile bolted across the passage, with a cash box beside it.

People here

  • Sun-bin (Cha-R Family man)
