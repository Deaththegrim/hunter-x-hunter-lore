Black Whale Propeller Hall

[Recommended Level: 24-34 · Danger: Severe]

The machinery hall at the stern of the hull, beside the lowest tier, where the propeller shaft turns day and night. The noise reaches every cabin on Tier 5.

Areas

  • Propeller Hall Control Room: A glassed room of gauges where the engineers watch the drive.
  • Propeller Shaft Gallery: A long gallery beside the turning shaft, slick with oil.
