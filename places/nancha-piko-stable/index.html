Nancha Piko Stable

[Recommended Level: 1-6 · Danger: Low]

A livery stable on the eastern edge of Nancha City where country people leave their animals while they shop. Tall, long-necked Piko stand in the stalls beside the horses and goats, and the stable boy knows which of them bite.

Areas

  • Piko Stable Loft: The loft above the yard, hay to the rafters and a cot where the boy sleeps.
  • Piko Stable Yard: The cobbled yard with its stalls of birds, horses and goats.
