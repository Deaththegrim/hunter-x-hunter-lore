Dalmont Boxing Gym

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 4-10 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A gym in an old warehouse on gym row, where an ageing coach trains boxers for the tower's lower floors. He is honest about what he can teach, which is rare on this street.

Areas

  • Dalmont Coach's Office: A glass box of an office with a desk of old fight records.
  • Dalmont Warehouse Ring: A canvas ring under a skylight with tape on the ropes.
