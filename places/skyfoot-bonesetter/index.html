Skyfoot Bonesetter

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 4-12 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A bonesetter's clinic in the back streets that treats fighters who cannot afford the tower's doctors or do not want their injuries on file. The old man who runs it works fast and talks the whole time.

Areas

  • Bonesetter Treatment Room: A padded table, a chart of the skeleton and a smell of camphor.
  • Bonesetter Waiting Bench: A bench in the corridor where fighters sit with their arms in towels.
