Tsubashi Tower

[Recommended Level: 2-8 · Danger: Low]

The tall office tower that stands beside the exam's address on Tsubashi Street, all glass and marble and corporate nameplates. Every January applicants march into its lobby sure that the exam must be here, and every January the guards march them out again.

Areas

  • Tsubashi Tower Lift Shafts: A bank of lifts and the dark service shafts behind them.
  • Tsubashi Tower Lobby: A marble lobby with a reception desk, turnstiles and two bored guards.
