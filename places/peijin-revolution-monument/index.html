Peijin Revolution Monument

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 26-30 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A bronze figure three storeys tall on a stone base: Ming Jol-ik as the young revolutionary who overthrew the old ruling class, rifle in one hand, the other pointing at the future. Citizens lay flowers on his birthday, and they lay them straight.

Areas

  • Monument Plinth: The base of the statue, its steps covered in rows of flowers and wreaths.
  • Monument Square: The wide paved square in front of the statue, where citizens must bow before crossing.

People here

  • Chae Il-man (East Gorteau soldier)
