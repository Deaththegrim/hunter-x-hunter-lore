Palm's Mansion

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 16-26 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A tall, gloomy house on the rise above Doli City, older than the flats around it and let out by the season to whoever can stand the draughts. The furniture comes with it, and so does the smell of damp in the upstairs hall.

Areas

  • Palm's Dining Hall: A long table under a dusty chandelier, chairs enough for a dozen and only one used.
  • Palm's Doll Shelves: Shelves along a back room crowded with home-made rag dolls and pincushions.
  • Palm's Upstairs Hall: A narrow hall of closed doors where the floorboards complain at every step.
