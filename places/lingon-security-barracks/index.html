Lingon Security Barracks

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 18-26 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The airport police's barracks and motor pool, a long concrete block with a radio room, a dog run and a row of jeeps that patrol the perimeter fence day and night.

Areas

  • Security Barracks Dog Run: A fenced run where the patrol dogs bark at every engine.
  • Security Barracks Radio Room: A small room of radios and a map of the perimeter stuck with pins.
