Bilgai Rest House

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 18-26 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A whitewashed rest house halfway across the Bilgai Desert on the road to Hass, with a water tank, a few beds and a radio to call the city if a vehicle breaks down. Herders water their camels here; drivers fill their radiators.

Areas

  • Bilgai Rest House Tank: A steel tank on stilts and the trough below it where camels and lorries both drink.
  • Bilgai Shade Porch: A deep porch of rugs and cushions where travellers wait out the heat.

People here
