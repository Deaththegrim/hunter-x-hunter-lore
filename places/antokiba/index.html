Antokiba

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-22 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The town of prizes, the nearest town to the Greed Island Starting Point and the first most new players find. Reward posters cover its walls, and on the fifteenth of every month a contest gives its winner a rare card.

Areas

  • Contest Stage: The stage in the square where the monthly contest is held.
  • Poster Wall: The wall of reward posters for lost dogs, odd jobs and rare items.
