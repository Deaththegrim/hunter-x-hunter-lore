Piko Road

[Recommended Level: 1-8 · Danger: Low]

A faint track running east out of Nancha City into the forests of the Lukso Province, six hours by riding bird and far longer on foot. It forks often, its markers are cut low on the trunks where a stranger would not look, and most who follow it end up back where they started.

Areas

  • Piko Road Ford: A shallow ford where the track crosses a cold river on flat stones.
  • Piko Road Night Camp: A clearing where riders once slept by turns with the birds tethered close.
