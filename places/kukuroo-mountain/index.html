Kukuroo Mountain

[Recommended Level: 12-30 · Danger: Severe]

A dormant volcano 3,722 metres high in the Dentora Region, wrapped in cloud, its foot hidden by jungle and ringed by huge stone walls. The Zoldyck Family owns all of it, and the estate runs from the gate to the summit.

Areas

  • Cloud Slopes: The upper slopes above the jungle, lost in cloud.
  • Summit Road: The road to the top, watched from somewhere above.
