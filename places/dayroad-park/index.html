Dayroad Park

[Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: High]

A large municipal park in Yorknew City, with lawns, paths and food stands, where people come to rest away from the auctions.

Areas

  • Food Stands: The stands along the main path.
  • Park Lawns: The wide lawns where people picnic.
