Hunter Association Headquarters

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 2-10 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

The headquarters of the Hunter Association in Swardani City, where the chairman and the twelve Zodiacs meet, the exams are planned and the licences are issued. A licence opens its doors; without one a visitor waits in the lobby.

Areas

  • Association Lobby: The lobby with its reception desks and the card readers that admit Hunters.
  • Records Floor: The floors of files, bounty lists and exam records.
  • Zodiac Chamber: The council room where the chairman meets the Zodiacs.
