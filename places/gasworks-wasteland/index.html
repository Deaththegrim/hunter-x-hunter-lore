Gasworks Wasteland

[Recommended Level: 14-26 · Danger: High]

Where the old gasworks stood on the west edge there is now only rubble, tall weeds and the iron skeleton of a gasholder. Children dare each other to climb it, stray dogs run in packs, and a few homeless men have built shacks under the embankment.

Areas

  • Gasholder Skeleton: The rusted iron frame of the gasholder, open to the sky.
  • Gasworks Embankment Shacks: Shacks of board and tarpaulin against the rail embankment.
  • Gasworks Rubble Field: Acres of broken brick, weeds and snapped pipes.
