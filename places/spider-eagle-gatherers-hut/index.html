Spider Eagle Gatherers' Hut

[Recommended Level: 4-10 · Danger: Low]

A stone hut near the lip of Split Mountain where a few families of egg gatherers have lived for generations. They climb down into the gorge on ropes for the eggs of the Spider Eagle and sell them to the finest kitchens in the world.

Areas

  • Gatherers' Hut Hearth: The single room of the hut, warm around an iron stove.
  • Gatherers' Rope Ledge: A ledge where the ropes are anchored to iron rings over the gorge.
