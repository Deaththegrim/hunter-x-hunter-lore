Church Screening Hall

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-18 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The nave of the big church, turned into a picture house on screening days. A bedsheet is hung for a screen, a projector clatters at the back, and every child in the inner zones crowds in for a cartoon dubbed by the city's own voices.

Areas

  • Screening Hall Backstage: A curtained corner behind the sheet, with a microphone on a cable and scripts on the floor.
  • Screening Hall Pews: Pews and chairs from a dozen dumps, packed shoulder to shoulder on screening days.

People here

  • Dov Sprocket (Meteor City resident)
