Taba Collective Farm

[Recommended Level: 26-30 · Danger: Severe]

A collective farm outside Taba City, its quota painted on a board at the gate and its progress read out over the barn loudspeaker every morning. The farmers keep private plots behind their houses that the state pretends not to see.

Areas

  • Collective Farm Fields: Long fields of maize and potatoes worked by hand.
  • Collective Farm Tractor Shed: A shed with one working tractor and two that are cannibalised for it.
