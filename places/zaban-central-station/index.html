Zaban Central Station

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 1-8 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The city's main railway station, a vaulted hall of glass and iron with lines to the coast and the inland cities. Commuters stream through it in their thousands, and in January it fills with applicants who got off at the wrong stop.

Areas

  • Central Station Concourse: The great hall under the glass roof, with the departures board and the crowds.
  • Central Station Lost Property: A counter and shelves of umbrellas, cases and things nobody came back for.
  • Central Station Lower Concourse: A lower level of left-luggage lockers and kiosks.
