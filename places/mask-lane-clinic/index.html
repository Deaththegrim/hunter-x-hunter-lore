Mask Lane Clinic

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 12-18 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

A clinic in a shipping container run by a resident who trained as a nurse somewhere else and never says where. Most of the medicine on the shelves is out of date; she knows which still works.

Areas

  • Clinic Container Ward: The inside of the container, three cots and a lamp, the walls painted white.
  • Clinic Medicine Shelves: Shelves of bottles and blister packs sorted by colour, dates scratched off.

People here

  • Vesna Kral (Meteor City resident)
