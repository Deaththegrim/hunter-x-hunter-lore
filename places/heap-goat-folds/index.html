Heap Goat Folds

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 12-18 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

Pens of pallet wood where the city's goats are kept, herded out each day to graze the heaps for anything green. They give milk and, now and then, a feast.

Areas

  • Goat Folds Grazing Slope: The heap slope the herd is driven up each morning, picked bare of green.
  • Goat Folds Milking Pen: A pen with a stool and a bucket, where the herders milk at dawn.
