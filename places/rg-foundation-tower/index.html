RG Foundation Tower

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 20-28 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The headquarters of the RG Foundation, a company that has grown fat on government contracts since Kakin's economy began to climb. Its boardroom has a view of the whole harbour, and its directors are invited to every royal function.

Areas

  • RG Foundation Boardroom: A glass-walled room at the top of the tower with a long table and a harbour view.
  • RG Foundation Lobby: A marble entrance hall where security men check every visitor against a list.
