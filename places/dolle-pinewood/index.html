Dolle Pinewood

[Recommended Level: 1-8 · Danger: Low]

The dark pine forest that runs from the back of Dolle Harbor up to the hill of the lone pine. It is a few hours on foot by the right path and a night of going round in circles by any other, and the sounds it makes after dark send most applicants back to the bus.

Areas

  • Pinewood Canopy Walk: High branches where the trees grow close enough for something quick to cross from one to the next.
  • Pinewood Fork Stones: A clearing where three paths meet beside a heap of mossy boulders.
  • Pinewood Ravine: A cleft in the forest floor with a stream at the bottom and a fallen trunk across it.
