VVIP Living Quarters

[Recommended Level: 30-40 · Danger: Deadly]

The residences on Tier 1 for the ship's most important guests from outside the royal family: politicians of the great powers, captains of industry and the bosses of Kakin's three great Mafia families, whose luggage allowance is second only to the king's.

Areas

  • Onior's Cross-Bridge: A private bridge from the Xi-Yu boss's residence to the tier below.
  • VVIP Princes' Wall Corridor: A long guarded corridor along the outer wall of the princes' district.

People here

  • Connelly (Xi-Yu Family man)
  • Fukataki (Have-Not guard)
