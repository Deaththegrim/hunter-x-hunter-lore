Ape Hollow

[Recommended Level: 3-10 · Danger: Low]

A sunken hollow deep in the Milsy Wetlands where the ground is drier and the trees grow close. The fog sits in it like water in a bowl, and anyone lost in the marsh seems to end up here.

Areas

  • Ape Hollow Bone Bed: The floor of the hollow, matted with bones and old clothes.
  • Ape Hollow Lookout Tree: A dead tree on the rim of the hollow, scratched by long nails.

People here

  • Hollowmouth (Man-faced Ape)
