Far Coast Road

[Recommended Level: 5-10 · Danger: Low]

The road that runs along the cliffs from Beeskafmarro to the tower's strait, with one filling station and long stretches of nothing.

Areas

  • Coast Road Cliff Bend: A sharp bend over the sea with a broken guard rail.
  • Coast Road Filling Station: A lone filling station with two pumps and a shop.
