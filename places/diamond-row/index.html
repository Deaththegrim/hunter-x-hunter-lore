Diamond Row

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 10-20 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

One short street where the city's diamond and gold trade lives: cutters upstairs, dealers on the pavement, showrooms with doors that buzz open only for a known face. A stone can change hands three times between one end and the other.

Areas

  • Diamond Row Cutting Lofts: Upstairs lofts of wheels and lamps where the stones are cut.
  • Diamond Row Showrooms: Showrooms of velvet trays behind buzzer doors.

People here

  • Vivienne Castellane (Treasure Hunter)
