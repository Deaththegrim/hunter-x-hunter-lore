Kakin State Lottery Hall

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 20-24 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The state lottery's main hall, where draws are made on a stage under television lights and winners collect at a row of glass windows. People of every caste queue here, the only building in the capital where they stand in one line.

Areas

  • Lottery Hall Draw Stage: The lit stage with its glass drum and a presenter's lectern.
  • Lottery Hall Ticket Windows: A row of barred windows where tickets are sold and prizes paid out.

People here

  • Kong Lingyu (Black Whale passenger)
