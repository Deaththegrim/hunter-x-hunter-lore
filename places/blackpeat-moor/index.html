Blackpeat Moor

[Recommended Level: 20-30 · Danger: Severe]

Open moor south of the villages where peat is cut, stacked and dried for winter fuel. In the long hollows the mist lies until noon, and the peat-cutters tie themselves together with rope in bad weather.

Areas

  • Blackpeat Cutter's Bothy: A stone shelter with a turf roof, where cutters sleep through the season.
  • Blackpeat Cutting Banks: Long black banks where the peat is sliced out with spades and stacked to dry.
