Skyfoot Night Market

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 4-8 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The street market that opens when the evening bouts end, filling three blocks behind the tower with food carts, cheap clothes and televisions on poles replaying the day's knockouts.

Areas

  • Night Market Clothes Stalls: Racks of knock-off fighter jackets and headbands.
  • Night Market Food Lane: A lane of grills, noodle carts and plastic stools.
  • Night Market Replay Poles: A small square of televisions on poles and a crowd arguing over every punch.

People here

  • Lazlo Brask (Mafia soldier)
  • Lupe Navarro (Heavens Arena fighter)
