Princes' Residential District

[Recommended Level: 30-40 · Danger: Deadly]

The walled district on Tier 1 where each of the fourteen princes lives in one of fourteen identical rooms, numbered by birth order, set back to back in two rows with walkways between and walls round them all.

Areas

  • District Gate: The single guarded entrance opposite the banquet hall.
  • Prince's Room Corridor: The walkway between the rows of princes' rooms.
