287th Hunter Exam Site Restaurant

[Recommended Level: 1-10 · Danger: Low]

A small cheap restaurant, Meshidokoro Gohan, on Tsubashi Street, with a cook at the grill and a waitress who shows certain customers to the back room. The food is ordinary; the back room is not.

Areas

  • Descending Room: The back room with its table and steak, which slowly sinks.
  • Restaurant Counter: The counter and grill where the password is given as an order.
