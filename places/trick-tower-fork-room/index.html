Trick Tower Fork Room

[Recommended Level: 6-14 · Danger: Moderate]

Near the bottom of the Majority Path the route splits: a long way round that takes most of the time left, and a short way that opens only when two of the party have locked themselves to the wall in shackles.

Areas

  • Fork Room Long Stair: The winding stair of the long path, which drinks time.
  • Fork Room Partition: The stone partition between the two paths, thinner than it looks.
  • Fork Room Shackle Wall: The wall of manacles that the short door counts before it opens.
