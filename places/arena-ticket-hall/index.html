Arena Ticket Hall

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 4-10 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The hall beside the fighters' entrance where spectators buy their seats. Cheap tickets cover a day on the lower floors with dozens of rings at once; a seat at the big ring for an upper-floor bout sells out within an hour of the posting.

Areas

  • Ticket Hall Tout Steps: The steps outside where men in caps murmur seat numbers at passers-by.
  • Ticket Hall Windows: Forty glass windows under a board of the day's bouts.
