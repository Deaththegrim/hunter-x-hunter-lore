Greed Island Starting Point

[Safe Haven · Recommended Level: 12-20 · No hostile spawns · Downtime protected]

The tower where every player arrives on entering the game, its floor and walls covered in designs. A woman on a floating seat, Eta, greets each newcomer and explains the rules; a stair winds down to an endless grassy plain.

Areas

  • Entry Chamber: The small room where a new player first appears.
  • Rules Hall: The round hall where Eta explains the game.
  • Starting Plain: The grass plain at the tower's foot, stretching away in every direction.

People here
