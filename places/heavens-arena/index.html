Heavens Arena

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 4-26 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The world's most popular fighting site: a tower 991 metres tall with 251 floors, the fourth tallest building in the world, where about four thousand fighters a day climb by winning. More than a billion spectators come each year, and the building has its own restaurants and shops. Anyone may register; most go home sore.

Areas

  • Arena Plaza: The plaza at the foot of the tower where airships, buses and taxis bring the crowds.
  • Ground Floor Rings: The ring-filled first floor where the newcomers are sorted by a single fight.
  • Registration Desk: The desk on the ground floor where a newcomer signs up and is sent to a first ring.
