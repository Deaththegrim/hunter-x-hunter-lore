Tier 1 Supreme Court

[Recommended Level: 30-40 · Danger: Deadly]

The highest court aboard the Black Whale, sitting on Tier 1 with the Supreme Magistrate Cleapatro on its bench. Even under martial law, the court hears cases that touch the royal family.

Areas

  • Supreme Court Bench: The raised bench where the magistrate sits, with a telephone at her elbow.
  • Supreme Court Hearing Floor: The floor below the bench where parties stand to be heard.

People here

  • Cleapatro (Ministry of Justice officer)
