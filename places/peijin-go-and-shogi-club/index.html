Peijin Go and Shogi Club

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 26-30 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A club on the first floor above a tea shop, where the capital's go and shogi players meet in the afternoons. It is less grand than the Gungi hall and likes itself better for it. The national champions of both games are members and come in to play the regulars.

Areas

  • Go and Shogi Club Champions' Wall: A wall of framed names, the two current champions at the top.
  • Go and Shogi Club Room: Tables of go and shogi boards under a cloud of tea steam and smoke.
