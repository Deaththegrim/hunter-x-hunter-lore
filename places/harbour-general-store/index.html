Harbour General Store

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 1-4 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

The only shop on the island that sells anything not caught in the sea: tins, rope, batteries, school exercise books, tobacco, boots and sweets. What it lacks it orders from the mainland and you wait a month.

Areas

  • General Store Back Room: A storeroom of crates waiting for the next boat out or just come off one.
  • General Store Counter: The single counter with its till, its jars of sweets and its order book.
