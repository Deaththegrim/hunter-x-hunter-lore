Roto Coal Mine

[Recommended Level: 18-28 · Danger: Severe]

A deep coal mine on the Rokario side of the Roto Mountains, with a winding tower, a washing plant and a village of company houses down the valley. The seam is old and the shafts go further every year.

Areas

  • Roto Company Row: A row of identical grey houses with coal sacks at every door.
  • Roto Mine Lamp Room: A room of racks where each miner hands in his tally and takes his lamp.
  • Roto Pithead: The yard under the winding tower where the cage brings the miners up.
