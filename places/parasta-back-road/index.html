Parasta Back Road

[Recommended Level: 8-18 · Danger: Moderate]

A narrow country road that leaves the highway and wanders through fields before rejoining it near Parasta. Locals use it to dodge roadworks.

Areas

  • Back Road Farm Gate: A farm gate where the road narrows to a single lane.
  • Back Road Field Verge: A grass verge between the road and the fields.
