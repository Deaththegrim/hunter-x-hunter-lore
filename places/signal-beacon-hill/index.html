Signal Beacon Hill

[Recommended Level: 22-28 · Danger: Severe]

A bald hill above the forest where a bonfire is kept stacked and ready. With no radio in the country, a lit beacon is the fastest warning there is, and the next beacon along the hills answers it.

Areas

  • Beacon Hill Bonfire: The great stacked pile of dry wood and tar on the hilltop, ready for a torch.
  • Beacon Hill Watcher's Hut: A turf hut where the watcher sleeps by day and watches by night.
