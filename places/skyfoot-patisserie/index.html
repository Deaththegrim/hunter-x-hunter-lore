Skyfoot Patisserie

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 4-6 · Danger: Low · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A cake and sweet shop on the plaza road whose window draws children like a magnet. Its owner keeps a framed photo of the best customer she ever had: a white-haired boy, years ago, who came in every day.

Areas

  • Patisserie Back Bakery: A hot bakery where the chocolate is tempered on marble.
  • Patisserie Window Counter: A counter of cakes under glass that the children press their noses to.
