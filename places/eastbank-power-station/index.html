Eastbank Power Station

[Recommended Level: 14-24 · Danger: High]

Four brick chimneys on the east bank mark the station that lights the downtown towers. Coal comes in by the trainload, steam goes up day and night, and the turbine hall is loud enough that the men inside talk with their hands.

Areas

  • Eastbank Coal Siding: A siding where wagons tip coal into the hoppers.
  • Eastbank Switching Yard: A fenced yard of transformers and breakers behind razor wire.
  • Eastbank Turbine Hall: A long hall of humming turbines and steel walkways.
