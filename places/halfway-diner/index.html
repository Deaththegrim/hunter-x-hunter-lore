Halfway Diner

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 6-14 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A diner halfway along the highway between the town and Parasta. Truckers stop for coffee and the jukebox, and tourists stop because it is the only lit window for miles.

Areas

  • Diner Counter: A long counter of stools and a coffee urn.
  • Diner Truck Park: A gravel car park of trucks.
