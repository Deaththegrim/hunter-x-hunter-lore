Binolt's Clearing

[Recommended Level: 14-20 · Danger: High]

A hollow in the badlands on the way to Masadora, closed in by walls of eroded rock, where the ground is trampled flat and the stone is scarred at head height. Players say a man with scissors lives near it and that it is wiser to walk around.

Areas

  • Clearing Fire Ring: A ring of stones blackened by many fires, with bones in the ashes.
  • Clearing Thrown Rocks: The open ground strewn with rocks thrown in a long fight.
