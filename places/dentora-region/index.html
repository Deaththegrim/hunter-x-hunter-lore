Dentora Region

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 6-12 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A region of the Republic of Padokea, a country anyone may enter without papers. Its market town lives off the tourists who come to ride a bus up into the mountains and look at the gate of the Zoldyck Family, the most famous assassins in the world, whose home is the whole of Kukuroo Mountain.

Areas

  • Dentora Bus Terminal: The terminal where the tour bus and the long-distance coaches leave, plastered with tour posters of the mountain.
  • Dentora Market: The open market of fruit stalls and souvenir sellers.
  • Mountain View Inn: A cheap inn with a view of the clouded peak, popular with bounty hunters on their way up.
