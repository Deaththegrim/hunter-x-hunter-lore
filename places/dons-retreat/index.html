Dons' Retreat

[Recommended Level: 18-28 · Danger: Severe]

The top floors of a private tower that the heads of the Mafia take every September, one office for each Don and a round room where they meet. No name is on the building and no lift reaches those floors without a key.

Areas

  • Dons' Guarded Landing: A landing with armed men, a metal detector and a single lift door.
  • Dons' Round Room: A round room under a skylight, ten chairs at one table.

People here

  • Corrado Bellini (Mafia bodyguard)
