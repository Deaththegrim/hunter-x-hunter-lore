Licensed Hunter

You passed the Hunter Exam and carry the card to prove it: free passage through most public places, doors that open in closed countries, and the bylaw that says a Hunter is always hunting something.

Gives: Wit +1; Lore +1, Tracking +1; Health +10 (maximum and starting); unlocks as you train: Hunter's Catch (Wildcraft 6, with Rod and Line), Licence at the Door (Lore 6), Tavern Intelligence (Lore 12), Recognised by Peers (Lore 18), Remarkable Work (Lore 24); starts with Hunter Licence Card.
Pairs well with: Beast Hunter, Ruins Hunter, Blacklist Hunter. The fields the licence opens.

Primary Attribute: Wit
