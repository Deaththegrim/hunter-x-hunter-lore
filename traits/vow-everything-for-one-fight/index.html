Vow: Everything for One Fight

Once, in the worst moment of your life, you can give up all your potential and every bit of Nen you will ever have for the strength to win one fight. You will win it. Then the body pays. There is no cure. It is open only to one who has already staked their life. The vow: once in your life, at a moment of your choosing, you may call it; you win that fight far above your level, and then your Nen is gone for good and your body fails, with no cure to be had.

Gives: technique All In (All the strength you would ever have grown into and every scrap of Nen you would ever have, poured into this one fight. Greatly improves the next action of the one fight you gave everything for. Once, ever).
Pairs well with: Enhancer, Vengeful. The last vow.
