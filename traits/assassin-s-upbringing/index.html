Assassin's Upbringing

You were raised the way the great assassin families raise their own: poison in the food, current in the punishments and killing taught before reading. It is offered only to a character who carries the conditioning it leaves.

Gives: Speed +1; Assassination +2, Poisons +1; Health +10 (maximum and starting); unlocks as you train: Soundless Step (Assassination 6), Pin From the Dark (Assassination 12, with Thrown Weapons), Slipped Joint (Assassination 12), Clawed Hand (Assassination 18), Afterimage Step (Assassination 24); starts with Assassin's Throwing Pins (damage +1 while equipped); resists poison, lightning.
Pairs well with: Poison Specialist, Master of Disguise. The family trades.

Primary Attribute: Speed
