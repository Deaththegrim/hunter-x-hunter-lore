Era: Yorknew City

The end of August and the first days of September 1999. The Mafia's auctions bring the world's collectors to Yorknew City, and the Phantom Troupe comes to the city as well.

Gives: no bonus at creation; what it does comes in the story.
Pairs well with: Mafia Bodyguard, Auction House Hand. The auction city.
