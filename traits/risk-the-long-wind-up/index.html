Risk: The Long Wind-Up

Your strongest blow needs a wind-up: a chant, a stance, a breath held. While you wind up you are wide open. The risk is what makes it hit so hard.

Gives: technique Wound-Up Blow (You chant, you set your stance, you take the risk of standing there open, and the blow comes out with everything. Greatly improves the next Nen strike made after an uninterrupted wind-up).
Pairs well with: Ko Striker, Reckless. The blow that costs the open moment.
