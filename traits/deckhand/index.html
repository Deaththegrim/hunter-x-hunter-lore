Deckhand

You worked the decks of ships on long voyages, hauling lines and keeping your feet in a storm.

Gives: Seafaring +1, Might +1; technique Deckhand Habit (A habit from the life before. Greatly improves the next action to work a ship, keep footing on a heaving deck or haul something heavy).
Pairs well with: Seaport Born, Mimbo Born. The decks.

Primary Attribute: Body
