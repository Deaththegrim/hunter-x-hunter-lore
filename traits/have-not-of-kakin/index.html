Have-Not of Kakin

You were born at the bottom of Kakin's ladder, in the caste barred from office, and you learned early that nobody upstairs would help.

Gives: Body +1; Endurance +1, Streetwise +1; Health +10 (maximum and starting); technique Bottom of the Ladder (A habit of home. Greatly improves the next action to go unnoticed by the powerful or to get by on almost nothing).
Pairs well with: Dockside Labourer, Grudge Against the Powerful. The ones at the bottom.

Primary Attribute: Body
