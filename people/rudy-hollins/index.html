Rudy Hollins

A gangly twenty-year-old in a security blazer a size too big, who walks the galleria's back corridors with a torch and a key ring and spends most of his shift reading comics on an upturned crate by the loading bay. He applied to the airport police twice and failed the eye test both times.

Speaking Style: Easy and drawling, a young guard who sounds pleased to be asked anything at all.
Combat Role: none; a heavy torch waved about and a whistle he forgets to blow
Relationship Hook: A lanky young guard dozing on an upturned crate in a bare corridor, a comic open on his chest.

Personality

  • Core: waves at everyone, guards and thieves alike; dreams aloud about the airport police; swears his naps are only resting his eyes
  • Quirk: reads comics on duty
