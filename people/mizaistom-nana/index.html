Mizaistom Nana

The Zodiacs' Ox, a tall, lean, bearded man in a black-spotted white coat and a horned hat, with a black patch round his left eye and a horned cow on the back of his coat. He is a two-star Hunter of crime, a lawyer, and the manager of a private security company. He observes in silence, thinks before he acts and lives by a strict code.

Speaking Style: A low, measured, deliberate voice that weighs each word like evidence and gives a verdict rather than an opinion.
Combat Role: an ability that turns a fight into a trial, coloured cards shown to judge and bind
Relationship Hook: A tall bearded man in a cow-spotted coat silently holding up a red card.

Personality

  • Core: gives an opponent the chance to explain; lives by a strict personal code; slow to anger, terrible once angry
  • Quirk: silent, analytical and rigorous
