Lorenz Abel

The chief pilot and charter manager at the Parasta blimp works, a lean man in a leather flying jacket with a pilot's watch and a smile for every client. He keeps the list of pilots and their rates on the desk and flies the difficult jobs himself.

Speaking Style: Easy and warm, a pilot's voice that sounds as if everything is under control, especially when it is not.
Combat Role: none; a pilot's nerve and a blimp he can take up in minutes
Relationship Hook: A pilot in a leather jacket running his hand along the seam of a freshly patched blimp.

Personality

  • Core: confident and charming; checks every seam twice; flies the jobs nobody else will
  • Quirk: keeps no record when asked
