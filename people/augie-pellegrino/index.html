Augie Pellegrino

The owner of the desert tow yard, a short, barrel-chested man of forty-five with grease worked so deep into his hands it never comes out. He drives into the desert for any broken car at any hour, and he will fix anything with an engine.

Speaking Style: Easy and chatty, a mechanic who explains your engine to you whether you asked or not.
Combat Role: a mechanic's brawl; a heavy wrench and the car hoist dropped at the right moment
Relationship Hook: A short, greasy mechanic in coveralls leaning on a car hoist, frowning at a bag of papers on his bench.

Personality

  • Core: explains engines to everyone; talks to his dog as if it were a partner; keeps everything in case it might be useful
  • Quirk: never asks whose car it was
