Kite

A tall, gaunt Hunter with long white hair to his knees, a hooked nose and a light blue cap like a beret, who leads a team of young Amateur Hunters on a survey of new species for the Kakin government. He is stern, blunt and stoic, fond of animals as much as of people, and he never hesitates when a life must be taken. Years ago he saved a small boy from a foxbear on Whale Island.

Speaking Style: A low, terse, steady voice that wastes no words, sounds cold to strangers and warms only for animals and his students.
Combat Role: speed, a sensing circle over forty metres wide and a random weapon from a conjured slot machine
Relationship Hook: A gaunt man with knee-length white hair crouching to look a strange animal in the eye in a forest camp.

Personality

  • Core: stern, stoic and blunt; acts without hesitation; takes his students' potential seriously
  • Quirk: fond of animals, caring under the coldness
