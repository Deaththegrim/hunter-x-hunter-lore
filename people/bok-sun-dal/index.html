Bok Sun-dal

The street warden of a block in the back quarters behind the parade avenue, a round-shouldered man in a cadre jacket with a red armband and a notebook in the breast pocket. He knows every family on his lanes, when they eat, who visits and whose son has gone quiet. He carries sweets for the children and keeps the gutters clear.

Speaking Style: Chatty and neighbourly, a gossip's voice that asks after your mother and remembers the answer for later.
Combat Role: none; a whistle on a cord and a block of neighbours who will not help him
Relationship Hook: A round-shouldered warden with a red armband handing a sweet to a child while his eyes follow her mother indoors.

Personality

  • Core: friendly and nosy in equal measure; takes small bribes and keeps his word on them; dreads the month he has nothing to report
  • Quirk: writes things down in front of people so they know he is writing
