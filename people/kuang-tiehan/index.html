Kuang Tiehan

A foreman of the spring foundry at the great shipyard, a scarred, soot-black man with forearms burned to leather. His shift casts springs taller than houses, and he can tell a bad pour by the colour of the steel before any instrument.

Speaking Style: Hoarse and shouted, a foundry foreman's voice made for the roar of furnaces.
Combat Role: a foundry brawler with a long iron rake and no fear of heat
Relationship Hook: A soot-black foreman shielding his eyes before a furnace as a spring taller than a house glows behind him.

Personality

  • Core: judges steel by its colour; shouts even when it is quiet; never takes his gloves off
  • Quirk: keeps a list of the springs he did not trust
