Teradein Neutral

A two-star Hunter of heads and a specialist in human resources, an older man with well-kept grey hair and hazel eyes in a teal suit and yellow tie. He has charisma and a gift for leadership, and he is honest to a fault.

Speaking Style: A dignified, charismatic, older man's voice, honest and earnest, that rallies a crowd.
Combat Role: a Hunter's Nen and the people he leads
Relationship Hook: A grey-haired man in a teal suit rallying a group of Hunters to his cause.

Personality

  • Core: a natural leader; naive about rivals; earnest
  • Quirk: charismatic and honest
