Sarahell

The captain of the second prince Camilla's guard, an imposing woman with short fair hair spiked to the left, eyeliner at the corners of her eyes and deep lines round her mouth. Like all Camilla's guards she is one of the Have-Nots, raised to a soldier's standing by the prince. She is devoted to Camilla and eager to die for her, but she reminds the others that their deaths must count.

Speaking Style: Hard, clipped and fervent, a captain's voice that preaches Camilla's cause and keeps her comrades in line.
Combat Role: not much stronger than an ordinary soldier, she works by disguise and a curse cast at a distance
Relationship Hook: An imposing spiky-haired woman in a guard's suit outside a prince's door, her face set like a mask.

Personality

  • Core: devoted to Camilla; fervent but strategic; keeps the others in line
  • Quirk: a master of disguise
