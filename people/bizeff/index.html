Bizeff

The Secretary of State of East Gorteau, a tired man with white hair combed to the right, thick eyebrows and a chiselled jaw, in a black pinstripe suit and violet tie. Every word between the country and the world, and every order between the leader and the nation's organisations, passes through him. He is exhausted all the time.

Speaking Style: Weary and official, a bureaucrat's careful tone that turns oily and eager in private.
Combat Role: none; a bureaucrat who survives by being the only man who knows how the country works
Relationship Hook: A tired official in a pinstripe suit signing papers at a palace desk while armed men pass behind him.

Personality

  • Core: exhausted and dutiful in public; survives by being indispensable; bends to whoever holds the gun
  • Quirk: lecherous in private
