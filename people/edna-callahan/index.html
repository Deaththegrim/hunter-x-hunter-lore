Edna Callahan

The terminal's ticket clerk, a stout grey-haired woman in a uniform cardigan with a voice that carries across twenty bays; since half the departures board went dark she calls the coaches out herself. She knows every night driver.

Speaking Style: A brass-lunged announcer's bellow, flat and unarguable, that carries over twenty bays.
Combat Role: none; she shouts for the drivers and they come
Relationship Hook: A stout grey-haired clerk leaning out of a ticket window and calling across a waiting hall.

Personality

  • Core: loud; fair; hates the families' men in her hall
  • Quirk: soft on runaways
