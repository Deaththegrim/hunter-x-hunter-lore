Riehlvelt

A fighter on the 200th floor of the Heavens Arena with spiky purple hair and yellow headgear who moves in a large motorised wheelchair, paralysed from the waist down by his welcome to the floor. He fights with whips, wants to be a Floor Master for the money and fame, and fights only those weaker than himself.

Speaking Style: A sneering, bullying voice that boasts from a wheelchair and wilts when stared down.
Combat Role: whips charged with aura from a motorised wheelchair
Relationship Hook: A man in a big wheelchair cracking two whips across the ring, grinning at a boy who can barely stand.

Personality

  • Core: ruthless and bullying; greedy; braver than his friends, barely
  • Quirk: fights only newcomers
