Desmond Pryce

A heavy-set guard of fifty who has sat in the brick hut at the private apron's gate for eleven years, lifting the barrier for black cars and writing their plates in a register nobody has ever asked to read. He chews liquorice instead of smoking because of the fuel lines, and he keeps a transistor radio tuned to the racing.

Speaking Style: Slow and dry, a man who answers a question with the fewest words that will close it.
Combat Role: none; he drops the barrier arm and radios the barracks, then stays inside the hut
Relationship Hook: A heavy guard in a brick gatehouse lifting a striped barrier arm for a black car without looking at the driver.

Personality

  • Core: says yes or no and nothing in between; never looks into a car, only at its plate; keeps the racing results better than the gate log
  • Quirk: chews liquorice when he is nervous, and chews a lot
