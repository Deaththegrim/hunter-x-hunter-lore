Zebro

A balding, middle-aged groundskeeper at the foot of Kukuroo Mountain who looks like a gate guard and is really the estate's cleaner. He opens the first door of the Testing Gate himself with real effort, lets visitors through the small side door if they insist, and clears up after the family's hound has eaten the intruders.

Speaking Style: A friendly, tired, middle-aged man's voice, slow and good-humoured, with a groan when he lifts something.
Combat Role: none he uses; real strength, and the estate's hound does the rest
Relationship Hook: A balding guard straining to push open a vast door while three visitors watch in disbelief.

Personality

  • Core: accommodating and kind; unshockable after years of cleaning up; good friends with Seaquant
  • Quirk: proud of opening the gate and afraid of failing it
