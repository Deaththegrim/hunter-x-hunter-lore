Sabazushi

A rotund man in his fifties with short fair hair, a darker moustache, a square face and a flat nose, in a camouflage uniform and a beret. He commands the armed guard at the castle where a billionaire keeps his game consoles running, and he runs it like a payroll: shifts posted, wages paid on the day, no questions about the rooms upstairs.

Speaking Style: Heavy and plain-spoken, a sergeant's voice that delivers bad news straight and then offers coffee.
Combat Role: a professional soldier with a rifle, a radio and a squad behind him, and no Nen to speak of
Relationship Hook: A heavy man in a beret and camouflage stopping at a castle gate to tell three Hunters something they will not like.

Personality

  • Core: runs his men by the clock; pays and expects to be paid; keeps out of his employer's business
  • Quirk: kinder than his uniform
