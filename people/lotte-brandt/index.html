Lotte Brandt

The baker, a broad, flour-dusted woman who lights her brick oven at three in the morning so the crews can take a loaf to sea. She is the first person awake on the island and the first to see who comes home late, and she has the tact never to mention it.

Speaking Style: Warm and drowsy, a baker's soft early-morning voice that hums between sentences.
Combat Role: none; a long wooden peel and the oven's heat
Relationship Hook: A flour-dusted baker sliding a loaf onto a windowsill in the dark before dawn.

Personality

  • Core: sees everything at dawn and says nothing; feeds the crews before herself; shy about kindness
  • Quirk: hums while she works
