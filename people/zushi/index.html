Zushi

A small, brown-haired, heavy-browed boy in a karate gi over a black turtleneck who climbs the Heavens Arena under his master Wing. He is earnest, hardworking and formal, loyal to Wing and honest even when it means disagreeing with him, and he fights well above his weight.

Speaking Style: An earnest, formal, eager boy's voice that answers with crisp respect and gets very serious about training.
Combat Role: Shingen-ryu kung fu with a stance that leaves few openings, guarded by an aura he can hold in a fight
Relationship Hook: A small boy in a gi bowing deeply to his opponent before flooring a man four times his size.

Personality

  • Core: earnest and hardworking; loyal and honest with his master; respectful and formal
  • Quirk: determined to catch up with those ahead
