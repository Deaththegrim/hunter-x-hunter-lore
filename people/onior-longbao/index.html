Onior Longbao

The boss of the Xi-Yu Family, the largest and richest of Kakin's three Mafia houses, a corpulent man with deep-lined cheeks, small eyebrows, a tuft of fair hair on top and two long scars across his forehead. He looks very like the king, his half-brother. He lives in the VIP area of the first tier and crosses to the lower decks by a drawbridge only soldiers and family heads may use.

Speaking Style: Heavy and philosophical, a rich man's slow voice that talks of balance and order between mouthfuls.
Combat Role: never shown fighting; a boss who commands the largest family on the ship
Relationship Hook: A corpulent scarred man at a laden table, chopsticks in hand, talking calmly about balance.

Personality

  • Core: philosophical about power; patient and calculating; lives in luxury and secrecy
  • Quirk: values balance above all
