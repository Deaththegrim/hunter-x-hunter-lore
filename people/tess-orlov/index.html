Tess Orlov

A night clerk at an internet café near the central station, short and bright-eyed, her hair in two short bunches, in a denim jacket covered in pin badges. She passed the Hunter Exam the year before last and still works the desk, because the licence turned out to need a plan she has not made yet.

Speaking Style: Quick and chirpy, a night-shift voice running on vending-machine coffee and good news.
Combat Role: an exam survivor with good reflexes and no art; she runs, climbs and throws whatever is close
Relationship Hook: A young woman in a badge-covered denim jacket swiping a card at a café desk, monitors glowing behind her.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful; curious about everyone's searches; unsure what to do next
  • Quirk: careless with her licence
