Johness

A tall, muscular man in early middle age with unkempt blond hair, sideburns running into a moustache and sunken blue eyes, in the brown shirt and olive trousers of the tower's lowest tier. Zaban City remembers him as Johness the Dissector, its worst mass murderer, and the guards still walk past his bars in pairs.

Speaking Style: A low, slow, savouring voice with no interest in deals, only in what his hands will do next.
Combat Role: a strangler and dismemberer who tears people apart with his bare hands and wants nothing but a kill
Relationship Hook: A hulking blond prisoner throwing off a hooded cape on a stone platform, hands spread like hooks.

Personality

  • Core: craves murder above freedom; contemptuous of bargains; blind to a smaller predator
  • Quirk: enjoys describing what he will do
