Matthias Brenner

The eldest son of the dairy family on the high pasture, a big, handsome young man of twenty-four in a wax jacket and wellingtons who brings the herd down every evening and drives the milk lorry to the road stand at dawn. He is the best worker on the farm and the least often there.

Speaking Style: Easy and cocky, a farmer's son who sounds like he has been out all night because he has.
Combat Role: a strong farmhand's fists and a lorry he drives too fast
Relationship Hook: A big young farmer in a wax jacket heaving a milk churn onto a roadside stand at dawn, mountains behind.

Personality

  • Core: charming and reckless; works hard when he is there; loves the farm and wants more
  • Quirk: lies to his father
