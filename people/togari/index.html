Togari

A Hunter and former examiner with blue hair and a face slashed with big scars, dressed in furs with bandaged wrists and legs. He sits in on the third phase with Lippo. He is proud and self-confident, and he has trained for a year for one purpose.

Speaking Style: A proud, tight, bitter voice that keeps trying to sound calm and keeps failing whenever a certain name comes up.
Combat Role: a Hunter's training sharpened by a year of hate, and not enough
Relationship Hook: A scarred man in furs stepping out of a hidden room to face a smiling jester.

Personality

  • Core: proud and self-confident; trains hard and believes it is enough; underestimates his enemy
  • Quirk: consumed by a grudge
