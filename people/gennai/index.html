Gennai

The butler who keeps the proving ground, a tall, rawboned man in a rubber-soled version of the black suit, with electrician's pliers in his breast pocket. He wires the posts, sets the current, repairs the craters and keeps a respectful distance whenever one of the family's children is practising.

Speaking Style: Measured and deep, a man who speaks in readings and tolerances and pauses to check a figure before he finishes a sentence.
Combat Role: a strong butler's Nen and reach, live cable used as a whip and a lineman's knowledge of where current runs
Relationship Hook: A tall butler with a coil of cable on his shoulder checking a meter while, behind him, a child in a kimono throws paper.

Personality

  • Core: exact about numbers; says nothing about what he thinks of the work; fixes things that are not yet broken
  • Quirk: keeps his distance from the children and watches them anyway
