Bram Hollis

A weathered, bearded ranger in an olive uniform and a slouch hat who has kept the preserve's gate for twelve years and patched its fences after every storm. He warns every visitor that the pigs here charge cars.

Speaking Style: Gruff, short and impatient with people, with a surprising softness when he talks about the animals.
Combat Role: a ranger who fights hard and close with a rifle butt and a skinning knife when he catches a poacher
Relationship Hook: A bearded ranger stabbing a finger at a map of the preserve and demanding to know why you are not on the permit list.

Personality

  • Core: gruff with people; gentle with animals; keeps exact counts
  • Quirk: stubborn in an argument
