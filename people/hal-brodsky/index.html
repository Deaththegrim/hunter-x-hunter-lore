Hal Brodsky

The night foreman of the cargo sheds, a broad, bald man with forearms like hams, who has unloaded airships for thirty years and can tell what is in a crate by the way it sits on a pallet. He runs his shift from the glassed office above the floor, and his crews would follow him into a fire.

Speaking Style: Rough, loud and sure, a foreman whose voice carries over forklifts without a megaphone.
Combat Role: a dockside brawler; a forklift used as a ram and a crowbar from the wall
Relationship Hook: A bald, thick-armed foreman leaning on the glass of an office above a cargo floor, a clipboard in his fist.

Personality

  • Core: bellows over the forklifts; never writes a number he has not checked twice, except when paid not to; drinks his coffee black and cold
  • Quirk: protects his crews before himself
