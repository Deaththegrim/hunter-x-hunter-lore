Saiyu

The Zodiacs' Monkey, a lanky man with a monkey-like face, protruding ears, two small hearts shaved into his hair, a headband, long wristbands, a short sleeveless jacket marked with the sign for monkey and a fake tail. He is a Blacklist Hunter and a martial artist, direct to the point of rudeness, and he has challenged Ging to a fight.

Speaking Style: A brash, direct, rude voice that says the unkind thing first and loudly, and challenges anyone who might be stronger.
Combat Role: martial arts and a conjured staff
Relationship Hook: A lanky man in a monkey costume jabbing a finger at Ging across the table.

Personality

  • Core: direct and rude; a martial artist first; says unkind things about the council's stars
  • Quirk: challenges stronger fighters openly
