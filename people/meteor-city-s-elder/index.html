Meteor City's Elder

One of the elders who sit in the Assembly, wrapped so thoroughly in dirty rags that nothing of him shows but his eyes, a mouthpiece strapped over his nose and mouth. Residents hurt by someone from outside come to his hut and tell him what happened. He hears every case through to its end and promises nothing.

Speaking Style: Muffled by the mouthpiece, dry and patient, each sentence ending a moment after it seems finished.
Combat Role: none of his own; he sends another body to do his fighting and stays behind the smoke
Relationship Hook: A shape of rags on a low stool behind drifting smoke, eyes glinting above a strapped mouthpiece.

Personality

  • Core: hears a whole grievance without interrupting once; answers a question with a longer silence; burns herbs until the room is hard to see across
  • Quirk: moves people like pieces on a board, kindly or not
