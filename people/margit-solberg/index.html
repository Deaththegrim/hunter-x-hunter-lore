Margit Solberg

The doctor of Dentora Clinic, a tall, quiet woman in her sixties with her hair in a long grey plait. She treats sprains from the mountain paths, food poisoning from the market and, once in a long while, something worse carried down from the gate road.

Speaking Style: Unhurried and low, a country doctor's voice that makes a sprain sound like nothing and a bite sound like something she has seen before.
Combat Role: none; a doctor's hands and the clinic's ambulance
Relationship Hook: A tall grey-haired doctor behind a pharmacy counter, unhurriedly filling a prescription while a tourist shouts.

Personality

  • Core: calm in any emergency; asks no questions a patient does not want; keeps records nobody else sees
  • Quirk: lets complainers wait
