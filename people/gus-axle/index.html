Gus Axle

The driver of the last bus, a squat man in a filthy cap who spends the hours between runs lying in the pit under his own axle. He carries anyone, takes whatever a passenger can give, and drives the long round out to the valleys after dark when no other driver will.

Speaking Style: Rough and drawling, words chewed with a grin, the voice of a man who swears at engines with real affection.
Combat Role: a driver who makes his bus the weapon, and a tyre iron when the bus is not enough
Relationship Hook: A stocky man sliding out from under a glassless bus on a board, wrench in hand, cap black with oil.

Personality

  • Core: calls his bus 'old girl' and means it; never refuses a passenger, never waits for one; hums through a cracked tooth
  • Quirk: drives faster the worse the road
