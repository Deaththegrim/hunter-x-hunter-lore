Zeno Zoldyck

Killua's grandfather, a short old man with a long drooping moustache, his hands clasped behind his hunched back, whose clothes carry the slogans one kill a day and never retire. He was head of the family before Silva and is still one of the finest Nen users alive. He is witty, pragmatic and talkative, and he says he has never killed anyone he was not paid to kill.

Speaking Style: A dry, chatty old man's voice that grumbles about money and work and drops to a level, deadly calm the instant he decides to fight.
Combat Role: an old master's aura shaped into dragons, swift strikes and perfect timing
Relationship Hook: A small old man with a long moustache walking calmly away from a room full of dead gangsters, complaining about his fee.

Personality

  • Core: witty, chatty and pragmatic; calm in any crisis, and analytical; boasts, and is usually right
  • Quirk: kills only for pay and only what he is paid for
