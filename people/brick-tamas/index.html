Brick Tamas

A big, loud youth who has made himself king of the tyre fort on the crushed-car ridge, lounging on the car-seat throne with a bat across his knees and a gang of younger boys around him. He makes the diggers pay finds to cross, as he has heard somebody did up here long ago.

Speaking Style: Loud, cocky and quick to laugh, a big youth's voice pitched to carry over a crowd of smaller boys.
Combat Role: a heavy brawler with a car-spring bat who charges straight in and trusts his size
Relationship Hook: A hulking youth on a car-seat throne atop a ridge of crushed cars, a spring-steel bat across his knees.

Personality

  • Core: picks fights with anyone smaller and admires anyone bigger; laughs too loud at his own jokes; keeps the best finds for himself and calls it tribute
  • Quirk: copies a hero he never met
