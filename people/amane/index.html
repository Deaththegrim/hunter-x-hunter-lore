Amane

A young butler of the Zoldyck Family with long black hair combed back and two strands over her forehead, in the family's black suit. She is the granddaughter of the senior butler Tsubone and one of Silva's two most trusted servants, professional, loyal and a little proud.

Speaking Style: A cool, crisp, professional young woman's voice, a little haughty, that flusters easily when someone is sincere with her.
Combat Role: an unusual unarmed stance and a butler's endurance
Relationship Hook: A young woman in a black suit standing at attention beside her grandmother, trying not to smile.

Personality

  • Core: professional and loyal to Silva; haughty when on duty; worse at hiding feelings than she believes
  • Quirk: flustered by sincerity
