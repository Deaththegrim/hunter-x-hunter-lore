Darlene Voss

The night waitress at the truck stop's diner, a tired, pretty woman of thirty-eight with a pencil in her bleached hair, who has poured coffee for lorry drivers since she ran away from a husband in the city. She bakes the pies herself at four in the morning, and she is the reason the place is famous.

Speaking Style: Husky and kind, a night waitress who calls every driver honey and means it about half the time.
Combat Role: none; a pot of scalding coffee and the cook's shotgun behind the counter
Relationship Hook: A tired blonde waitress pouring coffee at a chrome counter, her eyes on the payphone by the door.

Personality

  • Core: calls every driver honey; bakes before dawn and never sleeps enough; flinches at a city accent she half recognises
  • Quirk: watches the payphone out of the corner of her eye
