Sal Duquesne

A heavy-lidded man in a grey waistcoat and shirt sleeves who keeps the book in the gambling hall's cage, a fat book at his elbow and a pistol in a drawer. He takes bets on anything, and in January he takes them on the Hunter Exam.

Speaking Style: A dry, unbothered voice that quotes odds the way other men talk about the weather.
Combat Role: a mobster with a pistol in the drawer and two men within shouting distance
Relationship Hook: A heavy-lidded bookmaker behind bars looking up from his book as someone asks the odds on a boy with a fishing rod.

Personality

  • Core: dry and unhurried; loyal to the family; kind to regular losers
  • Quirk: hates a bet he does not understand
