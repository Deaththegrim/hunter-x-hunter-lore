Pokkle

A young archer on his second try at the exam, applicant 53, in a red vest, white shirt and trousers and a purple cap, with a bow and a quiver of arrows. He is self-confident about his skills but cautious, knows at once when he is in real danger, and always has a plan in reserve. He is proud and dislikes coming in behind younger rookies.

Speaking Style: A self-assured young man's voice, a little proud and quick to point out his plan, which goes very careful when he is in danger.
Combat Role: a bow, paralysing arrows and the stealth to use them first
Relationship Hook: A young archer in a red vest drawing his bow from the shadow of a tree.

Personality

  • Core: self-confident and cautious; proud, and stung to be outshone; knows real danger when he sees it
  • Quirk: always holds a plan in reserve
