Tonpa

A short, fat man in a blue V-neck with a satchel, wearing badge number sixteen, who greets newcomers like an old friend, explains the exam to them and offers them a drink. He has taken the exam thirty-four times and calls himself an expert.

Speaking Style: A chummy, wheedling, helpful-sounding voice that offers advice and juice to newcomers and giggles when they fall.
Combat Role: none worth the name; tricks, poisoned drinks and false friendship
Relationship Hook: A short, friendly man holding out a can of juice to a boy who sniffs it and frowns.

Personality

  • Core: friendly, helpful and false; a coward who survives everything; brags about his experience
  • Quirk: enjoys newcomers' failure more than anything
