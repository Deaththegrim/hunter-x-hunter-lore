Jory Calder

A broad-shouldered young man from a farming town on the mainland who came all the way out to Whale Island because a rumour said the ship from here was the surest road to the exam, and then missed it by an hour. He has taken a room at Harbour Rest Guesthouse to wait for the next one and spends the days running laps of the harbour.

Speaking Style: Loud, sunny and stubborn, a young man's voice that turns every setback into a plan within one sentence.
Combat Role: a strong farm lad who wrestles and throws, tough and untrained
Relationship Hook: A grinning young man running laps of a harbour in the rain while the fishermen stare.

Personality

  • Core: trains every hour he is awake; wildly overestimates himself and works hard to close the gap; friendly to everyone on sight
  • Quirk: turns every setback into a plan
