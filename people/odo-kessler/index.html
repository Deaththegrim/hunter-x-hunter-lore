Odo Kessler

The weighmaster, a barrel-chested man with a book and a stub of chalk, who hangs every big catch on the gantry hook and calls its weight to the crowd. He settles every bet on the island that turns on a fish, and he takes the job of witness as seriously as a judge.

Speaking Style: Loud and official, a weighmaster's voice built for calling numbers across a windy square, softening only when he talks about fish.
Combat Role: none; a heavy iron counterweight and a voice that carries over a gale
Relationship Hook: A big weighmaster shouting a number while a huge fish swings from the hook of a rusty gantry.

Personality

  • Core: will not let a bet stand without witnesses; grows quiet and gentle beside a really big fish; cannot stand being doubted
  • Quirk: calls every weight as if it were news
