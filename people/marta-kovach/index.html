Marta Kovach

A widow from Dentora who sets up a folding table in the lay-by at the cliff bend on weekends and sells flowers to drivers who pull in to look at the view. Her buckets are full of mountain lilies and garden roses, and she always keeps one bunch back.

Speaking Style: Soft and steady, a flower seller's voice with a catch in it when she looks at the rail.
Combat Role: none; a folding table and a loud voice for passing cars
Relationship Hook: A widow in a shawl tying a bunch of white lilies to a buckled guard rail above a long drop.

Personality

  • Core: kind to every driver; keeps one bunch back; will not look at cars from the mountain
  • Quirk: remembers every accident on the road
