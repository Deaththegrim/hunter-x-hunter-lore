Soffman

A player with fair slicked-back hair in a t-shirt over a striped long-sleeved shirt, baggy trousers and black shoes. He keeps jugs at the spring in the dell, and he was among the veterans who turned up for two boys' first prize in Antokiba.

Speaking Style: Easygoing on the surface and evasive underneath, a man who says 'something like that' when cornered.
Combat Role: a modest player who stays out of fights and lets bigger men use him as a signpost
Relationship Hook: A slick-haired man by a mossy pool sniffing a jug and trying to guess what it has turned into.

Personality

  • Core: easygoing; goes where he is paid to go; likes his drink
  • Quirk: evasive when questioned
