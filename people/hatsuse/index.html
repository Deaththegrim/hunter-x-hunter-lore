Hatsuse

The keeper of the Zoldyck kennels, a lean, weathered woman in a rubber apron over her butler's suit, with the sleeves rolled back from forearms crossed with old scars. She cuts the meat, hoses the pens and walks the rule sheet every morning, and she has kept her post for longer than any keeper before her.

Speaking Style: Flat, dry and low, a woman who talks to people the way she talks to dogs: short sentences and no tone to read.
Combat Role: none she trusts against her charges; a butler's reflexes, a cleaver and a fast retreat
Relationship Hook: A woman in a rubber apron hosing out a pen while Mike, the size of a truck, watches her from the back of it.

Personality

  • Core: moves at exactly the same pace every day; dry about danger, careful about habit; has no patience for visitors who want to pet anything
  • Quirk: talks to the dogs under her breath
