Kacho Hui Guo Rou

The tenth prince of Kakin and elder twin of Fugetsu, daughter of the sixth queen Seiko, a slender girl with fair shoulder-length blonde hair, long lashes and black eyes, dressed more flashily than her twin. She is shallow, spoiled and detestable to her servants and chooses ugly bodyguards on purpose. She has proposed an alliance with her sister against the rest.

Speaking Style: Shrill, bratty and spoiled on the surface, with a steady clear voice under it when she drops the act.
Combat Role: none; a spoiled prince behind her guards
Relationship Hook: A pouting blonde prince scolding her bodyguard while her thumb taps out a message under the table.

Personality

  • Core: spoiled and rude; close to her twin; plans ahead
  • Quirk: picks ugly bodyguards on purpose
