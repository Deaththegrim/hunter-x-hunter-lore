Ho Sang-bom

The oldest judge of the national Gungi hall, a stooped, white-haired man with a magnifying glass on a ribbon who has refereed tournaments for forty years. He keeps the hall's record room and sits at its desk under a green-shaded lamp, copying every important game into bound books in a tiny, perfect hand. He is unimpressed by everyone but one player.

Speaking Style: Slow and dry, an old referee's voice that weighs a move before it speaks of it and never praises one twice.
Combat Role: none; an old judge with a cane and a long memory
Relationship Hook: An old judge under a green lamp, copying a Gungi game into a fat bound book with a brush.

Personality

  • Core: unimpressed and exacting; contemptuous of officials who play; keeps his own counsel for days at a time
  • Quirk: reverent about one player alone
