Sekiya

A butler of the Zoldyck Family on leave in the market town, who comes to the hot springs on his free days and sits in the outdoor pool for hours without moving. He is broad and heavily scarred, and the other bathers give him the far end of the pool without being asked.

Speaking Style: Sleepy and slow, a man who speaks from the bottom of a deep breath and rarely bothers.
Combat Role: a butler's strength and an aura he can shut off completely
Relationship Hook: A scarred man sitting perfectly still in a steaming pool while tourists edge away from him.

Personality

  • Core: silent about his work; polite to the bathhouse owner; dislikes being stared at
  • Quirk: still for hours
