Kang Mun-gil

The station master of the national terminus, a grey, upright man in a black railway uniform with gold braid and a watch chain he checks without looking. He has run the station for twenty years, and in that time no train has left late under his clock. He is proud of the marble, the portraits and the trains, roughly in that order.

Speaking Style: Fussy and precise, a railwayman who gives every time to the minute and sighs at anyone who rounds it.
Combat Role: none; a station master with a whistle and two soldiers at every gate
Relationship Hook: A grey station master under a giant clock, checking his watch against it with a frown.

Personality

  • Core: proud and exacting; lies smoothly about the trains and truthfully about the time; fond of his clerks in a stiff way
  • Quirk: terrified of the gap between his two timetables
