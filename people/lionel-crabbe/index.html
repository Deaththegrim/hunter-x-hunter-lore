Lionel Crabbe

A ringside judge in the grey blazer of the tower's officials, a fussy, balding man with a pencil moustache who has scored lower-floor bouts for fifteen years. He believes in the rulebook with a schoolmaster's faith and lifts his paddle the moment he judges a blow clean, whoever it makes unhappy.

Speaking Style: Clipped and prim, a judge who explains the rulebook in full sentences while the crowd boos him.
Combat Role: none; a scoring paddle and a whistle that stops the bout
Relationship Hook: A prim balding judge in a grey blazer raising a white paddle while the crowd behind him rises to its feet in fury.

Personality

  • Core: will not be hurried; brave in small ways; polishes his paddle when nervous
  • Quirk: prim and proper
