Om Pun-i

The keeper of the road inn between Taba City and Luonton City, a handsome, sharp-eyed widow in an apron who ladles noodles for lorry drivers, rents bunks by the night and keeps the registration book on the counter. She knows every driver on the highway by his lorry, his load and his wife's name.

Speaking Style: Husky, teasing and quick, an innkeeper who flatters drivers and remembers everything they say over noodles.
Combat Role: none; a ladle, a cleaver and a room full of drivers who like her
Relationship Hook: A sharp-eyed innkeeper ladling noodles while reading the registration book upside down on the counter.

Personality

  • Core: sharp, teasing and observant; sells information to anyone who pays; curious to a fault
  • Quirk: kind to drivers down on their luck
