Pernille Saxe

A small woman of twenty-eight with cropped bleached hair, a hooded top and three telephones on her desk in the newspaper's clippings library, where she works nights as the paper's researcher. The reporters bring her names and she brings them back lives.

Speaking Style: A quick, teasing voice that types as it talks and laughs at its own cleverness.
Combat Role: fights at a keyboard first and in person only behind a locked door and a stun baton
Relationship Hook: A bleached-haired researcher spinning in her chair among steel drawers and asking what you will trade for a name.

Personality

  • Core: quick and teasing; nocturnal; trades secrets like cards
  • Quirk: protective of the newsroom's juniors
