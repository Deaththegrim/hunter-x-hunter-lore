Satotz

The first examiner of the 287th exam, a Hunter of ruins with pale lavender hair curling at the ends, a thin curled moustache and no visible mouth, in a dark coat and red tie. He leads the applicants on an endless walk through the tunnel under Zaban City at a pace that forces them to run, then out across the Milsy Wetlands. He is calm, courteous and silent most of the way.

Speaking Style: A polite, quiet, precise gentleman's voice that gives short instructions and never sounds out of breath.
Combat Role: speed, keen senses and a calm that catches thrown cards out of the air
Relationship Hook: A tall, mouthless gentleman walking calmly ahead of four hundred panting runners in a dark tunnel.

Personality

  • Core: calm, quiet and courteous; takes his duty as examiner seriously; quietly encourages the ones he likes
  • Quirk: strongly admires Ging
