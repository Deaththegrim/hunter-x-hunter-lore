Owen Pryce

The overman of the night shift at the coal mine, a short, deep-chested man in his fifties with blue scars of coal dust in the skin of his hands and a lamp on his helmet. He hands out the lamps, counts the men down and counts them back up.

Speaking Style: Lilting and grave, a mine overman's voice that sings a little even when it is giving bad news.
Combat Role: none; a pick handle and a body that has worked in the dark for forty years
Relationship Hook: A barrel-chested mine overman under a lamp-room light, handing out lamps to a queue of miners at shift change.

Personality

  • Core: responsible for every man; torn between the pit and the truth; sings under his breath
  • Quirk: counts twice
