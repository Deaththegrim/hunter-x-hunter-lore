Lammert

The ferryman at Mossford, a barrel-chested man who hauls the flat raft across the river on its rope with his son, and charges strangers in news instead of coin. He knows every face that has crossed in thirty years.

Speaking Style: A deep, rolling river-man's voice, slow and amused, that takes the fare in gossip.
Combat Role: none; a strong ferryman with a pole
Relationship Hook: A barrel-chested ferryman hauling the raft across on its rope, asking what you have heard on the road.

Personality

  • Core: genial and gossipy; strong as an ox; worried for his son
  • Quirk: knows everyone's business
