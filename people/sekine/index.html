Sekine

A junior butler who sits in the booth by the Testing Gate on the days Zebro is at his cabin, answering the wall telephone and the questions at the window. She is small and neat in the black suit, with a notebook always open, and the tourists who expect a monster at the gate get a polite young woman instead.

Speaking Style: Bright, practised and patient on the telephone; she sounds exactly the same to a lost tourist and to a man asking which way the hound runs.
Combat Role: a butler's training kept in reserve; a short baton and the telephone to the mountain
Relationship Hook: A young butler in the gate booth with the receiver at her ear, writing down a stranger's every word.

Personality

  • Core: polite to everyone through the window; laughs at the tourists' questions only after the bus has gone; afraid of the gate's silence at night
  • Quirk: writes down every word a caller says
