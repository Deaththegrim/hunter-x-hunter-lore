Henrik Strand

The captain of the watch that keeps the road, a hard, quiet man who has written in the book of arrivals longer than any watcher alive. He reads every name in it once to each new watcher, so that nobody who came down the road is ever forgotten.

Speaking Style: Hard, low and controlled, orders delivered as flat facts behind the mask.
Combat Role: a disciplined watch captain who holds the road with a shotgun and a line of masked neighbours, and fights to the last on it
Relationship Hook: A tall masked captain with a shotgun across his back, writing a name into a thick book by the glow of a radio.

Personality

  • Core: never takes off his mask before an outsider; speaks only in orders on duty; keeps the radio on even while he sleeps
  • Quirk: reads the arrivals book aloud to new watchers
