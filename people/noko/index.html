Noko

The only other child on Whale Island besides Gon, a little girl with green eyes and orange hair in two braids, in a pink long-sleeved shirt. She is at the front of the crowd when Gon brings in the great fish from the swamp and at the dock to wave him off.

Speaking Style: A small girl's shy, happy voice.
Combat Role: none; a village child
Relationship Hook: A little girl with orange braids waving from the end of a pier as a ship leaves.

Personality

  • Core: shy and smiling; Gon's friend; always at the front of the crowd
  • Quirk: waves until the ship is gone
