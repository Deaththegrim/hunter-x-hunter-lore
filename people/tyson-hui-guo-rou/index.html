Tyson Hui Guo Rou

The sixth prince of Kakin and only daughter of the fourth queen Katrono, a short, stout woman with a large round face, long straight black hair and dark upturned eyes, in fuzzy, flowery clothes with hearts on them. She picked her bodyguards for their looks, refuses to let the Hunter Association vet them and loves to be pampered and called cute. She has written a book of her teachings on love.

Speaking Style: Giggly, sweet and dreamy, a voice that preaches love and blushes at a compliment.
Combat Role: none; a prince who trusts love and her guards
Relationship Hook: A round-faced prince in a flowery dress pressing her book into a bodyguard's hands with a giggle.

Personality

  • Core: sweet and dreamy; possessive of her guards; opposes violence
  • Quirk: loves to be pampered
