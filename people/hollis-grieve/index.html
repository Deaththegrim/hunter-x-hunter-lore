Hollis Grieve

The chief of the tower's guards, a square-jawed former army sergeant with a grey crew cut and a radio on each hip. He runs forty men in grey uniforms, throws out drunks, confiscates the weapons people try to carry below the 200th floor and watches the whole building on a wall of small screens.

Speaking Style: Short sentences, no wasted words, a former soldier who gives orders in the same tone he orders coffee.
Combat Role: an ex-soldier's baton, cuffs and a takedown, with forty guards behind the radio
Relationship Hook: A grey-haired security chief with his arms folded before a wall of small screens, one of them showing a strange haze.

Personality

  • Core: disciplined and blunt; protective of his men; obeys orders he dislikes
  • Quirk: taps the screen when something is wrong
