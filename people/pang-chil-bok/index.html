Pang Chil-bok

The skipper of boat forty-one of the harbour's numbered fleet, a short, barrel-chested man with arms like hawsers, a wool cap and a face burned dark by the wind. He brings in his catch, watches it weighed and taken under a soldier's eye, and goes home with the share the state allows. His crew would follow him into a typhoon.

Speaking Style: Rough, salt-cracked and blunt, a skipper who swears at the sea and apologises to it after.
Combat Role: a gaff hook and a crew of fishermen at his back
Relationship Hook: A barrel-chested skipper on a stone quay, avoiding the eyes of a woman waiting by the scales.

Personality

  • Core: blunt and loyal; protects his crew before himself; trusts the sea more than the state
  • Quirk: lies badly to women and well to soldiers
