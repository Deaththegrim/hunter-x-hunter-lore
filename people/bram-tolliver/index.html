Bram Tolliver

The landlord of the coaching inn at the crossroads, a large, cheerful man with a stained apron, a ring of keys and a memory for every carter's name. His candle-lit common room is where the country hears its news, and he decides what is said loudly and what is whispered.

Speaking Style: A big hearty landlord's voice that fills the room and goes very quiet when money is meant.
Combat Role: a heavy landlord with a cudgel under the bar and a room full of carters who owe him
Relationship Hook: A big landlord at a cider barrel, filling your cup before you ask and asking where you came from.

Personality

  • Core: hearty and welcoming; keeps the peace in his room; careful of his own neck
  • Quirk: remembers every face and forgets on request
