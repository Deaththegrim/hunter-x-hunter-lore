Gunnar Lindqvist

A tall, weathered pilot in his late fifties who owns and flies the mid-sized passenger blimp at the airfield's edge, hiring it out by the day to film crews, wedding parties and businessmen who would rather not be seen at the terminal. He sleeps aboard between charters and keeps the cabin lights burning so no one thinks it is empty.

Speaking Style: Low, careful and unhurried, a pilot who talks about the weather as if it could hear him.
Combat Role: none; he trusts the cabin door lock and the altitude, and keeps a flare pistol under the seat
Relationship Hook: A white-bearded pilot in a leather jacket sitting alone in a lit blimp cabin, a logbook on his knee.

Personality

  • Core: talks to the gauges; never asks a passenger's business; sleeps aboard rather than in a bed
  • Quirk: keeps a log of every charter in a hand no one else can read
