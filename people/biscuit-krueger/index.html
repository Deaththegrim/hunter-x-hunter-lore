Biscuit Krueger

A two-star Hunter of precious stones and a master of Shingen-ryu who looks like a frilly little girl with blonde hair in neat twin tails, a dress and gloves, and asks to be called Bisky. She is devious, uses her doll-like look to fool people, and lies as easily as she breathes. She has come to Battera's selection to get into Greed Island and find a gem that exists only there.

Speaking Style: A sweet, girlish, sing-song voice that flatters and pouts and then drops an octave into an old master's dry, cutting judgement.
Combat Role: Shingen-ryu mastery, enormous strength in a tiny frame, and a true body that ends fights
Relationship Hook: A tiny frilly blonde girl in a queue of hard-bitten Hunters, smiling sweetly and lying through her teeth.

Personality

  • Core: an exacting, generous teacher once she cares; obsessed with beautiful gems; hates her true form
  • Quirk: devious, cutesy and a consummate liar
