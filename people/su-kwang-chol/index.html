Su Kwang-chol

A senior radar operator at the hilltop station, a heavy-lidded lieutenant in headphones who has watched the sky over the Balsa Islands on green screens for twelve years. He logs every aircraft that crosses the coast, and is said to be able to tell a fighter from a cargo plane by the way its blip moves.

Speaking Style: Dry and deep, an operator who makes jokes in a monotone so nobody can prove they were jokes.
Combat Role: a sidearm and a station of soldiers; he would sooner phone for a fighter
Relationship Hook: A heavy-lidded operator in headphones tapping a pencil against a green radar screen.

Personality

  • Core: dry and watchful; exact on the screen, careless in the canteen; bored enough to be dangerous
  • Quirk: keeps copies of what he is told to wipe
