Qian Mingyuan

The ship's senior navigating officer, a tall, spare man in Royal Army whites with a grey-flecked beard and a pair of brass dividers always in his breast pocket. He plots the voyage's course day by day in the chart room behind the helm and has sailed the empire's coasts for twenty-five years.

Speaking Style: Calm, deep and unhurried, a navigator's voice that reads out bearings like scripture.
Combat Role: a naval officer with a sidearm who would rather turn the ship than fight on it
Relationship Hook: A grey-bearded navigator in whites bent over a chart table, a sealed chart beside his hand.

Personality

  • Core: reads bearings aloud even alone; trusts the sea more than the palace; has never lost a ship
  • Quirk: keeps his dividers in his breast pocket
