Harriet Coyle

The schoolteacher of the settlement school, a plain, sturdy woman of fifty who came to teach for a year and stayed twenty. Most of the runway families' children learned their letters at her desks, and their mothers confide in her as in nobody else.

Speaking Style: Soft, patient and plain-spoken, a teacher who never raises her voice and never needs to.
Combat Role: none; she stands between a child and the danger and does not move
Relationship Hook: A sturdy teacher at a blackboard looking at an empty desk, chalk still in her hand.

Personality

  • Core: never raises her voice; remembers every pupil she ever taught; brings bread for the children who come hungry
  • Quirk: stubborn as a mule when a child is in trouble
