Rokka

A pupil at the butlers' school, eleven years old and small for it, in a child-sized black suit with the knees worn through. He came to the estate from an orphanage in Parasta two winters ago and has not yet budged the first door of the Testing Gate, which every pupil his age is expected to open.

Speaking Style: A boy's voice that runs fast and high when he is excited and drops to a mumble the moment an adult in a suit looks his way.
Combat Role: a child's scrappy grappling and the first lessons in silent footwork
Relationship Hook: A small boy in a worn black suit throwing his whole weight against an iron sled that refuses to move.

Personality

  • Core: eager to the point of foolishness; hero-worships Canary from a distance; talks to himself during lifts
  • Quirk: hides injuries rather than lose a day of training
