Jeitsari

A Hunter of middle height with a full fair beard, in a collared jacket, who keeps a stool at the bar under the castle and listens to the village talk. He is known as a collector who once owned several copies of the game the billionaire on the hill is buying up, and who has played it himself.

Speaking Style: Dry and unhurried, a collector's voice that seems to put a value on everything it mentions.
Combat Role: a seasoned Hunter who would rather settle by contract than by fists, with Nen behind him if it comes to it
Relationship Hook: A bearded man at a village bar turning an empty game case over in his fingers.

Personality

  • Core: patient as a man waiting for a contract to mature; keeps his own counsel; makes one glass last an evening
  • Quirk: puts a value on what he looks at
