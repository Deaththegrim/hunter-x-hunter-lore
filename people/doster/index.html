Doster

A tall, muscular Hunter with dark green hair under a thick gold headband, two red stripes beneath his eyes and three red marks on his forehead, in a sleeveless dark red and yellow vest. He passed the billionaire's aura test in the same batch as the two boys.

Speaking Style: Firm and spare, a fighter's voice that keeps its reports short.
Combat Role: a Hunter's trained Nen behind a big frame, straightforward and hard
Relationship Hook: A muscular man in a gold headband lowering a pair of binoculars and pointing down the road.

Personality

  • Core: disciplined; respects strength; keeps his own counsel
  • Quirk: spare with words
