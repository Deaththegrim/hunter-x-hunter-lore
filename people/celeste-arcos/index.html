Celeste Arcos

A licensed Hunter in a long coat who has parked at the road end with a case of documents and asked, very politely, to speak to the Assembly. She has been told to wait, and she waits well.

Speaking Style: Crisp and courteous, every word in its place, the politeness of someone who expects to win.
Combat Role: a Blacklist Hunter who studies a target for days and then ends it fast with aura drawn into wire
Relationship Hook: A tall woman in a trench coat waiting by her car at the road end, a document case in her gloved hand.

Personality

  • Core: files everything in labelled folders; assumes every silence is hiding something; never eats what she is offered
  • Quirk: polite to the point of menace
