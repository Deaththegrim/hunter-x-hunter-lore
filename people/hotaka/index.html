Hotaka

The summit lookout, a wiry, sun-browned butler who spends most of her days on the crater rim with binoculars and a radio. She reports the weather for the estate's airship and anything that moves on the slopes below, and she is happier in the cold than anyone else on the mountain.

Speaking Style: Calm and unhurried, warmed by long hours alone in the wind; she talks about the weather the way other people talk about friends.
Combat Role: a mountaineer's balance and a butler's Nen; she uses height and wind before she uses her hands
Relationship Hook: A butler in a padded jacket on a crater rim above the clouds, binoculars raised, a hawk on the rock beside her.

Personality

  • Core: at ease alone; names the clouds; reports exactly what she sees and no more
  • Quirk: feeds a wild hawk against the rules
