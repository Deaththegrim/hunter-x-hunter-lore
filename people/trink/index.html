Trink

A lean capo in his fifties with slicked-back black hair going grey at the temples, a thin moustache and tinted glasses, who keeps the same red booth at Club Velours every August. He is an old friend of Light Nostrade and one of the new names in his daughter's appointment book.

Speaking Style: A low, dry rasp that drops to a whisper whenever a waiter comes near.
Combat Role: a capo who fights through other men, a word in an ear and a car at the kerb
Relationship Hook: A lean capo in tinted glasses watching the door from a red velvet booth, a whisky untouched at his hand.

Personality

  • Core: dry and careful; trusts fortunes more than men; keeps old friends close
  • Quirk: never sits with his back to a door
