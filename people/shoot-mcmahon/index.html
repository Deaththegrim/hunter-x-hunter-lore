Shoot McMahon

A Hunter of unidentified beasts and Morel's other pupil, Knuckle's partner, a tall, thin, pale man with no eyebrows, tired black eyes and pink hair slicked back with a bandaged ponytail on one side. He wears bandages under a purple kimono with one great sleeve. He is timid, distrusts his own strength and hates to hurt anyone, and his caution balances Knuckle's recklessness.

Speaking Style: A soft, hesitant, tired voice that doubts itself aloud and goes steady once the fight has begun.
Combat Role: three floating hands that strike from every side, and a birdcage that takes what they hit
Relationship Hook: A pale, tired-looking man with one great sleeve watching three disembodied hands circle his opponent.

Personality

  • Core: dislikes hurting anyone; cautious where Knuckle is rash; steadier than he believes
  • Quirk: timid and self-doubting
