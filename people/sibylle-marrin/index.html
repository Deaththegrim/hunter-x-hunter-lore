Sibylle Marrin

A small, freckled woman in a fisherman's smock and rubber boots who lodges with a reed-cutting family on the boardwalk and goes out each morning in a tarred reed boat with sample cases. The village children call her the flower lady.

Speaking Style: Soft, earnest and precise, with a botanist's pleasure in a long scientific name.
Combat Role: a fieldworker who fights with what grows around her: a machete, a paralysing sap, a quick retreat by boat
Relationship Hook: A freckled botanist on a foggy boardwalk holding up a pressed flower and warning you not to smell it.

Personality

  • Core: earnest and precise; gentle with children; keeps the village's rules
  • Quirk: careless of her own safety for a sample
