Ging and Mito's Grandmother

Gon's great-grandmother, an old woman with grey hair in a neat bun who lives with Mito and Gon on Whale Island. She is cheerful and honest, smiles through narrow eyes, and enjoys nothing more than telling Gon embarrassing tales of Mito's childhood and of the wild boy Ging used to be.

Speaking Style: A slow, amused old woman's voice that tells stories nobody asked for and teases her granddaughter without mercy.
Combat Role: none; age, patience and a long memory
Relationship Hook: An old woman by the stove laughing at a story about a boy who climbed every tree on the island.

Personality

  • Core: cordial, honest and fond of a joke; remembers everything about the family's children; unworried by things that frighten younger people
  • Quirk: teases Mito about Ging at every chance
