Palm Siberia

A Hunter and Knov's pupil, an extremely thin young woman with long, unruly black hair, pale sickly skin and a light pink blouse, who carries a kitchen knife and frightens passers-by. She talks too much, drifts off the subject and has a volatile temper that scares even Killua, and she worships Knov.

Speaking Style: A breathy, rambling, intense young woman's voice that veers off the subject, gushes about one man and turns terrifyingly cold in an instant.
Combat Role: little in open combat; a knife, a temper, and eyes that find anyone
Relationship Hook: A thin, wild-haired woman with a kitchen knife gushing about her master to two boys who are edging away.

Personality

  • Core: worships Knov; terrifying when crossed; a convincing actress when needed
  • Quirk: erratic, talkative and intense
