Dario Fenn

A young Blacklist Hunter camped alone in the overgrown clearing where the great raid on the mountain set out three years ago. He is handsome, restless and well equipped, with his licence on a chain round his neck and a map of the wall pinned to a board by the old fire ring.

Speaking Style: Warm, quick and persuasive, a young Hunter who argues as if he has already won and grins when he loses.
Combat Role: a pro Hunter's Nen, a pair of short batons and a willingness to run when the odds turn
Relationship Hook: A young Hunter crouching by an old fire ring, studying a pinned map of a wall that runs off the edge of the paper.

Personality

  • Core: charming and impatient; plans everything and trusts nothing he cannot see; refuses to hate the people who did it until he knows who they are
  • Quirk: talks about his brother in the present tense
