Aldo Brisk

The island's forest warden, an outdoors man with a rifle he hates to use and a pocketful of red ribbon he ties on trees to warn people off the denning ground. He walks the forest every day and sits on the mossed trunk in the glade to eat his lunch.

Speaking Style: Hearty and outdoorsy, a forest warden's booming voice that drops to a whisper the moment he steps under the trees.
Combat Role: a forest warden with a hunting rifle he fires over animals' heads, and a heavy knife
Relationship Hook: A bearded warden on a mossy trunk eating a sandwich, a red ribbon fluttering on the beech beside him.

Personality

  • Core: loud in town, silent in the forest; hates his rifle and keeps it clean; eats lunch in the same spot every day
  • Quirk: ties red ribbons on every dangerous tree
