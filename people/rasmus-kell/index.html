Rasmus Kell

The captain of the exam airship, a weathered man with a grey beard and a leather flight jacket, who flies the applicants between the far sites and flies the examiners in and out when no one is watching.

Speaking Style: Unruffled and practical, an airman's voice that talks about wind as other men talk about weather.
Combat Role: none of note; a pilot who keeps the ship level and the cabin doors locked
Relationship Hook: A grey-bearded airship captain in a leather jacket on the lift of a mooring mast, an airship's gondola behind him.

Personality

  • Core: unflappable; proud of his ship; fond of a quiet night flight
  • Quirk: tight-lipped about routes
