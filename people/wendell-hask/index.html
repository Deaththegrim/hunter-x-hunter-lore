Wendell Hask

The desk sergeant at the Skyfoot Police Station, a heavy, slow man with a grey moustache and a newspaper always open on the desk. He takes complaints about pickpockets, drunks and fights that start outside the tower without a referee, and files most of them in a drawer.

Speaking Style: Weary and patient, a desk sergeant who says 'mm-hm' while he reads the paper.
Combat Role: an old policeman's truncheon and a revolver he has not fired in a decade
Relationship Hook: A heavy desk sergeant behind a high counter with a newspaper open, looking up at a young woman holding a photograph.

Personality

  • Core: slow and tired; kind underneath; afraid of trouble
  • Quirk: reads the fight results before the news
