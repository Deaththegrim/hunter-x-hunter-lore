Corwin Strand

The harbourmaster, a lean, neat man in a peaked cap with a pencil moustache, who logs every boat in and out, sells the passage to the mainland and talks the fleet home on the radio when the weather turns. He has rung the storm bell by the door more times than he cares to count, and he has never once rung it late.

Speaking Style: Calm and practical, a radio man's measured voice that repeats every number twice.
Combat Role: none; a flare pistol in the drawer and the storm bell
Relationship Hook: A harbourmaster in a peaked cap holding a radio handset, eyes on a falling barometer.

Personality

  • Core: logs the weather even on his days off; keeps promises to the Captain he would rather not have made; talks boats home in a voice that never rises
  • Quirk: repeats every figure twice to be sure
