Nobunaga Hazama

Member number one of the Phantom Troupe, a swordsman in a Jappon-style robe with his hair tied up in a topknot and his sword never out of reach. He is calm until crossed and quick to quarrel with the others, and his blade is fast enough that a word from Killua compares him to Hisoka. Uvogin is his closest friend.

Speaking Style: A gruff, sardonic swordsman's voice, short and cutting, that loses its temper with comrades and goes hushed and sincere for a fallen friend.
Combat Role: a sword drawn and swung inside a short sensing circle, faster than an eye
Relationship Hook: A swordsman in a loose robe crouched with his hand on his hilt, perfectly still, waiting for someone to step closer.

Personality

  • Core: sullen and calm until provoked; quarrels with his comrades and fights for them; respects courage even in an enemy
  • Quirk: grieves his friends openly
