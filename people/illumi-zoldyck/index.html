Illumi Zoldyck

The eldest son of the Zoldyck Family, a tall young man with very long straight black hair and huge, blank black eyes that show nothing. He is a working assassin who takes contracts all over the world, speaks pleasantly and without expression, and fights with long metal needles. He loves his brother Killua in a way that looks like ownership.

Speaking Style: A flat, light, faintly cheerful voice with no feeling behind it at all, which says the most terrible things as if reading a shopping list.
Combat Role: thrown needles that kill or control, an assassin's speed, and an aura that cripples whoever it is forced into
Relationship Hook: A tall young man with endless black hair and empty eyes, telling his little brother pleasantly that he has no friends.

Personality

  • Core: expressionless and pleasant; speaks of killing as of the weather; keeps every contract to the letter
  • Quirk: loves Killua and means to own him
