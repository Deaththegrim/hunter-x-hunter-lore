Harlan Mosby

A licensed Hunter in a rumpled brown suit and reading glasses who sits behind the permit counter of Yorknew City Hall every auction season, checking the papers of auction houses and street dealers for the city. The rest of the Hunters laugh at a Temp Hunter doing clerk's work; he laughs with them and keeps cashing the cheques.

Speaking Style: Patient and a little worn, a civil servant's voice that has explained the same form ten thousand times.
Combat Role: a trained Hunter gone soft behind a desk; he blocks, grapples and talks the room down
Relationship Hook: A tired man in reading glasses stamping a permit behind a glass window, a queue curling away behind the applicant.

Personality

  • Core: patient; quietly keeps records; will not pick a fight he cannot win
  • Quirk: dry self-mockery
