Nugui

The king's personal butler, a very small elderly man with well-combed hair, a square jaw, a bulbous nose and long earlobes, in a black suit and laced tie. He is calm, smiling and professional at all times, and he carries the king's word to the princes.

Speaking Style: Small, polite and unhurried, a butler's soft voice that delivers terrible news as if it were the weather.
Combat Role: none; a butler who carries the king's word
Relationship Hook: A tiny old butler setting a lacquered box before a prince and explaining the rules with a smile.

Personality

  • Core: calm and professional; loyal to the king; privately sorrowful for the children
  • Quirk: always politely smiling
