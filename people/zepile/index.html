Zepile

An antiques trader with short light-brown hair, long sideburns and a pair of thick, wavy eyebrows that do half his talking. He dresses loud, a red blazer trimmed in black over a white shirt with its big collar thrown open, and he haunts the Bull Market and the Preview Market with a loupe in his breast pocket. He will tell a stranger what a thing is worth for the pleasure of being right.

Speaking Style: A dealer's patter, warm and quick, that slows to a lecturer's care the moment a glaze or a grain of wood comes up.
Combat Role: no fighter at all; he talks, bargains and ducks, and wins a room by knowing more than it does
Relationship Hook: A man with enormous eyebrows holding a vase up to the light at a market stall, a loupe screwed into one eye.

Personality

  • Core: takes a cut and calls it fair; ashamed of his old forgeries; panics when money runs short
  • Quirk: shows off what he knows
