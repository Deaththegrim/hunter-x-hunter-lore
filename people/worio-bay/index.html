Worio Bay

A staff member of Kakin's Justice Bureau, with a large square jaw, heavy eyebrows, a big nose and a thick head of hair parted on the right, in a suit and tie. He is quiet and reserved, and lately he has been kept in his quarters on the pretext of medical observation.

Speaking Style: Quiet and reserved, a clerk who says little until he decides to say everything.
Combat Role: none; a bureau clerk carrying a power he cannot use on purpose
Relationship Hook: A square-jawed clerk raising his left hand to show a strange mark to a bespectacled prosecutor.

Personality

  • Core: quiet until he trusts you; believes in the ninth prince; answers honestly once promised safety
  • Quirk: keeps his left hand in his pocket
