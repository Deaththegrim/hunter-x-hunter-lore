Amadeo Ricci

The bonesetter of the back streets, a small, bald old man with enormous hands and a white coat gone grey with washing. He treats fighters who cannot afford the tower's clinic or do not want their injuries on file, works fast and talks the whole time, mostly about his late wife's cooking.

Speaking Style: Chatty, warm and never-ending, an old bonesetter who talks through every bone he sets.
Combat Role: none; an old man with hands strong enough to put a shoulder back in, or out
Relationship Hook: A small bald old man with huge hands setting a fighter's arm on a padded table, talking the whole time.

Personality

  • Core: cures cheap and asks nothing; curious to a fault; hums while he pulls a joint
  • Quirk: talkative and gentle
