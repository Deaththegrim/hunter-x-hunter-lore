Ash Corin

A grey, slow-moving man who pushes a handcart along the rutted road to the scorched hollow and burns there what the elders will not allow into the ground: plague bedding, poisoned stock, and now and then a body. When he has nothing to burn he sweeps the ash bed into lines.

Speaking Style: Unbothered and low, the even voice of a man who has stood beside a great many fires and none of them has hurried him.
Combat Role: a cart-pusher who fights with a long fire rake and the heat of the pyre at his back
Relationship Hook: A gaunt soot-masked man pushing a handcart down a rutted road toward a hollow of grey ash.

Personality

  • Core: sweeps the ash into patterns; talks to the fire as if it were a guest; eats his bread cold rather than warm it over the pyre
  • Quirk: never asks whose the body was
