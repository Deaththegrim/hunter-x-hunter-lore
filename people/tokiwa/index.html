Tokiwa

A young butler on the boundary wall rota, walking the patrol path along the top with a radio and a torch. He is loose-limbed and talkative, and he treats the long walks as a chance to be away from the senior staff.

Speaking Style: Cocky and easy, a young man's chatty voice that drops into a careful whisper the moment he is near the wall's edge.
Combat Role: a junior butler's speed and a heavy torch; he reports first and fights second
Relationship Hook: A lanky young butler on top of a giant stone wall, shining his torch down at a grappling hook on the outer side.

Personality

  • Core: talks to himself on long patrols; pretends to be braver than he is; obeys the hound's rule to the letter
  • Quirk: keeps a tally of climbers on the inside of his cap
