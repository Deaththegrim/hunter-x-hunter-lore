Cluck

The Zodiacs' Chicken, a slim woman with dark blue hair in a feathered outfit of blue and white, with little wings on her back. She hunts plants, and she is also a dancer and musician. She is short-tempered and blunt and has no patience for Pariston.

Speaking Style: A sharp, blunt woman's voice that loses patience fast and ends arguments by shouting at people to shut up.
Combat Role: birds under her command and abilities she keeps to the council
Relationship Hook: A woman in a blue feathered costume standing up to shout at the whole council table.

Personality

  • Core: short-tempered and blunt; has no patience with schemers; a performer at heart
  • Quirk: says what she thinks loudly
