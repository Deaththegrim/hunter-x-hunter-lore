Kosofftro

A man with two big buck teeth over his lip, in a broad-brimmed hat and matching shirt and trousers printed with arrows, his arms and legs wrapped in bandages and a sword at his side. He sits in the woods near the alliance's hideout all day, and he asks every newcomer to remember his name.

Speaking Style: Kind and patient, a host's voice that repeats its own name so nobody forgets it.
Combat Role: a sword he carries more than he uses; he is a meeting point, not a fighter
Relationship Hook: A buck-toothed man in an arrow-print hat raising a hand as three strangers pop into the woods beside him.

Personality

  • Core: cordial; patient beyond reason; content with a dull job
  • Quirk: insists on being remembered
