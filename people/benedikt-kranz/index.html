Benedikt Kranz

The innkeeper of the Mountain View Inn, a stout, cheerful man with a waxed moustache who serves goat cheese, mushrooms from the woods and venison he does not ask about. Every room has a view of Kukuroo Mountain, and he charges for it.

Speaking Style: Warm, round and hospitable, an innkeeper's voice that welcomes every guest as an old friend.
Combat Role: none; a stout innkeeper's shove and a cellar door
Relationship Hook: A stout innkeeper with a waxed moustache gesturing proudly at a window full of mountain.

Personality

  • Core: welcoming to everyone; proud of his view; never asks about the meat
  • Quirk: keeps a second register
