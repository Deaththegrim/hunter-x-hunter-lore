Tsukuma

A former police officer of twenty-nine with a gaunt, hard-jawed face and a muscular frame wasting under a hospital gown. Bandages cover the top of his head, his right eye and ear, and a pad is taped over one cheekbone. He has no legs below the blanket. He talks to the television crews calmly and without self-pity.

Speaking Style: Flat and steady, a policeman giving a statement from a hospital bed in short sentences, as if describing a road accident he only happened to see.
Combat Role: none now; once a patrolman's sidearm and baton, and a policeman's eye for the detail that matters
Relationship Hook: A bandaged ex-policeman sitting up in a hospital bed, telling a camera crew about a giant shrimp in a level voice.

Personality

  • Core: precise about what he saw and what he did not; refuses pity; still thinks like a policeman
  • Quirk: calm under any question
