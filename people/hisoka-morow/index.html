Hisoka Morow

A tall, muscular man dressed like a jester, with a star painted on one cheek and a teardrop on the other, who throws playing cards that cut like razors. He fights for the pleasure of fighting strong people and kills those who bore him, and he spares the promising ones, especially children, to let them ripen into worthy opponents. Last year he was thrown out of the exam for nearly killing an examiner. He is the fourth member of the Phantom Troupe, and he enjoys everything.

Speaking Style: A silky, lilting, amused voice that purrs and sing-songs, savouring words like food; it never hurries and it always sounds pleased.
Combat Role: razor playing cards, impossible flexibility, and aura that sticks and stretches like gum
Relationship Hook: A painted jester smiling at a boy with a fishing rod, a playing card turning slowly between his fingers.

Personality

  • Core: playful, theatrical and utterly self-serving; spares the promising and kills the dull; keeps his word only when it amuses him
  • Quirk: calls strong young fighters his unripe fruit
