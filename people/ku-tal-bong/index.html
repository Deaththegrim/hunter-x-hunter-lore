Ku Tal-bong

A moneychanger at the corner of the black market behind the bus station, a lean man in a quilted coat with a fat wallet inside it and a lookout boy at each end of the alley. He changes any money for any other at dusk and is gone before full dark. He never writes anything down, and he has never been robbed.

Speaking Style: Soft, deep and unhurried, a moneychanger who says a rate once and never repeats it.
Combat Role: a short blade in his sleeve and two boys who can whistle up help
Relationship Hook: A lean man in a quilted coat at the corner of a lamplit alley, one hand inside his coat, counting by touch.

Personality

  • Core: calm and calculating; trusts no one but his lookouts; fair to the poor, merciless to the greedy
  • Quirk: never says a number twice
