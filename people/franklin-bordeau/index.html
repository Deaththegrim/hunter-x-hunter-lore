Franklin Bordeau

Member number seven of the Phantom Troupe, a scarred giant who looks like a stitched-together monster, with a sewn mouth, long ear lobes hung with gold rings and blue overalls. He kills without hesitation and enjoys a fight, but he is also the Troupe's level head, the one who reminds the others what the Spider needs. He often works with Shizuku.

Speaking Style: A huge, slow, deep voice, calm and reasonable, that settles arguments between the others and laughs low in a fight.
Combat Role: ten fingers that fire streams of aura bullets, and a giant's durability
Relationship Hook: A scarred giant raising his hands, fingertips missing, as a crowd of gunmen turns to run.

Personality

  • Core: calm, analytical and reasonable; enjoys combat; puts the Spider before any member
  • Quirk: settles quarrels inside the Troupe
