Longhi

A royal guard of the fifth prince Tubeppa, a woman in her twenties with fair hair tied back in a long ponytail and gaunt cheeks, in the black suit and tie of the royal bodyguard. She came up through the Royal Military Academy and was always near the top of her class. She is careful and less vocal than her superior Maor, and prefers a written letter to any signal that could be intercepted.

Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and precise, a soldier who chooses each word as if it will be read back to her.
Combat Role: a trained royal guard who would rather bind an enemy with an agreement than draw on him
Relationship Hook: A gaunt guard with a long fair ponytail sealing a letter in a corridor of the royal tier.

Personality

  • Core: careful and discreet; quiet beside her superior; a cold anger she never names
  • Quirk: gives nothing away
