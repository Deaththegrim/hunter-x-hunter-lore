Dalzollene

The head of Neon Nostrade's bodyguards and Light's right hand, a well-built man with a short haircut and tattoos under both eyes reaching to the cheekbones, usually in a sweater and slacks, a slim engraved sabre at his side. He is calm, competent and fond of music, and he runs the guard detail like a business: the job is the girl, and the girl comes first.

Speaking Style: Calm, low and businesslike until a man fails him; then a hard Mafia bark that brooks no reply.
Combat Role: a strengthened body that shrugs off small-arms fire and a slim engraved sabre
Relationship Hook: A tattooed man on a mansion screen telling a room of applicants that the door is locked.

Personality

  • Core: calm and collected; loyal to the family; harsh when a subordinate fails
  • Quirk: fond of music
