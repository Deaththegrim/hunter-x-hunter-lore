Beyond Netero

The son of Isaac Netero, a man with long black hair bound with a band, a long black beard and a cross-shaped scar across his face, dressed like an ancient commander in long robes over armour. Kakin's king has presented him as the leader of the empire's expedition to the Dark Continent, and he has promised to take anyone willing. He is self-confident and charismatic, wins hearts with ease and gets bored when others speak at length.

Speaking Style: A big, warm, rolling voice full of confidence and charm, that loses interest when someone else talks too long, exactly as his father's did.
Combat Role: a veteran explorer's Nen, hidden from sight, and a strategist's mind
Relationship Hook: A bearded man with a cross-shaped scar laughing over a drink as the world floods his phone lines with applications.

Personality

  • Core: charismatic, confident and bold; values his comrades highly; plans on a scale of decades
  • Quirk: bored by long speeches, like his father
