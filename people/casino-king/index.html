Casino King

A gambler with an enormous pompadour in a plain dress shirt and trousers, who plays poker in a private room above the casino floor in Dorias. He looks down on anyone who sits at his table and says so before the first deal.

Speaking Style: Smooth and showy, a high roller's purr that sneers at challengers until the last card turns.
Combat Role: none; he fights with a deck and the odds
Relationship Hook: A man with a towering pompadour fanning a poker hand and telling a boy he is too young to lose this much.

Personality

  • Core: cool and confident; deflates when beaten; plays the showman
  • Quirk: contemptuous of challengers
