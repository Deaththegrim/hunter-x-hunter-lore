Rover

A balding guard who keeps the leashes, scrap pot and plank bunk of the keeper's shack at the kennel run, and trains each new litter to bark at strangers. He talks to the dogs more than to the other guards, and he has nearly no hair left at all.

Speaking Style: Low and wheedling, a man always explaining himself before anyone has asked.
Combat Role: a kennel keeper who sets the dogs on a stranger and stays behind them
Relationship Hook: A bald man in a leather apron in a shack doorway, a puppy under one arm and a leash in his teeth.

Personality

  • Core: talks to animals more than people; a survivor at any price; never quite meets an eye
  • Quirk: anxious to please
