Kaspar Imhof

The driver of the early coast route, a broad man of fifty with a thermos and a driver's cap, who eats his noodles at the stand under the canopy before every run.

Speaking Style: A warm, unhurried baritone that tells passengers where they are going before they ask.
Combat Role: none; a driver who closes the doors and drives on
Relationship Hook: A broad bus driver in a cap slurping noodles at a stand under a concrete canopy, a bus idling behind him.

Personality

  • Core: easy-going; punctual; generous with directions
  • Quirk: incurious by choice
