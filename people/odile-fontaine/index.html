Odile Fontaine

The owner of the Skyfoot Patisserie, a plump, rosy woman in her fifties with a lace cap and flour to the elbows. Her window of cakes and chocolate draws every child on the plaza road, and she gives the smallest ones a broken biscuit for pressing their noses to the glass.

Speaking Style: Soft and motherly, a confectioner who calls every child 'my little one' and every adult 'dear'.
Combat Role: none; a rolling pin, a bell over the door and a whole street of shopkeepers who adore her
Relationship Hook: A plump confectioner in a lace cap handing a ribbon-tied box across a counter of cakes, a framed photo of a white-haired boy behind her.

Personality

  • Core: kind and fussy; remembers every child's favourite; worries aloud
  • Quirk: dusts the framed photo every morning
