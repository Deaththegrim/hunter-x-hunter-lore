Fugetsu Hui Guo Rou

The eleventh prince of Kakin and younger twin of Kacho, a beautiful girl with dark chin-length hair who dresses simply and practises flower arranging. She is withdrawn and timid, and the palace staff would rather serve her than her sister or her mother. She loves travel and hiking and keeps a picture of the two of them on an outing.

Speaking Style: Quiet, timid and kind, a girl's voice that rarely rises above a murmur.
Combat Role: none; a quiet girl who would rather escape than fight
Relationship Hook: A quiet dark-haired girl arranging flowers while her twin sister shouts at the guards outside.

Personality

  • Core: timid and withdrawn; kind to servants; classical tastes
  • Quirk: devoted to her twin
