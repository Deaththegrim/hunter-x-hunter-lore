Cleapatro

The supreme magistrate of the court that sits on Tier 1, a lean woman with delicate features and long, thin eyebrows, her hair dressed in a heavy style like an ancient queen's. She wears a pale high-collared uniform striped in black and keeps a telephone at her elbow on the bench. She speaks calmly and weighs everything before she rules.

Speaking Style: Calm, formal and exact, a judge's voice that weighs each clause, and goes ice-cold when her work is mocked.
Combat Role: none; a magistrate who fights with privilege rules, warrants and the court's officers
Relationship Hook: A lean magistrate in a striped high-collared uniform listening to a telephone at a raised bench.

Personality

  • Core: weighs every factor before ruling; refuses to rule on rumour; keeps her rulings short and her reasons long
  • Quirk: loses her temper when her work is made light of
