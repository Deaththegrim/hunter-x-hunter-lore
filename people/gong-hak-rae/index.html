Gong Hak-rae

The chairman of the collective outside Taba City, a red-faced, cheerful man in a cadre cap who reads the farm's progress over the barn loudspeaker every morning and paints the quota board himself. He is a genial liar, beloved by the farmers he protects and despised by the ones he does not.

Speaking Style: Jovial and fast-talking, a farm chairman who answers every question with a slogan and a wink.
Combat Role: none; a chairman's voice on a loudspeaker and a barn full of farmers who owe him
Relationship Hook: A red-faced chairman painting a higher number onto a quota board while a tractor coughs behind him.

Personality

  • Core: jovial and shameless; protective of his farmers; secretly exhausted by the lying
  • Quirk: lies as easily as breathing
