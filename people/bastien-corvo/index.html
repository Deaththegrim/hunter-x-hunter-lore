Bastien Corvo

A stooped turnkey of sixty with a bucket, a stiff brush and a ring of heavy keys, who has looked after this chamber for most of his working life. He sharpens what is left on the rack and scrubs what is left on the floor.

Speaking Style: A dry old croak that complains about mess in the same tone as it describes a death.
Combat Role: none; an old turnkey who locks a door and stays on the far side of it
Relationship Hook: A stooped old turnkey scrubbing a dark stain on a stone floor beneath a rack of knives.

Personality

  • Core: unshockable; takes small liberties; privately gentle with the dying
  • Quirk: grumbles constantly
