Pekotero

A short Temp Hunter on Beyond's team with a short haircut, animal-like features, a bubble-patterned hat and a lollipop always in his mouth. He loves video and board games and is easy-going about everything.

Speaking Style: A laid-back, sugary voice that talks round a lollipop about games.
Combat Role: a specialist's ability he has not shown
Relationship Hook: A short man with a lollipop playing a handheld game while the rest of the team argues.

Personality
