Wei Qinglan

The senior clerk of the brokerage house, an old man in a scholar's grey gown who works an abacus faster than the young men can work their calculators. He keeps the house's real accounts in a room upstairs and greets every client by the name his father knew them by.

Speaking Style: Dry, courteous and old-fashioned, a clerk who addresses everyone as if they were his grandfather's guests.
Combat Role: none; an old clerk whose defence is that nobody else can read his books
Relationship Hook: An old clerk in a grey gown clicking an abacus beside a safe, his spectacles on the end of his nose.

Personality

  • Core: works an abacus faster than any machine; never writes a request he dislikes; drinks the same tea at the same hour
  • Quirk: greets clients by their fathers' names
