Geretta

A blowgun expert, applicant 384, with an afro puff, sunglasses, a white ruff and a heavy laced hat. He is shrewd and cautious about everything, officious toward those he beats, and generous with praise for a clever move.

Speaking Style: A smooth, officious, condescending voice that compliments a clever opponent as it beats him.
Combat Role: a blowgun from hiding and days of patient stalking
Relationship Hook: A man in a ruff and sunglasses lowering a blowgun, nodding approval at the boy he has just hit.

Personality

  • Core: shrewd and cautious; officious to the defeated; patient on the hunt
  • Quirk: praises cleverness, even an enemy's
