Kaiser

The Ministry of Justice's deputy chief prosecutor aboard, slightly tall, of average build, with dark combed-back hair, arched eyebrows and rectangular glasses, in the suit and tie of the Justice Bureau. He is calm and professional and rarely changes expression, even when he is saying something flirtatious. People take him for a workaholic.

Speaking Style: Flat, measured and formal, a prosecutor's even voice that says passionate things without a flicker.
Combat Role: none shown; a prosecutor who fights with procedure, custody and the bureau's independence
Relationship Hook: A bespectacled prosecutor in a quiet interrogation room, offering a bottle of water and an unreadable look.

Personality

  • Core: calm and expressionless; professional to a fault; believes words can change minds
  • Quirk: says he is ruled by love
