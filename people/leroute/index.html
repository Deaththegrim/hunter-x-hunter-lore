Leroute

A short young woman with long lashes, magenta eyes and pink hair tied with yellow ribbons into a big ponytail, two bangs left loose. She is serving a hundred and twelve years for illegal gambling and trafficking in endangered animals. She does not fight; she bets, and she asks the applicants to stake hours of their time against years of her sentence.

Speaking Style: Bright, reasonable and friendly on top, with a gambler's flat calm underneath that never gives anything away.
Combat Role: none; a gambler who wins a contest of nerve, odds and other people's habits
Relationship Hook: A small pink-haired prisoner smiling sweetly across a stone platform as she offers a bet in hours.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and reasonable on the surface; manipulative underneath; reads a mark at a glance
  • Quirk: a perfect poker face
