Mortimer Quayle

A neat, forgettable man in a navy blazer and a club tie who drinks gin in the corner booth of the hotel bar every evening and tips the staff well. He tells anyone who asks that he sells insurance.

Speaking Style: A soft, well-spoken, courteous voice that compliments the bar's gin and never says anything at all about himself.
Combat Role: a quiet killer with a garrotte and a thin blade who works close, alone and once
Relationship Hook: An unremarkable man in a blazer raising his gin to an applicant across the bar and asking what number she drew.

Personality

  • Core: courteous and forgettable; patient with small talk; afraid of anyone better at his trade
  • Quirk: watches exits, never doors
