Ivo Mink

The night clerk at Hotel Marro, a young man with dyed hair and a stack of comics at his elbow, who hands out keys and watches the vending machine for the whole of every night.

Speaking Style: A bored, half-awake drawl that becomes suddenly sharp when something is worth noticing.
Combat Role: none; a clerk behind a counter with a phone
Relationship Hook: A sleepy young clerk with dyed hair reading a comic behind a hotel counter, lit by a vending machine.

Personality
