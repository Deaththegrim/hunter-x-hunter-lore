Tolly Brannock

The chandler on Harbour Street, a wiry man in his thirties with a pencil behind each ear, who sells hooks, line, shackles, paint, oilskins and anything else a boat eats. His shop is narrow and dark and smells of tar and new rope, and he will talk to anyone who stands still in it.

Speaking Style: Quick and chatty, a shopman's patter that never quite finishes one sentence before the next begins.
Combat Role: none; a marlinspike he has never used on anything but rope
Relationship Hook: A wiry chandler leaning over a counter of fishhooks, pointing proudly at a hand-painted sign in his window.

Personality

  • Core: talks while he works and works while he talks; gives credit to anyone with a boat; loses his nerve in front of Mito Freecss
  • Quirk: boasts about the boy's catch as if it were his own
