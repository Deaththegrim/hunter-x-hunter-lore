Marta Kowalczyk

The cook of the ground crew village's canteen, a broad, red-armed woman of fifty who has served soup to every shift for twenty years and buried a husband who worked the runways. She knows every family in the village and every child's birthday, and she reads the night shift's faces when they come in.

Speaking Style: Big, warm and scolding, a cook who feeds you first and asks questions over the second bowl.
Combat Role: none; a ladle and a big voice, and the whole canteen on its feet behind her
Relationship Hook: A broad cook in a kerchief ladling soup through a hatch, eyeing the night shift as they file in.

Personality

  • Core: feeds everyone before she listens; scolds the young men like sons; keeps a calendar of every child's birthday
  • Quirk: goes quiet whenever someone mentions the night shift
