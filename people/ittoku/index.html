Ittoku

The consigliere of the Cha-R Family, an elderly man with small dark eyes bagged underneath, wrinkles across his forehead and chin, and fair hair to his shoulders. He is calm, asserts his authority whenever he can, and keeps a close eye on the family's crates in the holds.

Speaking Style: Calm and old, a consigliere's courtesy with authority laid under every word.
Combat Role: an old consigliere who fights through the family's guns and his own nerve
Relationship Hook: An old mobster with shoulder-length hair counting sealed trunks in a caged hold, a cigarette between his fingers.

Personality

  • Core: calm, then sharp; asserts his authority when he can; suspicious of the Troupe
  • Quirk: stubs out cigarettes wherever he is
