Goz

Applicant 371, a tall, heavily muscled spearman with shoulder-length hair and a long drooping moustache, in a teal sleeveless shirt, an orange sash and purple trousers. He reached the bottom of Trick Tower among the first handful and carries his long spear the way other men carry their name.

Speaking Style: Short, proud sentences in a hard voice that treats every exchange as the opening of a duel.
Combat Role: a spearman who fells a sapling with one swing and fights through wounds that should have stopped him
Relationship Hook: A moustached spearman in a teal shirt crouching in thick bushes at the edge of a clearing, spear held low.

Personality

  • Core: proud, with a code he will not bend; silent in cover; contemptuous of tricks
  • Quirk: hungry for a worthy opponent
