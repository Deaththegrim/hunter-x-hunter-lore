Emeric Varga

A fruit grower on the terraced hills west of town, a lean, sun-browned man in his fifties who fills the market's stalls and delivers fruit to the estate's gate twice a week. He has done so for twenty years and has never once been allowed through.

Speaking Style: Plain and chatty, a fruit grower who talks about pears the way other people talk about grandchildren.
Combat Role: none; a farm truck and a ladder
Relationship Hook: A sun-browned grower stacking crates of pears into his truck for the run up to the gate.

Personality

  • Core: asks no questions about the sealed boxes; chats to anyone at the stall; worries about the weather constantly
  • Quirk: proud of his fruit
