Yo Man-sik

The headman of a farming village near the border, a skinny old man with a wispy beard, a cloth cap and a medal from a war he will describe for an hour. He organises the anniversary festivals, hangs the bunting himself from a ladder too tall for him, and makes the speeches nobody listens to. The village loves him for the festivals.

Speaking Style: A cheerful, wheezing old voice that turns everything into an announcement for the square.
Combat Role: none; an old man with a ladder and a loud voice
Relationship Hook: A skinny old headman on a ladder too tall for him, stringing bunting across a village square.

Personality

  • Core: loves a celebration; fiddles the village's numbers for its own good; proud of a medal nobody else remembers
  • Quirk: cheerful and long-winded
