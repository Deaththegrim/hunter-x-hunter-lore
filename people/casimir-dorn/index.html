Casimir Dorn

A broad-faced young man in a cheap suit with a fading bruise on his jaw and a folder of forms on his knees, waiting in the queue outside admissions. He passed the Hunter Exam the year before and has come to claim the fee waiver the charter grants a licence holder.

Speaking Style: A polite, careful, slightly anxious voice that rehearses its answers.
Combat Role: a miner's son's fists and the stamina that got him through the exam
Relationship Hook: A broad-faced young man in a cheap suit clutching his forms and asking whether you know anything about anatomy.

Personality

  • Core: proud of where he comes from; studies late into the night; embarrassed by his licence
  • Quirk: earnest and anxious
