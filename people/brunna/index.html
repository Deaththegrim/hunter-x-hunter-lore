Brunna

The smith of the open-fronted forge on the road, a broad-shouldered woman with burn-speckled forearms who shoes the district's horses and mends its ploughs by hand and hammer. She may work only iron smelted inside the country, and the law counts her scrap pile twice a year.

Speaking Style: Loud, rough and impatient, a smith's bark used to being heard over a hammer.
Combat Role: a smith with a hammer and the arms to use it
Relationship Hook: A broad woman at the anvil, a red-hot horseshoe in her tongs, telling you to stand clear of the horse.

Personality

  • Core: blunt and hard-working; proud of her trade; has no patience for talkers
  • Quirk: bends the law for a good tool
