Orla Vesey

A painter from the mainland who rented a stone hut below the falls for one summer and has stayed three winters, painting the same waterfall in every weather. She is thin, paint-spattered and polite, and the islanders consider her harmless and slightly mad.

Speaking Style: Dreamy and distracted, a painter's soft voice that drifts mid-sentence to look at the light.
Combat Role: none; a palette knife and the habit of disappearing behind the water
Relationship Hook: A paint-spattered young woman on a mossy boulder sketching a waterfall through drifting spray.

Personality

  • Core: forgets to eat when she is painting; polite, distracted and quietly stubborn; keeps the ledge to herself
  • Quirk: looks at light rather than people
