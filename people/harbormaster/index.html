Harbormaster

A stocky man in a button-up shirt with a long collar, a flat captain's hat and a wristwatch, with coral-like antlers growing from his head, pointed ears, slitted eyes and hairy arms. He decides who may leave the island from its only port, and a ticket comes from him by bribe or by beating him.

Speaking Style: Grasping and changeable, a harbourmaster's grumble that names a different fare every morning.
Combat Role: a burly brawler who expects to win and goes down to a real fighter
Relationship Hook: An antlered harbourmaster leaning back in his chair and telling a boy the fare has gone up today.

Personality

  • Core: greedy; guards the way out; folds to force
  • Quirk: mercurial
