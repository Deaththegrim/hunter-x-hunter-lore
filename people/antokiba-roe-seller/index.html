Antokiba Roe Seller

A broad fishwife in a rubber apron and a headscarf who sells Galgaida roe from little glass jars at the back of the covered market. She greets every buyer with the same cry about long life and takes nothing but cards for her goods.

Speaking Style: Loud and singsong, a fishwife's cry that repeats the same three lines all day.
Combat Role: none; she shouts for the town and hides under the slab
Relationship Hook: A fishwife in a headscarf holding up a jar of roe and crying that it will add years to your life.

Personality

  • Core: loud; strict about card money; cheerful in the same words every day
  • Quirk: repeats her cry
