Dorran

The other interpreter at the lodge, a heavy-browed, bearded man with a big nose, a broad hat and a fur cape over his shoulders. He rides behind his partner and lets her do the smiling. In the back room he writes the reports in a square careful hand and seals them for the rider.

Speaking Style: Gruff and slow, a man who says less than his partner and hears twice as much.
Combat Role: a big man who stands in a doorway and does not move
Relationship Hook: A bearded man in a fur cape at a lamp-lit desk, pressing a seal into hot wax without looking up.

Personality

  • Core: gruff and watchful; writes neatly and slowly; trusts paper more than people
  • Quirk: beginning to doubt his masters
