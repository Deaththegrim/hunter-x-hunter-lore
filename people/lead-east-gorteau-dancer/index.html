Lead East Gorteau Dancer

The lead of the state dance troupe, a lithe young woman with short brown hair and green eyes who rehearses in a tight leotard, furry cuffs at wrists and ankles and a band of cat ears. She has danced for foreign delegations, for generals and for the leader himself, and she can switch on a smile at full brightness in the time it takes a curtain to rise.

Speaking Style: Breezy, bubbly and stage-bright, a performer's voice that laughs between sentences; offstage it drops low and tired.
Combat Role: none; quick on her feet and supple, but she has never struck anyone
Relationship Hook: A dancer in cat ears spinning alone in a mirrored room long after the others have gone home.

Personality

  • Core: radiant on stage, flat and weary off it; shields the youngest dancers from the box; practises until her feet bleed
  • Quirk: laughs at things that are not funny when an official is near
