Jakob Eisler

The metalworker of Ironweight Outfitters, a bald, leather-aproned giant who sews iron plates into vests, boots and wristbands for visitors who want to try the gate. He fits each one himself, makes the customer walk round the shop and adds a plate if they can still smile.

Speaking Style: Gruff, deep and short, a smith who growls instructions over the forge and laughs like a hammer blow.
Combat Role: a smith's arms, a forge hammer and a vest of his own iron
Relationship Hook: A bald giant in a leather apron cinching an iron-plated vest onto a grinning teenager.

Personality

  • Core: blunt and good-humoured; never asks where iron comes from; proud of every seam
  • Quirk: adds weight until a customer stops smiling
