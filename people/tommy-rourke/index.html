Tommy Rourke

The boss of the porters' union at the Hollis Wholesale Market, a barrel of a man in a rubber apron and boots who stands at the end of the fish hall at four in the morning and picks who works. He carries a pistol in his apron and a list in his head.

Speaking Style: Loud, flat and final, a union boss's voice that ends an argument by finishing his sentence.
Combat Role: a market bruiser with a pistol in his apron and a dozen porters at his whistle
Relationship Hook: A barrel-chested man in a rubber apron pointing at porters in a fish hall under hanging lamps.

Personality

  • Core: blunt; brutal to strikers; obeys the family without love
  • Quirk: fair to his own men
