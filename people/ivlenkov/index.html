Ivlenkov

One of Neon Nostrade's bodyguards, a plump man with brown hair combed back and green eyes, in a black suit, white shirt and dark red tie. He is calm and correct around his client and in front of the other families, and quick on his feet for his build.

Speaking Style: Even and professional, a bodyguard's low voice that names the room's dangers without fuss.
Combat Role: a trained bodyguard who keeps his client covered and dodges fire rather than trading it
Relationship Hook: A plump man in a black suit holding a car door on an airport runway while the boss's daughter climbs in.

Personality

  • Core: notes every face in a room; quick for a heavy man; keeps his opinions to himself
  • Quirk: calm and professional
