Ambrose Kettering

A lean, sunburnt man in an expensive field coat with a hired tracker and two pack mules, camped at the old riders' clearing on the forest track. He tells the loggers he is mapping old trails for a museum.

Speaking Style: A soft, reasonable, amused voice that makes grave-robbing sound like scholarship.
Combat Role: a seasoned field man who fights with a rifle and two hired guns, and keeps a mule between himself and trouble
Relationship Hook: A man in a field coat crouched over a carved marker on a tree, smiling as if he has found an old friend.

Personality

  • Core: charming and reasonable; utterly without shame; methodical
  • Quirk: contemptuous of the dead
