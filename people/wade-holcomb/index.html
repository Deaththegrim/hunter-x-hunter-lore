Wade Holcomb

A young sergeant of twenty-six, top of his class at the academy, who asked for the highway post because his father patrolled it before him. He is tall, clean-shaven and very careful with his paperwork.

Speaking Style: Polite and earnest, a young policeman who still says sir to the men he pulls over.
Combat Role: a police academy graduate; a service revolver, a baton and a patrol car's radio
Relationship Hook: A young sergeant in a tan uniform writing in a notebook at a counter, glancing at the door.

Personality

  • Core: says sir and ma'am to everyone; polishes his boots every morning; speaks of his father with pride
  • Quirk: writes everything down
