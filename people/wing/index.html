Wing

An assistant master of Shingen-ryu kung fu living near the Heavens Arena, a man of average height with messy black hair and glasses, his pink shirt only half tucked in. He is modest, quiet and absent-minded, a patient but strict teacher who slaps a reckless student, and his only pupil is the boy Zushi.

Speaking Style: A mild, absent-minded, gentle voice that explains carefully and becomes very firm when a student is about to do something reckless.
Combat Role: Shingen-ryu kung fu and aura flowed through anything he touches, a paper page that cuts a can
Relationship Hook: A bespectacled young man with his shirt half untucked raising a playing card that has just sliced through a can.

Personality

  • Core: modest, quiet and absent-minded; patient but strict; tells a harmless lie to protect the unready
  • Quirk: cares deeply for his students' safety
