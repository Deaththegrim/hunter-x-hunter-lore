Matyas Varnay

A ten-year-old boy of the tenements, small and serious, with a buzz cut, scabbed knees and a hand-me-down tracksuit. He plays at being a Floor Master with the other children in the courtyard and always wins, and lately he has stopped wanting to play.

Speaking Style: Bright, earnest and breathless, a small boy who explains things with his whole body.
Combat Role: a courtyard brawler's scrambles and something heavy in the air that makes bigger boys back off
Relationship Hook: A small boy standing with his eyes closed in a chalk ring, the washing on the lines above him stirring though there is no wind.

Personality

  • Core: earnest and solemn; copies everything he sees; proud and a little frightened
  • Quirk: sits very still for a child
