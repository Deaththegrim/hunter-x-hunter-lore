Joska

A young carter who hauls salt inland and cloth to the border, sleeping at the inn twice a week and talking to anyone who sits still. He knows every road in the district and the state of every bridge.

Speaking Style: Quick and chatty, a carter who tells three stories at once and swears all of them are true.
Combat Role: none; a carter who whips the horse and drives
Relationship Hook: A wiry carter crouched by his horse's hoof, glancing over his shoulder at the gate.

Personality

  • Core: chatty and cheerful; cannot keep a good story to himself; loyal to his horse
  • Quirk: jumpy since that night
