Ging Freecss

A short man with stubble, spiky black hair and a cloth-wrapped visor, who looks like a vagrant and is one of the most famous Hunters alive. He left Whale Island before he was twelve, passed the exam as its only success that year, found the Lurka Ruins, and spends his fortune restoring ruins for the public; he holds two stars as a ruins Hunter. He sits on the Zodiacs, the chairman's council, as the Boar. He is clever, whimsical and shy of being found, and he makes plans years ahead and then acts on a whim.

Speaking Style: A rough, careless man's voice that laughs at its own jokes and drops into sudden, exact seriousness; he speaks to a crowd as if to one friend and to his son as if to a stranger.
Combat Role: a master's Nen and a talent for imitating any punching technique he has once been hit with
Relationship Hook: A scruffy man sitting at the top of an enormous tree, looking out over the world as if it were his garden.

Personality

  • Core: whimsical, sharp and impossible to predict; respects Netero more than he admits; plans years ahead, then follows a hunch
  • Quirk: shy of crowds and of meeting his own son
