Agathe Loam

The eldest of the growers on the stepped beds, a bent woman with a hoe who has grown greens, beans and mushrooms in the city's rot for fifty years. She can tell a heap that will feed a family from one that will poison it by crumbling a handful.

Speaking Style: Old, warm and blunt, a grower who scolds and feeds you in the same breath.
Combat Role: an old woman with a hoe who hooks an ankle and does not let go
Relationship Hook: A bent old grower crumbling black soil between her fingers above terraced green beds.

Personality

  • Core: feeds strangers before asking their business; talks to the mushrooms; has no patience for anyone who steps on a bed
  • Quirk: crumbles soil and sniffs it before any greeting
