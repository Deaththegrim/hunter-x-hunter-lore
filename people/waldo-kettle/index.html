Waldo Kettle

The unloading boss of the road end, a big-bellied man whose voice carries over truck engines, who tells each driver where to tip and each scavenger crew which load is theirs. Nothing coming off a truck is touched until he says it belongs to the city.

Speaking Style: Big and booming, a voice built to beat a truck engine, gone soft and gravelly in a quiet room.
Combat Role: a big-bellied brawler who stops a man by walking into him and letting the crews close in
Relationship Hook: A big-bellied man in work gloves waving a dump truck back toward the heaps, his beard full of dust.

Personality

  • Core: bellows over engines and whispers in quiet; slaps a truck's side to send it off; gives the first find of every load to a child
  • Quirk: never lets anyone take anything back
