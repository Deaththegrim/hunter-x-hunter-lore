Marek Vossberg

A broad, grey-bearded man in a salt-stained pea coat who rents the room over the chandler's on Harbour Street between contracts. He was born on the island, left it on a trawler at sixteen, and came back with a Hunter licence and a reputation among salvage firms as the man to call when a hull sinks somewhere nobody else can reach. He swims every morning off the quay in water that makes the fishermen wince.

Speaking Style: Slow and weathered, a big man who rations his words like water on a long passage and laughs once, low, when something genuinely surprises him.
Combat Role: a heavy brawler who fights best waist-deep or under the surface, a boat hook in one fist and aura packed into the other
Relationship Hook: A grey-bearded man stepping out of icy harbour water at dawn as if it were a warm bath.

Personality

  • Core: unhurried in everything except the water; says little about his work and nothing about his failures; measures every stranger by how they stand on a moving deck
  • Quirk: buys the old skippers their drinks and listens to them for hours
