Bourbon

A bulky man with a big nose and black hair under a tan turban, in a loose robe of light blue and purple, known on the exam circuit as the snake charmer. Applicant 103, he has sat the exam more times than he admits, and he passed Menchi's retrial by diving for a Spider Eagle egg and then grinning over how good it tasted boiled.

Speaking Style: A heavy, unhurried rasp, pleased with itself, that names a price before it names anything else.
Combat Role: none of his own; a charmer who fills a room with venomous snakes and bargains with the bitten
Relationship Hook: A turbaned man in a blue and purple robe lying back in a dark cave while snakes pour over the floor around him.

Personality

  • Core: lazy, and proud of it; lets his snakes do the work; a haggler first and a fighter never
  • Quirk: talks to the snakes as if they were staff
