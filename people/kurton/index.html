Kurton

A Hunter with short spiky hair and thick eyebrows in a bodyguard's black suit, the third of the trio who have signed on to guard the youngest prince. He is calm and serious.

Speaking Style: A calm, serious, few-worded voice that betrays shock only when something truly strange appears.
Combat Role: a conjurer's ability and a bodyguard's discipline
Relationship Hook: A serious bodyguard with thick eyebrows staring in shock at something only some can see.

Personality

  • Core: calm and serious; keeps to the mission; quiet
  • Quirk: shaken by the unknown
