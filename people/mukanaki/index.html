Mukanaki

A player with neatly combed hair and long sideburns, in a sweater with a line down each sleeve. He was one of the veterans who crowded round two boys for their first prize, and these days he spends his mornings chasing puffballs down the slope.

Speaking Style: Jaunty and talkative, a man who narrates his own chase out loud.
Combat Role: an eager chaser who runs his quarry rather than reads it
Relationship Hook: A neatly combed man sprawled at the bottom of a grassy slope while a fluffy blur sits on his back.

Personality

  • Core: jaunty; tireless; keeps his hair neat even in a chase
  • Quirk: won't ask for help
