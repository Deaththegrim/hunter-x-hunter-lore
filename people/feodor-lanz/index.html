Feodor Lanz

The prison's medic, a plump man in his fifties with round glasses and his sleeves always rolled, who stitches guards and inmates on the same beds. In January he also takes in applicants pulled out of the tower early.

Speaking Style: A warm, tired voice that talks a patient through the pain while his hands do the real work.
Combat Role: none; a medic who backs away and keeps the instruments between himself and trouble
Relationship Hook: A plump medic with rolled sleeves bandaging a guard's arm beside a row of iron beds.

Personality

  • Core: kind without being soft; keeps exact notes; tired of fixing what the tower breaks
  • Quirk: talks while he works
