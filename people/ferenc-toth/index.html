Ferenc Toth

The old schoolmaster of the heaps, who taught sums and letters somewhere far away until something sent him here, and has taught them on a sheet of beaten tin ever since. He uses whatever schoolbooks the trucks bring, in whatever language they happen to be printed, and his pupils leave able to read three alphabets and argue in four.

Speaking Style: Dry, precise and patient, an old schoolmaster's diction, correcting a word in passing without breaking the sentence.
Combat Role: none; an old man with a pointer and a stare that stops a fight across the room
Relationship Hook: A stooped old teacher in wire-mended spectacles tapping a word on a tin blackboard.

Personality

  • Core: corrects grammar in four languages; lets a pupil teach the lesson they know better; chalks the date on the tin every morning though nobody asks
  • Quirk: keeps his own past to a single sentence
