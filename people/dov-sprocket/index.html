Dov Sprocket

The projectionist of the screening days, a lanky man with a tool roll who keeps the church's one projector clattering on parts begged from Dead Set Hill. He threads the reels, shoos children off the cable, and stands behind the bedsheet with the voice actors when one of them forgets a line.

Speaking Style: Easy and a little harried, a handyman's patter that drops into a mutter whenever a machine misbehaves.
Combat Role: none; he swings his tool roll and a heavy reel can and hopes the children scatter first
Relationship Hook: A lanky man threading a film reel by torchlight behind a bedsheet screen, a fuse tucked behind his ear.

Personality

  • Core: talks to the projector as if it were a mule; carries spare fuses behind his ear; lets nobody else touch the reels
  • Quirk: mouths along to every line of the cartoon
