Lucien Varga

The manager of the seaside hotel the commission takes over each year, a slim man with a pencil moustache and a perfect tie. His staff are paid extra to forget the week's guests.

Speaking Style: Smooth and deferential, a hotelier's voice that never asks a guest anything personal.
Combat Role: none; a manager who calls the commission's guards
Relationship Hook: A slim hotel manager with a pencil moustache standing behind a lobby desk, sorting room keys.

Personality

  • Core: discreet; observant; takes pride in service
  • Quirk: privately judgemental
