Halkenburg Hui Guo Rou

The ninth prince of Kakin, born to the first queen and raised by the second, a young man of average build with shaggy blond hair, small black eyes and a wide flat nose like his brother Benjamin's. He went away to boarding school young and studied abroad, dresses always in a suit and has said in public that he wants the monarchy gone. Of all his siblings he says he can accept only Tserriednich.

Speaking Style: Earnest, precise and principled, a young scholar's voice that hardens into resolve.
Combat Role: a scholar-prince and archery medallist who fights only when his men stand with him
Relationship Hook: A young prince in a suit at a desk, writing a letter by lamplight with a soldier at the door.

Personality

  • Core: principled and strict; estranged from his family; resolute once decided
  • Quirk: wants to abolish the monarchy
