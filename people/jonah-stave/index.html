Jonah Stave

The bottom man in the island's sawpit, a cheerful, sawdust-caked young man who stands in the trench under the log and pulls the long saw down while his partner guides it from above. He has a permanent cough and a permanent grin.

Speaking Style: Coughing and good-natured, a pit sawyer's sawdust-roughened voice that laughs at its own hard luck.
Combat Role: none; a pit saw that needs two men and a young man's stubborn back
Relationship Hook: A sawdust-caked young man grinning up from a sawpit, the long blade above him.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful through any discomfort; doubts the old forest beliefs and keeps them anyway; works for two when his partner is down
  • Quirk: coughs sawdust and laughs about it
