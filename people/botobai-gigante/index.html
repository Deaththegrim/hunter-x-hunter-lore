Botobai Gigante

The Zodiacs' Dragon and its eldest member, a towering, heavy man of late middle age with a face as wrinkled as a dragon's hide, a curling moustache, upswept eyebrows and hair bound into two flame-shaped bundles. He is a three-star Hunter of terrorists, a military analyst and a public prosecutor, collected and fiercely loyal to Netero.

Speaking Style: A deep, booming, formal voice that rarely rises, and when it does the table shakes.
Combat Role: a former sparring partner of Netero's, a giant's strength and a soldier's analysis
Relationship Hook: A giant old man in a flame-collared coat slamming a fist on the council table.

Personality

  • Core: collected, grave and loyal to Netero; favours cooperation over faction; carries the weight of the eldest
  • Quirk: loses his temper rarely and memorably
