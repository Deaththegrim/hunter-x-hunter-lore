Otis Bramwell

A sunburnt, cheerful guide of thirty who drives tourists out from the city in an old open jeep to see the desert at sunset. He parks in the hollow behind the low ridge because it has shade and a view, and he tells every party the same ghost story about the silver camp.

Speaking Style: Bright, practised and theatrical, a guide whose every sentence sounds like the start of a tour.
Combat Role: none; he guns the jeep and drives off across the sand, passengers or not
Relationship Hook: A sunburnt guide leaning on an open jeep in a sandy hollow, binoculars raised toward the flats.

Personality

  • Core: never asks a passenger's business; drives too fast on sand; proud of his sunset like he made it
  • Quirk: tells the same ghost story to every party
