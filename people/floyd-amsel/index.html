Floyd Amsel

The clerk and part owner of the Gordeau Motor Court, a gaunt, unsmiling man of sixty who sits under the slow ceiling fan in the office day and night. He takes cash in advance, asks no names, and has a bell and a pistol under the counter.

Speaking Style: Flat, quiet and unblinking, a clerk who sounds bored right up until he does not.
Combat Role: a cold old shooter; a revolver from under the counter, fired without warning
Relationship Hook: A gaunt old clerk under a slow ceiling fan, one hand on a counter bell, the other out of sight.

Personality

  • Core: never smiles; takes cash before he hands over a key; watches the road more than the guests
  • Quirk: rings the counter bell once when he wants silence
