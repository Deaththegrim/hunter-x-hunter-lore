Clem Dorsey

The airport police's dog handler, a lanky, sunburnt man of thirty-five who sleeps in the kennel block more nights than at home. His four shepherds patrol the fence with him, and he swears they can smell a lie on a man's breath.

Speaking Style: Slow and drawling, a dog man who talks to his animals more gently than to people.
Combat Role: a handler and his dogs; four shepherds loosed on a runner and a baton for what gets past them
Relationship Hook: A lanky handler kneeling in a dog run with his arm round a grey shepherd, both staring toward the cargo sheds.

Personality

  • Core: trusts an animal's nose over a manifest; eats his lunch in the dog run; keeps a photograph of each dog he has lost
  • Quirk: talks softly to dogs and roughly to men
