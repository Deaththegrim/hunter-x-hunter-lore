Mito Freecss

Gon's aunt, a young woman with short orange hair who keeps house and sells fish on Whale Island and has raised Gon since he was a baby. She is kind, patient and capable, embarrassed by teasing, and fiercely protective of the boy. She never wanted him to follow his father into hunting, and set him an impossible task to stop him.

Speaking Style: A warm, practical woman's voice that scolds and fusses in the same breath and goes soft and unsteady when she has to let go of someone she loves.
Combat Role: none; a broom, a sharp tongue and a whole island that would come running
Relationship Hook: A young woman at a kitchen table reading a letter twice, with a boy's fishing rod leaning in the corner.

Personality

  • Core: motherly, patient and practical; still angry at Ging and still fond of him; keeps every promise, even the ones she hoped not to keep
  • Quirk: blushes when her grandmother tells old stories
