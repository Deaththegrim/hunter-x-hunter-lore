Gotoh

One of the Zoldyck butlers and among the most trusted, a tall man in a black suit with combed-back black hair, a trimmed beard and pointed glasses. Every call from outside reaches the main house through him. He is courteous, strict about the household's secrecy and intimidating to strangers, and he cares for Killua, whom the family placed in his charge.

Speaking Style: A clipped, formal, perfectly courteous voice that never raises itself; under strain it thickens with a temper as hot as Leorio's.
Combat Role: coins flicked from the fingers like bullets, a butler's speed and a body that shrugs off a crash
Relationship Hook: A butler in pointed glasses flicking a coin from hand to hand faster than a boy can follow.

Personality

  • Core: courteous, strict and loyal; hot-tempered under the courtesy; fond of Killua and will not say so
  • Quirk: hates cheating, even in a game
