Augustin Pell

A retired schoolmaster in a cardigan with leather elbows, who taught the island's children until there were too few to teach. He lives alone at the top of Upper Town with a telescope, a wall of books shipped over one crate at a time, and a cat that belongs to nobody. Ging Freecss and Mito Freecss both sat in his classroom.

Speaking Style: Dry and courteous, an old teacher's voice that corrects grammar out of habit and apologises for it every time.
Combat Role: none; a walking stick and the stare that once silenced a classroom
Relationship Hook: An old schoolmaster at a window with a brass telescope, a red pencil behind his ear.

Personality

  • Core: courteous to everyone, even the cat; corrects grammar and then apologises for it; keeps a telescope trained on the harbour
  • Quirk: speaks of former pupils as if they were still eleven
