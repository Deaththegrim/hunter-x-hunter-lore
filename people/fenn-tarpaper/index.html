Fenn Tarpaper

The builder everyone on the shanty rows calls when a roof leaks or a wall leans, a long-armed man who can raise a shack on top of a shack in a day from doors, sheet tin and tarpaulin. He works for a meal and a story.

Speaking Style: Rich and easy, a builder who sings while he works and talks the same way.
Combat Role: a long-armed builder who fights with a claw hammer and drops a wall on a pursuer
Relationship Hook: A long-armed builder balancing a sheet of tin on a shack roof, a claw hammer at his belt.

Personality

  • Core: sings as he works; will not build higher than three; takes his pay in stories
  • Quirk: knocks on every wall he passes to hear it
