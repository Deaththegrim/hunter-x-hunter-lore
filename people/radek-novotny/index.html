Radek Novotny

The chief of the window-cleaning crews, a weathered, wiry man of fifty with a sun-cracked face and hands like rope. He has worked the outside of the tower for twenty years, hung from the roof in a gondola, and he runs the duty board of who cleans which face of the building.

Speaking Style: Few words, heavy accent, a steeplejack who sounds the same at a thousand metres as on the ground.
Combat Role: a steeplejack's balance, a cable hook and the strength to hang by one hand
Relationship Hook: A weathered steeplejack running a cut steel cable through his hands in a hall of hanging gondolas.

Personality

  • Core: calm and terse; fearless of heights; fiercely loyal to his crews
  • Quirk: checks every knot twice
