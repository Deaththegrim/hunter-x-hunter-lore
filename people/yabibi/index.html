Yabibi

An elderly man with high cheekbones and very little hair left, in a hooded top and matching trousers. He leads a two-man team with the younger Montreux, and between them they hold more than fifty specified cards.

Speaking Style: Dry and careful, an old man who answers a bold plan with a question about what nobody knows yet.
Combat Role: an old hand who avoids fights and shields his cards with the right defensive spells
Relationship Hook: An old man in a hooded top raising one hand at a meeting to stop a young woman's plan to use force.

Personality

  • Core: calm and reserved; rational; takes precautions before risks
  • Quirk: surprised by little
