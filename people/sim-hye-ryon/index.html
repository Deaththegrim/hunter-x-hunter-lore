Sim Hye-ryon

A ward doctor at the state hospital in Mandai, a tired, capable woman in a white coat gone grey with washing, who works double shifts in a ward of patients two to a bed. She takes the gifts relatives bring, because her salary will not feed her son, and gives the best of them to the patients who brought nothing.

Speaking Style: Low, tired and kind, a doctor's voice that delivers bad news gently and good news cautiously.
Combat Role: none; a doctor with a scalpel she would only use to heal
Relationship Hook: A tired doctor in a greying coat stitching a wound by lamplight in a ward where every bed holds two.

Personality

  • Core: tired, kind and practical; silent about the leaks for her patients' sake; angry in a way she cannot afford
  • Quirk: takes gifts and redistributes them
