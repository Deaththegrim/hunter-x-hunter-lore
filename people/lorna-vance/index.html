Lorna Vance

The receptionist of the Hotel Starling, a round-faced woman in her thirties in a green blazer with a name badge, who knows the bus routes, the cheap restaurants and which of the twin rooms has the best view of the rooftops. Families and students like her.

Speaking Style: Warm and chatty, a receptionist's voice that offers a city map before you ask.
Combat Role: none; she rings the manager and locks the key rack
Relationship Hook: A round-faced receptionist in a green blazer handing a room key over a lobby desk among potted palms.

Personality

  • Core: warm; practical; mothers the young guests
  • Quirk: does not ask questions
