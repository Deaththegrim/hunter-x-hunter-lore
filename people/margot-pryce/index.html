Margot Pryce

The head nurse of the private floor of the Yorknew Metropolitan Hospital, a tall woman in her fifties with steel-grey hair in a tight bun and a fob watch on her chest. She runs the floor's single rooms and the armed men who sit outside their doors with the same unblinking calm.

Speaking Style: Low, measured and in charge, a head nurse's voice that never lets a guard argue twice.
Combat Role: none; she has the floor locked and security on the phone before a gun clears its holster
Relationship Hook: A tall grey-haired head nurse with a clipboard staring down an armed guard in a carpeted corridor.

Personality

  • Core: unflappable; accepts gifts without blinking; fiercely protective of patients
  • Quirk: runs her floor like a ship
