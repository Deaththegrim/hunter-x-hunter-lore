Anselm Weir

The priest of the Skyfoot Chapel, a tall, stooped man in his forties with a gentle face and a cassock frayed at the hem. He keeps the candles lit for fighters before their bouts and for families after, and he writes each name in the book by the door himself.

Speaking Style: Quiet and grave, a priest who listens far longer than he speaks.
Combat Role: none; a priest in a chapel the whole town treats as sanctuary
Relationship Hook: A stooped priest writing a name in a heavy book on a lectern while candles burn on a plain altar behind him.

Personality

  • Core: patient and grave; never preaches; lights a candle for every name he writes
  • Quirk: keeps a count he cannot stop keeping
