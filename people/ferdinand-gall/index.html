Ferdinand Gall

The owner of the glassworks on the edge of Setl City, a big soft man in a linen suit gone grey with ash, who inherited the furnaces from his father and has never been as good with money as with glass. He can blow a bottle himself and sometimes does, to show off.

Speaking Style: Rich and heavy with an old-country accent, a works owner who sighs before every number.
Combat Role: none; a glassblower's pipe and the heat of the furnace
Relationship Hook: A heavy man in an ash-grey linen suit and leather apron sweating in front of a glowing furnace, glassblowing pipe in hand.

Personality

  • Core: sentimental about the works; bad with money; proud of his craft
  • Quirk: weak with stronger men
