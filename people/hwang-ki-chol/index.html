Hwang Ki-chol

The projectionist of the people's cinema, a cheerful, sweating man in a vest who lives half his life in the hot booth above the hall among stacked cans of film. He can splice a torn reel in the dark and knows every one of the state's films about the leader by heart, including the scenes he cut for running time.

Speaking Style: Warm and a little hoarse, a projectionist who talks about films like old friends and lowers his voice for the good parts.
Combat Role: none; a reel can to swing and a ladder to pull up after him
Relationship Hook: A sweating projectionist in an undershirt threading a reel, one eye on the ladder hatch.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and sentimental; loves films more than politics; trusts his friends too quickly
  • Quirk: sweats when anyone climbs the ladder
