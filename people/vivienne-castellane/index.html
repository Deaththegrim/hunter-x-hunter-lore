Vivienne Castellane

A gem dealer in her forties with a sharp black bob and a grey trouser suit, who keeps a showroom of velvet trays behind a buzzer door on Diamond Row. Her stones come from mines most dealers have only read about, because she went and found them.

Speaking Style: Clipped and elegant, a gem dealer's voice that prices a stone and its owner together.
Combat Role: an aura-trained Hunter who fights from cover with a small pistol and a cool head
Relationship Hook: A woman with a black bob studying a diamond through a loupe over a tray of velvet.

Personality

  • Core: elegant and exacting; proud of her finds; dislikes being told to stay out
  • Quirk: will not deal in fakes
