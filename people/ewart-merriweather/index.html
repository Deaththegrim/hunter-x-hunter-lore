Ewart Merriweather

The keeper of the walled orchard, a round, rosy man in a straw hat, whose grandfather was the sea captain who planted the trees. He prunes in winter, picks in autumn with the whole island, and runs the cider press for a week of the best weather of the year.

Speaking Style: Genial and rambling, an orchardist's mellow voice that drifts into the past and comes back when it remembers you.
Combat Role: none; a pruning hook and a heavy cider mallet
Relationship Hook: A round old orchardist in a straw hat patting the trunk of a gnarled apple tree.

Personality

  • Core: rambles and comes back to the point; treats the trees like elderly relatives; generous with cider and sparing with everything else
  • Quirk: superstitious about his grandfather's chest
