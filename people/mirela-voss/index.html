Mirela Voss

A woman from outside who drove in with a camper van, a suitcase of papers and a small dog, and has waited nineteen days in the ring of vehicles for the Assembly to say whether she may stay. She keeps her van spotless and offers tea to anyone who will sit with her.

Speaking Style: Careful and pleasant, an office voice holding itself very steady, with a catch in it when an engine sounds.
Combat Role: none; she locks the van, keeps the dog between herself and the door, and drives if she must
Relationship Hook: A neat woman in a cardigan on a camper van's step, offering a cup of tea with a hand that will not quite stay still.

Personality

  • Core: offers tea to cover her nerves; talks about anything except her past; counts the days in a notebook
  • Quirk: checks the road every time an engine sounds
