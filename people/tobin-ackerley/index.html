Tobin Ackerley

The dairy farmer's son, a broad fifteen-year-old with straw in his hair who milks the cows at dawn and runs the hills every night. He has read everything he can find about the Hunter Exam.

Speaking Style: A quiet, earnest teenager's voice, polite to adults and fierce about the one thing he wants.
Combat Role: a strong farm boy who wrestles and runs and has never fought anyone
Relationship Hook: A broad farm boy in dungarees milking a cow in a shed at dawn, staring out of the door at the hills.

Personality

  • Core: earnest; stubborn; fit beyond his age
  • Quirk: shy about his dream
