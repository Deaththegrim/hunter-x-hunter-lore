Ryu Tong-sik

A goatherd who drives a small flock across the stony plain beyond the palace, sleeping in a hollow of the ridge in a felt coat and moving on before anyone thinks to ask him for papers. He is lean and leathery, carries a crook and a skin of water, and knows where every trickle in the rock can be found.

Speaking Style: Spare and windblown, the voice of a man who talks to goats more than people.
Combat Role: none; a crook and a sling for wolves
Relationship Hook: A leathery goatherd on a ridge of broken stone, crook in hand, watching an airship drone low over the empty plain.

Personality

  • Core: distrusts anyone with a uniform; kind to animals, wary of men; superstitious about the empty plain
  • Quirk: silent and self-reliant
