Underground Clinic Nurse

The head nurse of a clinic the state does not admit exists, a short, stout woman with black hair, narrow eyes and a stethoscope over her starched uniform. She runs the bed, the drugs, the telephone and the books, and charges for all four. She speaks to a patient the way a bank speaks to a debtor, and her walls are corroded and filthy.

Speaking Style: Flat, nasal and relentless, a cashier's voice that reads out the bill before it asks how the patient feels.
Combat Role: none; she has orderlies, a locked door and a scalpel she knows exactly where to put
Relationship Hook: A short stout nurse at the foot of a bed, clipboard raised, reading a bill to a patient who has only just opened his eyes.

Personality

  • Core: greedy without a scrap of shame; polite only to a patient whose account has cleared; never raises her voice; she does not need to
  • Quirk: enjoys telling debtors what their organs are worth
