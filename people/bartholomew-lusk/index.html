Bartholomew Lusk

The painter of the fighters' mural in Skyfoot Station, a stooped old man in white overalls spattered with thirty years of colours. He paints each new face the arena's publicity office sends him and scrapes off the old ones when they fall, and he talks to the faces while he works.

Speaking Style: Slow, paint-flecked and philosophical, an old sign painter who talks about faces as if they were weather.
Combat Role: none; a stooped old man on a scaffold with a bucket of paint
Relationship Hook: An old painter on scaffolding adding a fighter's face to a vast station mural, a brush between his teeth.

Personality

  • Core: slow and philosophical; remembers every face he has scraped off; wipes his brushes on his trousers
  • Quirk: talks to his paintings
