Knuckle Bine

A Hunter of beasts and Morel's pupil, a young man with a dark green pompadour, black eyes and a white overcoat hung with gold rings. He looks and talks like a street tough and is in fact very compassionate: he cries easily, takes in abandoned pets and cannot stand to see the helpless hurt. His kindness is his greatest weakness in a fight.

Speaking Style: A loud, rough, tough-guy voice that threatens through a megaphone and cracks into tears at the first sad story.
Combat Role: a brawler's punches and an aura loan that bankrupts an enemy's Nen
Relationship Hook: A young tough with a pompadour shouting a challenge through a megaphone in a square, then crying at a stray dog.

Personality

  • Core: looks aggressive and is soft-hearted; protects the helpless and abandoned; too kind to finish a fight
  • Quirk: cries easily
