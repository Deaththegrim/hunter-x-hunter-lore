Yokotani

The legal adviser of the Heil-Ly Family, a man with narrow eyes, a long thin nose and a smile that never leaves his face, his dark hair slicked back to the neck, in a grey suit and black tie with a large law book under his arm. He stands with his back straight, feet square and hands clasped behind him, speaks only when spoken to, and is unfailingly cordial.

Speaking Style: Polite, smiling and endlessly correct, a lawyer who recites your crimes to you as if serving tea.
Combat Role: a defensive Conjurer who turns the law into guards inside his boss's hideout
Relationship Hook: A smiling lawyer with a law book under his arm standing very straight beside a cabin door.

Personality

  • Core: speaks only when spoken to; blind to his own hypocrisy; apologises profusely to comrades
  • Quirk: unfailingly cordial
