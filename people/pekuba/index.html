Pekuba

A skinny young man with big lips and a mess of white hair, a long-sleeved green shirt and a pink-purple scarf he keeps pulling up over his chin. He joined Pokkle's survey team for the adventure and has been regretting it out loud since the second day.

Speaking Style: Quick, breathless and too loud for a hiding place, every sentence climbing toward panic.
Combat Role: none worth the name; a nervous young man who shouts warnings and runs
Relationship Hook: A skinny white-haired young man crouched behind a rock, scarf up to his nose, asking if anyone else heard that.

Personality

  • Core: jumpy and easily rattled; means well and talks too much; fidgets with his scarf
  • Quirk: says out loud what the others are thinking
