Linnea Dahlgren

A tall young woman in a red flannel shirt and steel-toed boots with a clipboard and a tree-borer, who has bunked at the sawmill for a month. The company hired her to survey the deep woods past the ridge before they cut.

Speaking Style: A quick, friendly, enthusiastic voice that apologises for the mud.
Combat Role: a forester who fights with a hatchet and her height, and would rather climb out of reach
Relationship Hook: A tall forester with a clipboard showing you a core of swirling wood and asking who planted these trees.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful and tireless; honest to her client and to the trees; curious about the loggers' stories
  • Quirk: afraid of losing the job
