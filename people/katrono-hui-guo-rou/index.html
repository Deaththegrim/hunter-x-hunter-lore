Katrono Hui Guo Rou

The fourth of the king's eight queens and the mother of the sixth prince Tyson, a woman with a round face, small features, small beady eyes under double lids and round eyebrows her daughter inherited, her dark hair at her shoulders. She is the second shortest of the queens. She is disappointed in her daughter and tells her so.

Speaking Style: Clipped and brittle, a fourth queen's voice that is always answering a slight nobody else heard.
Combat Role: none; a queen who fights with spies placed on the younger princes and her right to see the king
Relationship Hook: A short round-faced queen in a dark gown standing stiffly in a corridor of guarded doors.

Personality

  • Core: expects nothing of her daughter and says so; spies on every prince below her own; remembers every slight by the senior queens
  • Quirk: hates being pitied
