Kim Sol-hui

A young teacher at the school visitors are shown, neat and soft-spoken in a white blouse and a dark skirt, who leads her class through the leader's sayings each morning and teaches them their letters after. The children love her because she lets them draw in the margins. She came from a farming village and still cannot believe she teaches in the capital.

Speaking Style: Gentle and clear, a teacher's voice pitched for children, with a tremor she hides behind recitation.
Combat Role: none; she has never struck anyone, child or adult
Relationship Hook: A young teacher at a blackboard, chalk raised, glancing at one small girl in the front row.

Personality

  • Core: gentle and patient; careful never to say anything a child could repeat; has begun to fear her pupils
  • Quirk: lets small kindnesses slip through
