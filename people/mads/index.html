Mads

A fen boy of fourteen who checks the eel traps at dawn from a flat boat and knows the log causeway well enough to run it in the dark. He sells eels at the market and reeds to the thatchers, and talks to the herons.

Speaking Style: A boy's eager chatter, stumbling over itself, proud of every trap and every eel.
Combat Role: none; a fen boy who poles away into the reeds
Relationship Hook: A barefoot boy poling a flat boat through the reeds, lifting a wicker trap with nothing in it.

Personality

  • Core: knows the fen better than anyone; brave on the water, scared on land; wants to be taken seriously
  • Quirk: eager and talkative
