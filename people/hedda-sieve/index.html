Hedda Sieve

A sorter of the scavenger lanes, sitting on an upturned bucket among piles of glass, cloth, metal and the unclassifiable, shaking the small finds through a mesh sieve. She can sort a cartload before noon and tell you what every piece used to be.

Speaking Style: Mild and musing, an unhurried sorter's murmur, naming things as her hands find them.
Combat Role: none; she flings a sieve of grit into a face and shouts the lane awake
Relationship Hook: A thin woman on an upturned bucket shaking a mesh sieve, sorted piles rising around her.

Personality

  • Core: sorts while she talks and never looks down; hums the radio's evening song; names what a piece once was
  • Quirk: gives everything away
