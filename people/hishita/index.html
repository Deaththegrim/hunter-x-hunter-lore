Hishita

A young butler of the Zoldyck Family with short light blond hair, in the black suit the whole staff wears. He is one of the estate's regular drivers and does what the senior butlers tell him promptly and without comment. When Gotoh plays one of his games with visitors, he stands at the back of the room and keeps his face empty.

Speaking Style: Quiet and even, a junior butler's voice that answers in as few words as the order needs and never offers an opinion of its own.
Combat Role: a trained butler's speed and strength, and a car driven as a weapon when that is the order
Relationship Hook: A young butler polishing a black limousine until he can see his own blank face in the door.

Personality

  • Core: obedient, quick to carry out an order; keeps his face still while he is being tested; defers to Gotoh in everything
  • Quirk: talks to engines more freely than to people
