Sebastien Laroque

The head waiter of the Cloudline Restaurant, a tall, silver-haired man in a white jacket who has served dinner above the clouds for eighteen years. He seats every guest himself, remembers what they drank last time and moves through the room so quietly that diners forget he is there.

Speaking Style: Silky and discreet, a head waiter who murmurs and never once interrupts.
Combat Role: none; a waiter with a silver tray, a carving knife on the trolley and a lift to the kitchens
Relationship Hook: A silver-haired head waiter folding a napkin by a window table where the clouds drift past below.

Personality

  • Core: impeccably polite; notices everything; disapproves of shouting
  • Quirk: folds a napkin while he listens
