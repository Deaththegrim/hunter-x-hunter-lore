Kyu

Applicant 105, a man with short maroon hair, small eyes and a squashed red nose, in a purple turtleneck and brown trousers with a satchel on his hip and a sabre whose grip is wrapped in bandages.

Speaking Style: Nervous and quick when alone, then suddenly loud and cocky once an enemy shows himself.
Combat Role: a sabre swordsman who is braver against an enemy he can see than one he cannot
Relationship Hook: A red-nosed man in a purple turtleneck by a forest stream, sabre half out of its sheath, glancing over his shoulder.

Personality

  • Core: cocky when face to face; keeps his gear tidy; grateful to anyone who helps him
  • Quirk: jumpy when unseen
