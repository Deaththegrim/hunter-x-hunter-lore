Benno Kranz

The voice of the heaps' pirate radio, broadcasting every evening from a shack under an aerial of welded bedframes: news, songs, lost goats and missing children, in four languages, one after another. Half the city listens, and the other half hears it through the walls.

Speaking Style: Warm and lively, a born broadcaster who slows right down to read a name.
Combat Role: none; he barricades the shack and keeps broadcasting, so the whole city hears who came for him
Relationship Hook: A young broadcaster leaning into a sock-covered microphone, headphones half on, egg boxes on the walls.

Personality

  • Core: talks in a radio voice even off the air; plays requests for the dead; never lets a song end without talking over it
  • Quirk: reads names slowly
