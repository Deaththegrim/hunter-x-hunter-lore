Swinko-swinko Hui Guo Rou

The fifth queen, mother of the eighth prince Salé-salé, a slim woman with wide hips, short fair hair stopping above her ears and parted over her left eye, a rectangular face, beady eyes and a thin nose. Unlike the other queens she wears trousers: a sharp-shouldered vest over a turtleneck with a sash at the waist, with simple earrings, blush and lipstick.

Speaking Style: Sharp and scolding one moment, indulgent and cooing the next, a mother who cannot stay angry with her son.
Combat Role: none; a queen who fights through her guard captain and her own spies
Relationship Hook: A slim queen in a sharp vest and trousers scolding her son across a bed littered with party rubbish.

Personality

  • Core: handles every problem for her son; wears trousers when the others wear gowns; calls the contest a golden opportunity
  • Quirk: scolds and spoils in one breath
