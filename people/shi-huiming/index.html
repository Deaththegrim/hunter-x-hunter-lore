Shi Huiming

The keeper of the temple's manuscript library, a monk in grey with ink-stained fingers who has spent his whole life copying brittle scrolls from the age of the warlords. He lets pilgrims look through the lattice at the locked cases and tells them stories of the First King that the guidebooks leave out.

Speaking Style: Quiet, careful and kind, a librarian monk who speaks as if the scrolls could hear.
Combat Role: none; a scholar monk who has never lifted anything heavier than a scroll case
Relationship Hook: A grey-robed monk unrolling a brittle scroll by an oil lamp among locked cases.

Personality

  • Core: speaks softly to the scrolls; tells pilgrims the stories the guidebooks leave out; does not sleep the night before a courier comes
  • Quirk: copies everything twice
