Anneliese Kord

A compact woman of forty-five in a flight jacket with a headset round her neck, who runs the control tower over the Association's airship field. She flew Hunters into war zones for twenty years before she took the tower.

Speaking Style: Flat, terse and dry, a pilot's voice that reads out bad news as if it were the weather.
Combat Role: a pilot who fights with a flare pistol and a cool head, and would rather fly away
Relationship Hook: A pilot in a flight jacket looking down from the control tower and telling you, flatly, that the exam ship needs a navigator.

Personality

  • Core: terse and unflappable; loyal to the chairman alone; contemptuous of office Hunters
  • Quirk: keeps her logs to herself
