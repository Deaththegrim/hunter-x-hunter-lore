Ferris Kolb

The foreman of the tower's freight yard, a thick-necked man in a hi-vis vest with a clipboard and a pencil always behind his ear. He checks off the lorries that feed and clothe a building of 251 floors and runs the yard from a hut with a kettle and a calendar.

Speaking Style: Gruff and easy, a yard foreman who talks with a pencil between his teeth.
Combat Role: a working man's fists, a forklift and a crew of loaders who back him
Relationship Hook: A stocky foreman with a clipboard frowning at a crate on a loading dock while a forklift idles beside it.

Personality

  • Core: easy-going until crossed; loyal to his crew; knows he is in too deep
  • Quirk: chews pencils to splinters
