Morena Prudo

The boss of the Heil-Ly Family, the smallest of Kakin's three Mafia houses, a slim young woman with almond eyes and long dark blue hair past her waist, a row of small hoops up her left ear and two parallel scars from her forehead across her left eye to her mouth, the mark of a royal bastard. She takes no interest in the contest, the world or her own life.

Speaking Style: Quiet, flat and unafraid, a young woman's voice that speaks of mass murder as calmly as the weather.
Combat Role: a Specialist who rarely fights herself, arming her followers with a kiss and growing stronger as they kill
Relationship Hook: A scarred young woman with long blue hair kissing each of her kneeling men on the forehead in turn.

Personality

  • Core: indifferent to her own life; grateful for her scar; a calm and patient leader
  • Quirk: wants the world destroyed
