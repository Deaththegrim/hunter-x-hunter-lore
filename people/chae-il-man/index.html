Chae Il-man

A corporal of the monument's honour guard, nineteen and very proud of it, with a pressed uniform, white gloves and a ceremonial rifle he is not allowed to load. He stands his hour at the foot of the great bronze, eyes front, and afterward tells anyone who will listen how still he stood. He believes every word on the plinth.

Speaking Style: Bright, eager and loud, a young soldier who says everything as if reporting it to an officer.
Combat Role: a drilled soldier with a bayonet on an empty rifle and a whistle for the real guard
Relationship Hook: A young honour guard at rigid attention in front of a heap of wreaths, one heel pressed against a crack in the stone.

Personality

  • Core: earnest and proud; fidgets once his relief arrives; wants an officer to notice him
  • Quirk: believes what he is told, and is starting not to
