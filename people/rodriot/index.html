Rodriot

A Hunter on Tsezguerra's team, a stout man with dark skin and a snub nose, in the team's military-style gear and a plain bandana over short fair hair with shaved sides. He is the most volatile of the four and the best bowler on the island.

Speaking Style: Gruff and short-fused, the volatile one of the team, who wants to chase every thief at once.
Combat Role: a heavy brawler with an ability that suits a bowling lane
Relationship Hook: A stout man in a bandana demanding they chase the thieves now.

Personality

  • Core: eager to give chase; loyal to the team; proud of his bowling
  • Quirk: volatile
