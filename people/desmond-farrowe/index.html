Desmond Farrowe

A tanned man in a pale linen suit and loafers that are not meant for mud, who has been at the frog farm three days running with a briefcase and a cheque book. He wants to buy every breeding frog on the place and move the lot to a farm outside Yorknew City.

Speaking Style: Smooth, sunny and persuasive, a salesman's voice with an offer ready before the question ends.
Combat Role: avoids a fight with talk, and when that fails, with a hired man and a fast car
Relationship Hook: A man in a muddy linen suit smiling across a frog pond and naming a sum that makes the farmer's son stop breathing.

Personality

  • Core: charming and persistent; never raises his voice; lies only about numbers
  • Quirk: finds the weak point in a family
