Trick Tower's 3rd examiner

A big bald Hunter with a large nose and thick lips, in a pale suit, who works the third phase beside the warden. He never gave the applicants a name and none of them asked; he is the large man who stands at the lottery box and holds it out.

Speaking Style: A heavy, patient bass that sounds faintly amused by everything an applicant does.
Combat Role: a heavy Hunter who ends trouble with one grip and rarely has to move his feet
Relationship Hook: A big bald man in a pale suit kneeling on a flat stone roof, pressing on one flagstone to test the hinge beneath.

Personality

  • Core: calm and slow-moving; amused rather than impressed; meticulous with machinery
  • Quirk: says nothing he does not have to
