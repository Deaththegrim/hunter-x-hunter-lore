Ambrose Tilney

An old beekeeper who rents his hives out to the orchards around Quen City every blossom season, a slow, smiling man with a veil pushed back on his hat and a smoker in his hand. He lives in a caravan between the rows until the petals fall.

Speaking Style: Soft and drowsy, an old beekeeper who talks the way his bees hum and seldom finishes a sentence quickly.
Combat Role: none; a smoker, a veil and a hive he can kick over
Relationship Hook: An old beekeeper with his veil pushed back puffing smoke into a hive among blossoming apple trees.

Personality

  • Core: slow and contented; kind without fuss; unafraid of strangers
  • Quirk: talks to his bees
