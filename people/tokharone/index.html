Tokharone

A man with an undercut, thick brows, a bulbous nose and a hard, chiselled face who leads a team of his own. Other players say his is one of the strongest teams in the game, and they give it a wide berth.

Speaking Style: Rough and decided, a team leader who talks like a man who has already chosen.
Combat Role: a strong Nen user who leads from the front with his team at his back
Relationship Hook: A hard-faced man with an undercut kneeling to study footprints that walk into a cliff and never come out.

Personality

  • Core: ruthless with rivals; keeps his team close; plays the long game
  • Quirk: decisive
