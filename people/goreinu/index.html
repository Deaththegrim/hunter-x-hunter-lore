Goreinu

A Hunter hired by Battera, muscular, with spiky black hair, brown eyes, thick eyebrows, a flat nose and hairy forearms, in a striped sleeveless top. He is wary of working with children and more wary of a naked man joining the party, but he stays, plays fair and admits he runs on feeling more than logic.

Speaking Style: Gruff and fair, a wary man's voice that warms once he has decided he likes someone.
Combat Role: a grappler with two conjured gorillas that trade places, and a strong Shu throw
Relationship Hook: A muscular man with two gorillas, one white and one black, sizing up a team of children.

Personality

  • Core: wary but fair; a good negotiator; keeps his word to friends
  • Quirk: acts on feeling over logic
