Ottilie Gutter

The tank warden of the rain terraces, a sharp-elbowed woman who climbs the stilts every morning to lift the lids and sniff the water. She says which tank is drunk from and which is rested, and a resident who argues with her waits at the end of the queue.

Speaking Style: Sharp and hard-edged, a warden's bark that brooks no argument about water.
Combat Role: a wiry climber who fights from the scaffolding with a hooked lid-pole and drops things on heads
Relationship Hook: A wiry woman perched on scaffolding beside a great plastic tank, its lid lifted, her nose wrinkling.

Personality

  • Core: sniffs every cup she is handed; climbs faster than any child; rations her own water tighter than anyone's
  • Quirk: treats a fouled tank like a murder
