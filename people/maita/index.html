Maita

A large, bulky prizefighter with a bald head, narrow eyes and thick bushy eyebrows that meet when he frowns. He came to the tower to make his size pay, and on the floors where size still counts it mostly does. He eats on the benches between bouts and never shares.

Speaking Style: Slow, heavy and wheezing a little, a big man who announces each punch before he throws it.
Combat Role: a heavyweight's clinch and shove, leaning his whole weight on the smaller fighter
Relationship Hook: A huge bald fighter with bushy eyebrows eating a rice ball on the bench while his name crackles over the speaker.

Personality

  • Core: slow to anger and slower to calm; thinks size settles everything; respects anyone who knocks him down
  • Quirk: eats whenever he is not fighting
