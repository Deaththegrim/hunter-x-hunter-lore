Fen Ruolan

The head of reception at the RG Foundation's tower, a woman in her fifties in a neat grey suit who has signed in every visitor for twenty years. She knows the directors' wives by name, their drivers by face and their moods by the way they cross the marble.

Speaking Style: Soft, even and kind, a receptionist's voice that drops to a murmur when she means a warning.
Combat Role: none; she sees trouble coming across the lobby and has the security men there first
Relationship Hook: A grey-suited receptionist behind a marble desk, quietly telling a young clerk to go home early.

Personality

  • Core: remembers every face; never gossips in the lobby; keeps barley sweets in her desk for the drivers
  • Quirk: sends the new girls home early on certain days
