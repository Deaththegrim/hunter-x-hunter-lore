Clement Ashby

The vicar of the stone church in the market town, a gangling, untidy man of forty with chalk on his sleeves and a bicycle he cannot ride well. He organises the dances, the blood drives and the jumble sales, and he cannot sing in tune.

Speaking Style: Mild and apologetic, a vicar who sounds unsure of everything except what he is doing.
Combat Role: none; he stands on the trapdoor and talks
Relationship Hook: A gangly vicar in a chalky tweed jacket setting out jumble on trestle tables in a parish hall, standing very still on one spot.

Personality

  • Core: clumsy and kind; braver than he looks; devoted to his parish
  • Quirk: a poor liar who lies anyway
