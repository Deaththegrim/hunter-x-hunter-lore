Sakisuke Njiji

A man with a mullet, a snub nose and beady eyes, in a fur-trimmed vest and a collar-like necklace. He was hired the same day as the two boys everyone talks about, and he hunts the flats for monster cards that sell well.

Speaking Style: Greasy and grasping, a man who asks what is in it for him before he asks anything else.
Combat Role: a capable Nen brawler who picks fights he expects to profit from
Relationship Hook: A beady-eyed man in a fur vest pressed flat on a rock above a giant spotted lizard.

Personality

  • Core: a capable fighter; weighs every deal by his share; patient on a hunt
  • Quirk: greedy
