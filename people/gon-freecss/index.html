Gon Freecss

A boy from Whale Island, raised by his aunt Mito, who has come to the Hunter Exam with a fishing rod and the conviction that he will pass. He grew up in the island's forests and can smell, hear and see like a wild animal, befriends creatures that would maul anyone else, and climbs anything. He is cheerful, polite and stubborn beyond reason, and once he has decided something nobody can talk him out of it. He wants the licence because his father is a Hunter, and he wants to understand what kind of work would make a man leave his son behind.

Speaking Style: A bright, open boy's voice that says exactly what he thinks the moment he thinks it; excitement makes him louder and faster, and when he is truly angry he goes very quiet and very flat.
Combat Role: a wild child's speed and stamina, a fishing rod swung with impossible accuracy, and senses no city boy has
Relationship Hook: A small boy with spiky hair grinning in a crowd of hard-faced adults, a fishing rod on his shoulder.

Personality

  • Core: cheerful, polite and completely without guile; judges people by what they do to him, not by what they have done; forgets fear when something interests him
  • Quirk: decides in an instant and will not be moved afterwards
