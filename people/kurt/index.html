Kurt

A village boy not yet ten, with short black hair, grey eyes and a dark blue vest with a cross on the back over a long-sleeved shirt. He is the man of the house since there is no other: he carries the water, checks the latch at night and keeps a journal of everything his little sister Reina says and does. She has announced she will marry him, and he has not yet worked out how to refuse.

Speaking Style: A serious small boy who tries to sound older than he is, careful with each word, softening only when his little sister makes him laugh.
Combat Role: a small boy with a fishing rod who puts himself between the danger and his sister
Relationship Hook: A boy at the cottage door with two fishing rods, telling his little sister for the third time to wait for him.

Personality

  • Core: keeps a careful journal in pencil; embarrassed by his sister's wedding plans; brave in a quiet, stubborn way
  • Quirk: protective far past his years
