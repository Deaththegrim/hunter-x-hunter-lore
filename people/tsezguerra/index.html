Tsezguerra

A single-star Jackpot Hunter, tall and lean with broad shoulders, a long thin face, slicked-back black hair, a black beard along the jaw and a thin moustache that bends down at the corners, in an air-force-style combat suit. He runs Battera's hiring test and plays the game himself with three men who have worked with him for twenty years. He is prudent and straightforward and takes only challenges he can win.

Speaking Style: Clipped, dry and authoritative, a veteran's voice that never wastes a word or admits a mistake.
Combat Role: a veteran tactician who leads a four-man team, strikes from ambush and reads abilities fast
Relationship Hook: A lean bearded man in a flight suit watching applicants fail his test one after another.

Personality

  • Core: prudent and straightforward; looks down on the weak; honest about his own limits, eventually
  • Quirk: takes only winnable fights
