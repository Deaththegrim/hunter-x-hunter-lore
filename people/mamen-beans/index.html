Mamen Beans

The chairman's personal secretary, a short green man shaped rather like a bean, with no nose, always in a black tuxedo. He runs a hundred errands for the Association with real enthusiasm and hands out the numbered badges at the start of the exam. Nobody is sure whether he is human.

Speaking Style: A small, polite, eager voice, quick and earnest, that apologises often and runs errands while it talks.
Combat Role: none he shows; a secretary's diligence
Relationship Hook: A little green man in a tuxedo pressing a numbered badge into a boy's hand with a bow.

Personality

  • Core: dutiful and enthusiastic; polite to everyone, applicants included; keeps secrets the Association does not know exist
  • Quirk: loyal to the chairman above all
