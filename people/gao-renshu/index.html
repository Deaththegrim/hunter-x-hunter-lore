Gao Renshu

The senior weapons instructor of the military academy, a grey-cropped sergeant major who has timed every cadet's rifle drill since the first intake. He runs the armoury hall like a watchmaker's shop: every part laid on the cloth in the same order, every hand clean, every second written in his book.

Speaking Style: Gravel and stopwatch: short counts, long silences, and a dry remark when a cadet fumbles.
Combat Role: a veteran rifleman, patient and exact, who shoots once and rarely needs to again
Relationship Hook: A grey-haired instructor clicking a stopwatch as a cadet slams a rebuilt rifle onto the bench.

Personality

  • Core: writes every time in a book, even the bad ones; never raises his voice above the click of a bolt; eats with the cadets, not the officers
  • Quirk: will not discuss the record holder
