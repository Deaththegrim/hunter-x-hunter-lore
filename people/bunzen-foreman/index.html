Bunzen Foreman

A big red-bearded man in flannel with an axe on his shoulder and sawdust in his beard, who runs the woodcutters' yard at Bunzen's edge. He talks about the weather before anything else and pays players to clear the beasts from the felling ground.

Speaking Style: Gruff and weather-minded, a foreman who opens every conversation with the sky.
Combat Role: an axe swung like a woodcutter swings it, not a fighter
Relationship Hook: A red-bearded foreman squinting at the sky, then at a stranger, and asking if they can swing an axe.

Personality

  • Core: gruff; pays fairly; never satisfied a job is done
  • Quirk: talks about the weather
