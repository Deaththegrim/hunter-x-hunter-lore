Ilse Marrak

Sergeant of the gantry guards, a lean woman with a scarred jaw and a rifle slung across her back, who has walked the steel walkways above the well for twelve years. The prisoners call her by a name she pretends not to know.

Speaking Style: A hard, carrying parade-ground voice that never needs to say anything twice.
Combat Role: a trained marksman on a gantry, firing down into the well and never coming within reach
Relationship Hook: A lean guard sergeant with a scarred jaw looking down over a steel railing into a deep well of cells.

Personality

  • Core: strict and fair; notices everything on her tier; loyal to her guards
  • Quirk: disapproves of the bargain
