Ponzu

A young woman applicant, number 246, in a big yellow hat with white flaps, a salmon blouse and pale trousers. She is serious, collected and observant, fights with chemical traps and gas, and her hat hides a hive of bees that pour out when she is threatened or falls.

Speaking Style: A serious, collected young woman's voice, precise and wary, that keeps its fear in check.
Combat Role: chemical traps, sleeping gas and a hat full of bees
Relationship Hook: A young woman in a big yellow hat backing away calmly as a cloud of bees rises from it.

Personality

  • Core: serious and collected; perceptive and analytical; keeps her distance from everyone
  • Quirk: objective about danger
