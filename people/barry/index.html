Barry

A Hunter on Tsezguerra's team, young-looking and muscular with freckles on his cheeks and chest, a cleft chin and short spiky fair hair, in the team's military-style gear with a cap worn sideways. He follows orders, watches carefully and is a fine boxer who fights in close.

Speaking Style: Brisk and youthful, a boxer's quick talk that points out what others miss.
Combat Role: an in-fighting boxer who overwhelms an opponent without taking a hit
Relationship Hook: A freckled man in a sideways cap ducking under a punch and answering with three of his own.

Personality

  • Core: cautious and observant; follows Tsezguerra's lead; a boxer at heart
  • Quirk: quick to warn the team
