Viktor Szabo

The captain of the Rokario border guard post west of Quen City, a lean officer in his forties with a pencil moustache, pressed fatigues and mirrored glasses he takes off only to frighten someone. His patrols ride the NGL line in jeeps, since the other side will not let an engine past.

Speaking Style: Clipped and cold, an officer who gives a reprimand and a compliment in the same hard tone.
Combat Role: an army officer's rifle at long range and a pistol close, with a squad at his back
Relationship Hook: A lean border captain in mirrored glasses inspecting a line of guards on a dusty parade square, flag at half-mast behind him.

Personality

  • Core: hard on his men and loyal to them; proud of his marksmanship; never puts anything in writing
  • Quirk: disciplined and corrupt
