Hsu Lianqiao

A palace attendant assigned to the princes' private pool and screening lounge, a graceful young woman who folds towels, mixes drinks and switches the projector on for whichever prince arrives. She was chosen for the post because she belongs to none of the queens' households.

Speaking Style: Light, sweet and careful, an attendant's chatter that says nothing anyone could repeat.
Combat Role: none; she dives into the pool and stays under
Relationship Hook: A graceful attendant laying out towels beside a glowing indoor pool, smiling at two guards who will not look at each other.

Personality

  • Core: chatters without saying anything; learns each prince's drink by heart; never stands between two guards
  • Quirk: takes every gift, gives nothing back
