Tamazo

Gantai's co-presenter for the voyage ceremonies, a short woman with big round eyes and her hair plaited into five sections that hang like strings of beads. Her sleeveless dress is scattered with round baubles of every size, her shoes match it, and she wears puffed bloomers like her partner. On stage she is all excitement; between takes she checks the cue cards twice.

Speaking Style: Quick, bright and bouncing, a co-host who answers her partner's shouts with a squeal and a fact.
Combat Role: none; she ducks, shrieks into the microphone and lets security do its job
Relationship Hook: A tiny presenter in a bauble dress counting dancers onto a stage with her binder held to her chest.

Personality

  • Core: remembers every performer's name and hometown; laughs at Gantai's jokes even off air; quietly redoes the work of anyone who cuts corners
  • Quirk: bounces on her heels when she talks
