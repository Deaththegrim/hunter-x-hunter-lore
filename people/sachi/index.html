Sachi

An egg and honey seller with a trestle at the market every week, a round-faced young woman who walks in from her family's farm before dawn with two baskets on a yoke. She gives the hungry children a cracked egg and the reeve a hard time.

Speaking Style: Warm and quick, a market seller's patter that turns into real kindness halfway through.
Combat Role: none; she throws eggs and shouts for the reeve
Relationship Hook: A round-faced young woman behind a trestle of eggs and honey jars, asking if you have come from the north.

Personality

  • Core: kind and quick-witted; talks to everyone; never gives short weight
  • Quirk: worried for her husband
