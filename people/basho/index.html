Basho

A Hunter from Jappon with a pompadour, a moustache and sideburns, thick eyebrows and a cleft chin, very muscular and hairy-chested, in a small vest, trousers and tall boots. A brush for calligraphy and a sheaf of paper ride in his breast pocket. He is talkative, easy-going and sure life should be lived to the full, and he swears by the herbal smoke he mixes himself.

Speaking Style: Loud, cheerful and easy, a man who whistles when he is impressed and laughs off a rude answer.
Combat Role: a strong brawler who wins the hard cases with a poem written on the spot
Relationship Hook: A muscular man with a pompadour whistling as he writes a poem with a brush on a scrap of paper.

Personality

  • Core: outgoing and talkative; laughs off an insult; honest about a fight he cannot win
  • Quirk: whistles when impressed
