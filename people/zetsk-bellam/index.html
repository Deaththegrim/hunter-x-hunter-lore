Zetsk Bellam

A huge man with a heavy brow and a jaw like a figure from the stone age, in a sleeveless shirt with a ruffled collar. He plays alongside his brother Gashta and snickers at anyone else who speaks.

Speaking Style: Rough and snickering, a violent man who laughs at others and listens only to his brother.
Combat Role: a brute with keen senses who fights at his brother's side
Relationship Hook: A brow-heavy giant of a man snickering behind his brother as a cyclops lumbers into a narrow gully.

Personality

  • Core: violent; trusts only his brother; keen-sensed
  • Quirk: snickers at others
