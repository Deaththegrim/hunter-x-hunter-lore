Ottokar

A heavy, grey-stubbled labourer from Dentora who works under Zebro at the bone midden, raking the gully and sorting metal from what the hound leaves behind. He came to the estate as a scrap dealer's driver and stayed when the groundskeeper offered him steady work and asked no questions. He wears thick rubber gloves and a cap pulled low.

Speaking Style: Hoarse and unhurried, a scrapman who names each piece of metal as he picks it up, as if ticking it off a list.
Combat Role: a labourer's strength behind a long iron rake, and a shout for Zebro
Relationship Hook: A heavy man in rubber gloves lifting a rusted knife out of a pile of bones and reading its steel by the sound.

Personality

  • Core: unbothered by bones, upset by waste; keeps his cap on even indoors; sends money home and tells nobody where it comes from
  • Quirk: names every piece of metal aloud
