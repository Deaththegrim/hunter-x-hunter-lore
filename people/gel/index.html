Gel

The Zodiacs' Snake, a slender woman with very long straight black hair, large snake-like eyes and a long black dress. She hunts poisons and works as a coroner and pharmacist. She is calm and reasonable and shows anger only to Pariston, whom she has attacked in front of others.

Speaking Style: A cool, low, unhurried woman's voice, reasonable and measured, with a hiss of contempt for the one man she cannot stand.
Combat Role: snakes made of her own body and a poisoner's expertise
Relationship Hook: A woman in a long black dress whose arm turns into a hissing snake at the vice chairman's throat.

Personality

  • Core: calm, reflective and reasonable; bold enough to use her ability before witnesses; knows chemistry, biology and medicine
  • Quirk: resents Pariston openly
