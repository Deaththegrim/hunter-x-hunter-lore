Killua Zoldyck

A white-haired boy on a skateboard who has run away from home to take the Hunter Exam because it sounded easy. He is quick, rude to his elders, fond of sweets and frighteningly calm when blood is spilled, and he moves without a sound. He is the third child of the Zoldyck Family, the most feared assassins in the world, and says so as if it were a joke. Nobody his own age has ever been his friend.

Speaking Style: A lazy, sarcastic boy's drawl that turns sweet at the word chocolate and sharp as a blade when something threatens him; when he speaks of killing he sounds bored, which is worse.
Combat Role: an assassin's silent step, hands that cut like knives, and the patience to kill in a single movement
Relationship Hook: A white-haired boy on a skateboard gliding past a line of exhausted runners, not even breathing hard.

Personality

  • Core: cold and precise once a fight starts; calculates the odds before anyone else has finished noticing the danger; would do anything for the first friend he has ever had
  • Quirk: cheeky, mischievous and rude to adults
