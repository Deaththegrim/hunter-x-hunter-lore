Cherry

A martial artist of middling height in loose training clothes and soft shoes, applicant 76, with a flat-top cut finished in a short tail, a drooping nose and a stare that fixes on people. He fights with his hands alone and has sat the exam before, which is how the old hands in the hall know his face.

Speaking Style: Short, level and unhurried, a fighter's voice that wastes nothing and goes quiet when something in the room is stronger than he is.
Combat Role: an unarmed martial artist, hands and feet, who reads an opponent before he commits
Relationship Hook: A flat-topped martial artist nodding once to a running companion and telling him only one applicant has fallen out so far.

Personality

  • Core: stoic under pressure; knows when he is outmatched; keeps his own counsel
  • Quirk: counts and measures as he goes
