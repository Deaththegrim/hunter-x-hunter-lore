Corrado Bellini

The captain of the guard on the Dons' floors, a broad, bald man in his forties with a boxer's flattened nose and a suit cut to hide a shoulder holster. He has kept that landing every September for twelve years, and no one has passed the metal detector without his nod.

Speaking Style: Quiet and level, a guard captain's voice that counts the exits while it says good evening.
Combat Role: a heavy professional bodyguard who shields the door with his body and shoots from behind it
Relationship Hook: A bald guard captain with a broken nose waving a metal detector wand over a visitor on a guarded landing.

Personality

  • Core: trusts procedure over people; proud of his record; suspicious of the unseen guards
  • Quirk: methodical
