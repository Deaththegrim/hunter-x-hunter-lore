Maizan

A corporal of the Royal Army posted to the third tier, with squinting eyes, a large nose and a moustache under a dark helmet. He calms crowds outside the court, takes statements and keeps order with a smile, and he is shrewd and careful about whom he trusts.

Speaking Style: Easy and wheedling in a crowd, low and grasping when the talk turns to money.
Combat Role: an army corporal with a rifle who trusts the safety catch off
Relationship Hook: A moustached corporal leaning close to a mobster, palm open for cash, while a crowd waits behind.

Personality

  • Core: fears double-crosses; calms crowds with easy lies; flinches at an icy smile
  • Quirk: sells information for cash up front
