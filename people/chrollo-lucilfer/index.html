Chrollo Lucilfer

The founder and head of the Phantom Troupe, a handsome man in his twenties with slicked-back dark hair, a cross tattooed on his forehead and a long fur-collared coat. He leads the world's most notorious band of thieves, who carry the highest bounty there is and kill as a matter of course, and they revere him. He is calm, charismatic and a lover of books and beautiful things, and he loses interest in a treasure once he has it.

Speaking Style: A calm, low, thoughtful voice that speaks gently and listens closely, as a reader speaks of a good book; it never threatens, and it never needs to.
Combat Role: a knife fighter's speed, a perfect Zetsu, and a book of other people's abilities
Relationship Hook: A man in a fur-collared coat reading by candlelight in a ruined building, a cross on his brow, his thieves waiting.

Personality

  • Core: calm, charismatic and courteous; puts the Troupe before himself, and expects the same; kills without anger or regret
  • Quirk: loves books, music and beautiful things
