Branka Szabo

The landlady of the Ringside Hostel, a small, tough woman of sixty with a headscarf, forearms like a stevedore's and a book she keeps chained to the desk. She rents bunks to lower-floor fighters by the night, payment first, no cooking after ten, no blood on the sheets.

Speaking Style: Loud, suspicious and unexpectedly tender, a landlady who shouts the rules and whispers the condolences.
Combat Role: none; a broom, a voice that wakes the whole building and twenty fighters in the bunks
Relationship Hook: A small fierce landlady in a headscarf writing in a chained book under a board of hanging keys.

Personality

  • Core: fierce about rules; soft about tenants; trusts no one with a key
  • Quirk: crosses herself at the 200th floor's name
