Shishito

Applicant 89, a tall man with a long face, a cleft chin and dark shoulder-length hair, in a green long-sleeved shirt under a brown vest, his badge pinned to the vest. On the boat he spent the whole crossing wondering who had drawn him.

Speaking Style: A worried, reasonable voice that talks itself through every possibility out loud.
Combat Role: a cautious brawler who keeps his back to rock and loses to numbers
Relationship Hook: A long-faced man in a brown vest edging along the bottom of a rocky ravine, glancing up at the rim.

Personality

  • Quirk: thinks out loud
