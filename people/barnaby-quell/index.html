Barnaby Quell

A plump young man in a tracksuit and a head torch, with a sandwich in one pocket and a map of the tower in the other, who has been hiding on a service ladder in the lift shafts since the lobby closed last night. He has the right block and is entirely sure it means this building.

Speaking Style: An eager, breathless voice that whispers too loudly and is always certain it has worked something out.
Combat Role: none; a heavy lad who climbs better than anyone would guess
Relationship Hook: A chubby young man on a lift-shaft ladder, waving a blueprint and whispering that he has found the hidden floor.

Personality

  • Core: sure he has found the secret; endlessly optimistic; kind to anyone who believes him
  • Quirk: terrible at whispering
