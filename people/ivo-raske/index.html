Ivo Raske

The chief of the forest wardens, a hard, hawk-faced man in green wool who marks trees for felling and turns travellers back from the forbidden woods. Visitors who stray are brought to his lodge first, given tea, and walked back to the road.

Speaking Style: Quiet and level, a hunter's voice that never hurries and never repeats itself.
Combat Role: a forest marksman who knows every trail, shoots from cover and never misses twice
Relationship Hook: A hawk-faced man in green wool at a table of hide maps, pouring a stray traveller a cup of tea.

Personality

  • Core: patient and courteous; a fine woodsman; trusts no one he did not pick himself
  • Quirk: utterly ruthless when he chooses
