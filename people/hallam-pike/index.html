Hallam Pike

The owner of the boatyard, a big bald man with forearms like hawsers and sawdust in his beard, who has hauled out, scraped and patched every hull on the island at least once. Each winter he builds a dinghy on the back bench for the pleasure of it, and gives it away in spring to whoever needs one.

Speaking Style: Hearty and generous, a shipwright's booming voice that softens to a mutter when money comes up.
Combat Role: a big shipwright with an adze and a caulking mallet, slow and very heavy
Relationship Hook: A huge bald shipwright tapping a hull with a mallet and frowning at the sound.

Personality

  • Core: generous until the subject is money, then evasive; talks to the hulls he works on; cannot say no to a skipper in trouble
  • Quirk: gives away a dinghy every spring
