Ezra Hollis

The sexton of the chapel on the hill, a tall stooping man who rings the bell, cuts the grass between the stones and unlocks the doors on the rare days a visiting minister steps off the mail boat. In between he carves the little boats on the headstones of those the sea never gave back.

Speaking Style: Low and careful, a sexton's hushed voice used to speaking in graveyards and to the grieving.
Combat Role: none; a spade and the patience of a man who digs graves by hand
Relationship Hook: A tall sexton in a dark porch, one hand resting on a bell rope, watching a grey sea.

Personality

  • Core: speaks softly even outdoors; keeps a count of every time he has rung the bell; carves boats on the stones of the drowned
  • Quirk: stands near the rope in every storm
