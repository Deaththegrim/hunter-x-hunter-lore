Zoldyck guard hound

A giant hound of the Zoldyck estate, near four metres tall, trained until it seems more machine than animal, obeying only the family and still keeping an old order to kill every intruder. Silent and unblinking. Fights with hand-like clawed forepaws, and eats what it kills.
